Ryan Specter & Community Contributors

Hey! I'm Ryan, the developer behind Innioasis Updater CE, the Community Firmware Archive for Y1 and Y2, and the Themes Gallery. I pay for these hobby projects out of my own pocket with my own money to keep them free and accessible for everyone. Server hosting, firmware archive storage, domain renewals, and ongoing maintenance cost around $200 each month, so any support you can share helps take the weight off and keeps the project thriving for the community!
