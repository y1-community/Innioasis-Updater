Why Inniclassic for your Innioasis Y1

Inniclassic is an iPod Classic tribute built on JJ Launcher. The menus, the slowly panning album covers, the click-wheel habits, even a from-scratch Music Quiz — all on the Y1, with modern format support underneath.

Why people pick it

The Classic, back in your hand.

  • 1:1 iPod Classic look — Plain-text menus, system-matched bold type, and the screen-name status bar — light and dark theme variants included.
  • Cover browsing — Two-pane menus with a slowly panning album cover that bleeds to the top of the screen, like the real thing.
  • A real Music Quiz — A from-scratch recreation using your own library: 5 rounds of 8 questions with round and victory screens.
  • Synced lyrics — Time-synced scrolling lyrics from .lrc files or embedded tags, toggled straight from Now Playing.
  • Plays everything you own — MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG Vorbis, Opus, ALAC/M4A, and AAC — a modern codec set under a retro face.
  • Radio, podcasts, video, audiobooks — FM radio via the hardware tuner, podcast subscriptions, full-screen video, and bookmarked audiobooks.

Community

What the community says

Inniclassic is nostalgia, carefully engineered. It takes the look and behaviour of an iPod Classic and puts it on the Y1 — the menus, the covers, even a from-scratch Music Quiz. For people who miss the click-wheel years, this is the software they show off.

Because it is built directly on JJ Launcher’s media engine, Inniclassic inherits a mature base while layering on the Classic details. The project’s releases document a long list of refinements aimed at one goal: making it feel like the real thing, only with FLAC.

  • r/innioasis · Inniclassic threads

What the project offers

  • Bundled iPod Classic theme (light + dark), aiming for a 1:1 look
  • Two-pane Main Menu and Music menu with Ken Burns album covers
  • Redesigned Now Playing with an angled, reflected cover and a marquee for long titles
  • Center-click cycles Progress → Seek → Shuffle & Repeat → Rating, like a real Classic
  • On-The-Go playlist and a long-press Now Playing hold menu
  • Fast-scroll letter jump on every alphabetised list
  • Favorites with a heart indicator
  • Synced lyrics from .lrc files or ID3/FLAC/ALAC tags
  • Wide format support: MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG Vorbis, Opus, ALAC/M4A, AAC
  • Last.fm scrobbling — automatic local scrobble log, optional account sync
  • Podcasts, FM radio via the real hardware tuner, video via libVLC, and resumable audiobooks
  • Wireless PC upload over Wi-Fi

Things to know first

  • Currently Y1 only — there is no Y2 package
  • Method 1 ships Y1 Software 3.1.2 with Inniclassic baked in; Method 2 installs an APK over an existing JJ Launcher setup (uninstall first)
  • Gapless playback activates for playlists of 15 tracks or fewer with shuffle off and no FLAC in the queue
  • Keep video files modest (640×480, 30fps, ~2 Mbps or less) for smooth playback
  • Still a community project — restore Original Software if anything misbehaves
