## Installation Steps
  - Launch Innioasis Updater CE on your Windows, Mac, or Linux computer.
  - Select Y1 from the Device Model dropdown.
  - Select Rondo from the Software dropdown.
  - Click Install / Restore and wait for the tool to download and prepare the files.
  - When prompted by Updater CE, power off your Y1 and connect the USB cable.
  - Complete post-install setup

Follow the steps in [this guide](post_install.html?software=rondo-y1&model=Y1) to complete setup on your Y1.[View Guide](post_install.html?software=rondo-y1&model=Y1)
