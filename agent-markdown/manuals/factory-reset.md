# Factory reset: what it does and how to do it safely

A factory reset returns the software to its first-run state. It also erases the player's internal storage, so read this first.[All player manuals](index.html)[Firmware archive](../firmware.html)

There are two different things people call a reset:

### The reset pin

Forces the player to shut down when it freezes. It does not erase anything and does not restore factory settings.

### Factory reset

Erases internal storage and returns settings, accounts and the home screen to their first-run state.

### Before you reset
  - Copy music, recordings, photos and documents to a computer.
  - If the player has a microSD card, remove it or check that your files live on the card. Cards are usually left alone, but a backup is cheap.
  - Note any Wi-Fi or Bluetooth device you will need to pair again.
  - Charge the player to at least half.

### Reset from Settings
  - Open Settings.
  - Choose Factory reset.
  - Read the warning, confirm, and wait. The player restarts on its own.

If the player will not reach Settings, use the model guide: [Y1](../guides/factory-reset-y1.html), [Y2](../guides/factory-reset-y2.html), [G1](../guides/factory-reset-g1.html), [G3](../guides/factory-reset-g3.html), [G5](../guides/factory-reset-g5.html), [R1](../guides/factory-reset-r1.html), [PR1](../guides/factory-reset-pr1.html), [SR1](../guides/factory-reset-sr1.html), [Q3](../guides/factory-reset-q3.html) or [Q5](../guides/factory-reset-q5.html).
