# Put music, audiobooks and video on your player

Your player behaves like a USB drive with a screen. Copy files into the right folder and it finds them.[All player manuals](index.html)[Firmware archive](../firmware.html)

Connect the player with a USB data cable. It appears in Finder or File Explorer as a device, sometimes called MTP. If nothing appears, try another cable first: many USB cables are charge-only.

### Windows
  - Plug the player in and unlock its screen.
  - Open File Explorer and look under This PC.
  - Open the player, then its internal storage or microSD card.
  - Copy your music into `Music`, audiobooks into `Audiobooks`, video into `Video`, and photos into `Pictures`. Creating the folder if it is missing is fine.
  - Eject the player before unplugging it.

### macOS

macOS does not speak MTP on its own. Install a small helper such as [OpenMTP](https://openmtp.ganeshrvel.com/), open it, connect the player, and copy files into the folders above. Music that lives in the Apple Music library must be exported first; a protected library file will not play anywhere else.

### microSD cards

Players with a card slot accept microSD cards up to the size printed in the manual, and most current models accept 512 GB or more. The card appears as a second storage location. Files on the card survive a factory reset; files in internal storage do not.

Tip: keep filenames simple and put each album in its own folder. The music app groups by the tags inside the files, but a tidy folder tree makes browsing by folder much nicer.
