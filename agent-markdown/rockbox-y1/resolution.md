# Rockbox-Y1 240p and 360p builds

Y1 community builds are labelled by screen resolution. 360p is current: Rockbox-Y1 0.5 and later is 360p only. 240p builds ended at 0.4 and use the Rockbox iPod classic themes.[Get it in Updater CE](../guide.html?model=Y1&software=rockbox-y1)[Full written tutorial](../guides/rockbox-y1.html)

## What 240p and 360p mean

Resolution labels describe the display layout and theme format that the build uses. A 240p theme is designed for 320×240 builds, while a 360p theme is designed for 480×360 builds.

## How to choose
  - Choose 360p. Rockbox-Y1 0.5 and later is 360p only, and it is the build every Y1 can run.
  - Updater CE 2.5 or later installs every 360p Rockbox-Y1 theme for you during a Rockbox-Y1 install.
  - 240p builds stopped at 0.4. They can't be used on a Y1 bought new after May 2026, or on any Y1 that shipped with Original Software 3.0.7 or later.
  - 240p themes are the Rockbox iPod classic themes on rockbox.org, installed by hand.
[Rockbox-Y1 theme guide](../rockbox-y1-themes.html)[Firmware Downloads](../firmware.html?model=Y1&software=rockbox-y1)

## Frequently asked questionsCan installing the wrong resolution harm my player?

A 240p build is a pre-0.5 build, and on a Y1 bought new after May 2026 or one that shipped with Original Software 3.0.7 or later it stops the player from starting. Use 360p unless you know your Y1 is older.Can I switch between 240p and 360p later?

Yes, by installing the other build. Themes don't carry over: 240p and 360p themes are separate sets.
