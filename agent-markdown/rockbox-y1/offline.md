# Rockbox-Y1

Rockbox-Y1 turns your Innioasis Y1 into a dedicated offline music player: local files on a MicroSD card, no accounts, no subscriptions, and no internet connection required.[Get it in Updater CE](../guide.html?model=Y1&software=rockbox-y1)[Full written tutorial](../guides/rockbox-y1.html)

## Built for local music libraries
  - Plays MP3, FLAC, AAC, Ogg Vorbis, WAV, and more directly from your MicroSD card.
  - Indexes tracks by artist, album, genre, and year using audio tags, or lets you browse folders directly.
  - Includes gapless playback, ReplayGain, crossfade, and a parametric equalizer.
  - Works 100%% offline with zero accounts or cloud services.

## Switching from streaming

Copy your audio files onto a MicroSD card, insert it into your Y1, and run Database → Update Now.

## Frequently asked questionsDoes Rockbox-Y1 require Wi-Fi or an account?

No. Everything runs locally from the files on your MicroSD card.Which audio formats are supported?

Standard lossless and lossy formats including FLAC, MP3, AAC/M4A, Ogg Vorbis, Opus, and WAV.
