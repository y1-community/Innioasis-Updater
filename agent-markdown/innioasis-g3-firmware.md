## Reliable Firmware for G-Series Players

The Innioasis G3 and Innioasis G1 are compact, pocketable audio players built for long battery life and offline listening. If your player is unresponsive, frozen on boot, or in need of fresh system software, our archive provides tested factory restore images.

Features of our G-series firmware catalog:
  - Original Stock Software: Restores factory audio players, FM radio functionality, and Bluetooth drivers.
  - Cross-Platform Installation: Install directly using [Innioasis Updater CE](guide.html) on macOS, Linux, or Windows.
  - Always available: hosted by the community, with no expiring file-sharing links.

## Flashing Your Player with Updater CE

To restore your G3 or G1 player:
  - Open Innioasis Updater CE on your desktop or laptop.
  - Select G3 or G1 from the Device Model menu.
  - Choose Original Software and Updater will fetch the right file: `rom_g3.zip` or `rom_g1.zip`.
  - Follow the connection prompt to attach your player over USB and complete flashing.
