# Rockbox-Y2 first start

After installing Rockbox-Y2 on your Innioasis Y2, the first boot sets up the <code>.rockbox</code> folder on your MicroSD card and indexes your music library.[Get it in Updater CE](../guide.html?model=Y2&software=rockbox-y2)[Full written tutorial](../guides/rockbox-y2.html)

## First boot checklist
  - Turn on the player and let it finish its initial startup.
  - Insert a MicroSD card formatted as FAT32 or exFAT with your music files.
  - Allow the Rockbox database to scan your music library.
  - Install themes using Updater CE or copy them to the `.rockbox` folder on your card.

## Useful settings on day one
  - Database → Update Now after adding new music folders.
  - Theme Settings to change fonts, status bars, and Now Playing screens.
  - Sound Settings for gapless playback, crossfade, and the parametric equalizer.

See the [Rockbox-Y2 theme guide](../rockbox-y2-themes.html) and the [MicroSD card guide](sd-card.html) for more details.

## Frequently asked questionsMy player started with no music showing — what should I do?

Make sure your music is on the MicroSD card, then open Database in the menu and initialize or update it.How do I install themes?

Updater CE transfers Rockbox themes for you when it can, and walks you through copying them when manual transfer is needed. See the [theme guide](../rockbox-y2-themes.html).Can I switch back to the original software?

Yes. You can reboot into the original software on builds that support dual boot, or restore Original Software in Updater CE.
