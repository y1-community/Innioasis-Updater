# Keep the good stuff going.

Updater CE is free. It is built by people who want Innioasis and Timmkoo players to stay useful. Your support pays for the archive, the servers and the time.

## This is a people-powered project.

Every contribution helps someone find a working firmware, try a new project, or keep an old player alive a little longer. ## Where the money goes

Running Updater CE, the Themes Gallery and the firmware archive costs about $200 a month. On top of that comes the Apple Developer fee each year, a one-off Mac mini for testing, and the hardware: a player for every model and release worth testing, and some of those players do not survive the work. Community contributions currently cover roughly 15% of the real total; the rest comes out of my own pocket.Total cost so far$0Adding it up...Community contributions$0Received from community supporters.
