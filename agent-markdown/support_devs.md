# Keep the good stuff going.

Updater CE is free. It is built by people who want Innioasis and Timmkoo players to stay useful. Your support pays for the archive, the servers and the time.

## This is a people-powered project.

Every contribution helps someone find a working firmware, try a new project, or keep an old player alive a little longer.
