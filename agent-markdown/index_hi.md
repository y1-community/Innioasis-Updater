## Innioasis अपडेटर सीई क्या है?

Innioasis Updater सीई is an open-source tool that simplifies updating and restoring your player across Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Flashing through Updater CE preserves your music library, video collection, and Bluetooth pairings while guiding each step on screen.[Updater CE प्राप्त करें](guide_hi.html#platform)[Compare flashing tools](updater-comparison_hi.html)[सॉफ़्टवेयर ब्राउज़ करें](firmware_hi.html)

Official software, restore files, and custom systems including Rockbox, Better-Y, and Solar.[Y1 फ़र्मवेयर](firmware_hi.html?model=Y1)[Y2 फ़र्मवेयर](firmware_hi.html?model=Y2)Innioasis

### Innioasis G1, G3 & G5

### Innioasis R1 & PR1

Original firmware and recovery images for dedicated audio players.[R-Series ROMs](firmware_hi.html?brand=Innioasis)Timmkoo

### Timmkoo A5, Q5 & Q3

Updater CE supports packages provided for Timmkoo players.[Timmkoo guide](firmware-faq_hi.html)

Every package is verified before publication. New builds appear as soon as they are tested.

## Getting started with Rockbox

### Rockbox को Y1 पर इंस्टॉल करें

Select Y1, choose Rockbox stable, and connect only when prompted.[Y1 instructions](guides/rockbox-y1.html)[क्यों Rockbox?](rockbox-benefits_hi.html)

### Rockbox को Y2 पर इंस्टॉल करें

Select Y2 in the tool. The Y2 route uses hardware-specific binaries.[Y2 instructions](guides/rockbox-y2.html)[क्यों Rockbox?](rockbox-benefits_hi.html)

### Restore factory software

Returning to factory software uses the same flow: select your model and choose Original Software.[Y1 factory restore](guides/restore-original-y1.html)[Y2 factory restore](guides/restore-original-y2.html)

Compatible with Windows, macOS, and Linux.
