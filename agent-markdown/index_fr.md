## Qu'est-ce que Innioasis Updater CE ?

Innioasis Programme de mise à jour CE is an open-source tool that simplifies updating and restoring your player across Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Flashing through Updater CE preserves your music library, video collection, and Bluetooth pairings while guiding each step on screen.[Obtenir Updater CE](guide_fr.html#platform)[Compare flashing tools](updater-comparison_fr.html)[Parcourir le logiciel](firmware_fr.html)

Verify the model printed on the back of your device. Y1 and Y2 packages differ.02

### Select firmware

Choose the build you want from the catalog for your hardware revision.03

### Installer ou restaurer

## Getting started with Rockbox

### Installer Rockbox sur Y1

Select Y1, choose Rockbox stable, and connect only when prompted.[Y1 instructions](guides/rockbox-y1.html)[Pourquoi Rockbox ?](rockbox-benefits_fr.html)

### Installer Rockbox sur Y2

Select Y2 in the tool. The Y2 route uses hardware-specific binaries.[Y2 instructions](guides/rockbox-y2.html)[Pourquoi Rockbox ?](rockbox-benefits_fr.html)

### Restore factory software

Returning to factory software uses the same flow: select your model and choose Original Software.[Y1 factory restore](guides/restore-original-y1.html)[Y2 factory restore](guides/restore-original-y2.html)

Compatible with Windows, macOS, and Linux.
