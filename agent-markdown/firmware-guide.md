## Which tool should I use?

Innioasis Updater CE is the safest way to update your player. It keeps your music, videos and Bluetooth pairings. It tells you when updates are ready. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux. The Innioasis Universal Flash Tool wipes your player and works on fewer computers.[Open the full comparison](updater-comparison.html)

### Windows won't launch the app? How to turn off Smart App Control

Windows Defender SmartScreen or Smart App Control can block newly downloaded community applications before signature reputations propagate. Smart App Control is currently impacting popular applications like the Epic Overlay Service (used by Fortnite) — Updater CE is safe and verified to use. Turning Smart App Control off allows Updater CE to open and run normally.
  - Open Settings on your Windows PC (press Win + I).
  - Click Privacy & security in the left sidebar, then click Windows Security.
  - Click App & browser control.
  - Under Smart App Control, click Smart App Control settings.
  - Set the toggle to Off to allow Updater CE to launch.

Blocked by Edge? If Microsoft Edge blocks the download itself, click the More options button (…) → Keep → Show more → Keep anyway.

Re-enabling Smart App Control later: When you want to turn Smart App Control back on, you can re-enable it via the Windows Registry under `HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Control\CI\Policy` by setting `VerifiedAndReputablePolicyState` to `1` (On) or `2` (Evaluation Mode) and restarting.Close help
