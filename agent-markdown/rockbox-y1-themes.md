# Rockbox-Y1 themes

Change how Rockbox looks on your Y1. The quickest way is to let Updater CE install the themes for you. You can also copy them over yourself.[Theme pack (360p)](/download-proxy?url=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2Frockbox-y1%2Fthemes%2Freleases%2Fdownload%2Ffor_the_gays_and_the_theys%2Fthemepack.zip&filename=rockbox-y1-360p-themepack.zip)[Rockbox tutorials](rockbox-tutorials/)[Community themes gallery](https://themes.innioasis.app/)

## Recommended: let Updater CE install them

Installing Rockbox-Y1 with Updater CE 2.5 or later? It installs every 360p Rockbox-Y1 theme for you during the install. There's nothing to download or unzip.

Already on Rockbox? Open Updater CE and choose Settings → Install Rockbox 360p Themes, or press Cmd+T on a Mac and Ctrl+T on Windows and Linux. If it can't reach the player on its own, it walks you through USB Storage Mode.[Get Updater CE](guide.html)[Install Rockbox-Y1](rockbox-y1/install.html)

## 360p themes, by hand

Recommended if you're copying themes yourself. They work on Rockbox-Y1 0.5 or later on every Y1, and you can copy them onto a Y2 by hand too.

The theme pack is every theme merged into one `.rockbox` folder. The individual download has each theme in its own folder. Either way, you merge a `.rockbox` folder into the player's: copy what's inside, merge folders, and replace files that already exist.[Theme pack (360p) · 90 MB](/download-proxy?url=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2Frockbox-y1%2Fthemes%2Freleases%2Fdownload%2Ffor_the_gays_and_the_theys%2Fthemepack.zip&filename=rockbox-y1-360p-themepack.zip)[Individual themes (360p) · 294 MB](/download-proxy?url=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2Frockbox-y1%2Fthemes%2Freleases%2Fdownload%2Ffor_the_gays_and_the_theys%2Fthemes_individual.zip&filename=rockbox-y1-360p-themes-individual.zip)[Rockbox fonts 4.0](https://download.rockbox.org/release/4.0/rockbox-fonts-4.0.zip)

## 240p themes

240p themes are the Rockbox iPod classic themes on rockbox.org. Download one there and copy it into `.rockbox` by hand, the same way. They need a 240p Rockbox-Y1 build, and those ended at 0.4.Check your Y1 first

240p builds can't be used on a Y1 bought new after May 2026, or on any Y1 that shipped with Original Software 3.0.7 or later. Flashing one there stops the player from starting.[iPod classic themes on rockbox.org](https://themes.rockbox.org/index.php?target=ipod6g)

## Pick a theme on the player
  - Unplug the Y1 once the files are on it.
  - Open Settings → Theme Settings → Browse Theme Files.
  - Choose a theme. It applies straight away.

## Frequently asked questionsWhich Rockbox-Y1 version do 360p themes need?

Rockbox-Y1 0.5 or later. Every release from 0.5 on is 360p only.Do I need to unzip anything if I use Updater CE?

No. Updater CE 2.5 or later installs every 360p theme during a Rockbox-Y1 install.Can I use 240p themes on my Y1?

Only with a 240p build, and those ended at 0.4. 240p builds can't be used on a Y1 bought new after May 2026, or on any Y1 that shipped with Original Software 3.0.7 or later. Flashing one there stops the player from starting.Do these themes work on a Y2?

You can copy the 360p files onto a Y2 running Rockbox-Y2 by hand. Rockbox-Y2 is a separate port, so check its release notes.
