## Was ist Innioasis Updater CE?

Innioasis Updater CE is an open-source tool that simplifies updating and restoring your player across Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Flashing through Updater CE preserves your music library, video collection, and Bluetooth pairings while guiding each step on screen.[Updater CE holen](guide_de.html#platform)[Compare flashing tools](updater-comparison_de.html)[Durchsuchen Sie Software](firmware_de.html)

## Verwendung von Innioasis Updater01

### Check your model

Verify the model printed on the back of your device. Y1 and Y2 packages differ.02

### Select firmware

Choose the build you want from the catalog for your hardware revision.03

### Installieren oder wiederherstellen

Updater prepares the archive and prompts you when to connect your player.

## Getting started with Rockbox

### Installieren Sie Rockbox auf Y1

Select Y1, choose Rockbox stable, and connect only when prompted.[Y1 instructions](guides/rockbox-y1.html)[Warum Rockbox?](rockbox-benefits_de.html)

### Installieren Sie Rockbox auf Y2

Select Y2 in the tool. The Y2 route uses hardware-specific binaries.[Y2 instructions](guides/rockbox-y2.html)[Warum Rockbox?](rockbox-benefits_de.html)

### Restore factory software

Returning to factory software uses the same flow: select your model and choose Original Software.[Y1 factory restore](guides/restore-original-y1.html)[Y2 factory restore](guides/restore-original-y2.html)

Compatible with Windows, macOS, and Linux.
