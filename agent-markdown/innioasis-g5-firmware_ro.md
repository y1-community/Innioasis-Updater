## How to Update or Restore Your Innioasis G5

Here's how to update or reinstall factory firmware on your G5:
  - Download Innioasis Updater CE: Install the community tool for your computer ([macOS](guide_ro.html#macos), [Windows](drivers_ro.html#windows), sau [Linux](drivers_ro.html#linux)).
  - Select Model G5: Alege G5 in the Device Model dropdown.
  - Choose Original Software: Updater CE fetches `rom_g5.zip`.
  - Connect and restore: follow the on-screen steps to put the player in recovery or power it off, then plug in USB. The flash runs from there.
