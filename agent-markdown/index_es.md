## ¿Qué es Innioasis Actualizador CE?

Innioasis Actualizador CE is an open-source tool that simplifies updating and restoring your player across Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Flashing through Updater CE preserves your music library, video collection, and Bluetooth pairings while guiding each step on screen.[Obtener Updater CE](guide_es.html#platform)[Compare flashing tools](updater-comparison_es.html)[Explorar software](firmware_es.html)

## Getting started with Rockbox

### Instale Rockbox en Y1

Select Y1, choose Rockbox stable, and connect only when prompted.[Y1 instructions](guides/rockbox-y1.html)[¿Por qué Rockbox?](rockbox-benefits_es.html)

### Instale Rockbox en Y2

Select Y2 in the tool. The Y2 route uses hardware-specific binaries.[Y2 instructions](guides/rockbox-y2.html)[¿Por qué Rockbox?](rockbox-benefits_es.html)

### Restore factory software

Returning to factory software uses the same flow: select your model and choose Original Software.[Y1 factory restore](guides/restore-original-y1.html)[Y2 factory restore](guides/restore-original-y2.html)

Compatible with Windows, macOS, and Linux.
