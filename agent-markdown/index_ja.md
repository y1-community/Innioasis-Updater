## Innioasis Updater CE とは何ですか?

Innioasis アップデータ CE is an open-source tool that simplifies updating and restoring your player across Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Flashing through Updater CE preserves your music library, video collection, and Bluetooth pairings while guiding each step on screen.[Updater CE を入手](guide_ja.html#platform)[Compare flashing tools](updater-comparison_ja.html)[ソフトウェアを閲覧する](firmware_ja.html)

## Supported device models[すべてのダウンロードを参照する](firmware_ja.html)Innioasis

### Innioasis Y1 & Y2

Official software, restore files, and custom systems including Rockbox, Better-Y, and Solar.[Y1 ファームウェア](firmware_ja.html?model=Y1)[Y2 ファームウェア](firmware_ja.html?model=Y2)Innioasis

### Innioasis G1, G3 & G5

Official software and recovery files for touchscreen models. Restore factory apps or unbrick.[G-Series ROMs](firmware_ja.html?brand=Innioasis)Innioasis

### Innioasis R1 & PR1

Original firmware and recovery images for dedicated audio players.[R-Series ROMs](firmware_ja.html?brand=Innioasis)Timmkoo

### Timmkoo A5, Q5 & Q3

Updater CE supports packages provided for Timmkoo players.[Timmkoo guide](firmware-faq_ja.html)

Every package is verified before publication. New builds appear as soon as they are tested.

## Getting started with Rockbox

### Y1 に Rockbox をインストールします

Select Y1, choose Rockbox stable, and connect only when prompted.[Y1 instructions](guides/rockbox-y1.html)[なぜRockbox?](rockbox-benefits_ja.html)

### Y2 に Rockbox をインストールします

Select Y2 in the tool. The Y2 route uses hardware-specific binaries.[Y2 instructions](guides/rockbox-y2.html)[なぜRockbox?](rockbox-benefits_ja.html)

### Restore factory software

Returning to factory software uses the same flow: select your model and choose Original Software.[Y1 factory restore](guides/restore-original-y1.html)[Y2 factory restore](guides/restore-original-y2.html)

Compatible with Windows, macOS, and Linux.
