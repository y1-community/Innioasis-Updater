### [Rockbox](rockbox-benefits_ms.html)

The gold standard in open-source audio playback. Parametric EQ, gapless FLAC/MP3 playback, themes, and font packs installable directly in Updater CE.[Y1 Rockbox Guide →](guides/rockbox-y1.html)

## How to Install or Update Y1 Firmware

Upgrading or un-bricking your Innioasis Y1 is simple with our multi-platform software suite:
  - Open Innioasis Updater CE: Launch the updater on macOS, Windows, or Linux.
  - Select Model Y1: pilih Y1 in the Device Model dropdown.
  - Pick Your Operating System: Select Rockbox, Better-Y, Solar, or Original Software.
  - Click Install: Updater CE downloads the firmware package directly from the developer's release. When prompted, connect your powered-off Y1 via USB to begin flashing.
