## Start here
  - New to Rockbox on the Y1? The [install guide](../rockbox-y1/install.html) walks through Updater CE.
  - Want the themes without the steps? Updater CE 2.5 or later installs every 360p Rockbox-Y1 theme during the install.
  - On a Y2? Rockbox-Y2 is its own port. The [Rockbox-Y2 hub](../rockbox-y2/) has its downloads and notes.

## Other firmware for the Y1 and Y2

Rockbox isn't the only option. The [firmware archive](../firmware.html?view=firmware) lists every project with a released build, including Solar, Rondo, Koensayr, Better-Y, Inniclassic, JJ Launcher, Y2Player and Reborn (Y2Linux).
