## Lithium 7 Beta (pre-release) v7.1.06.09.25

[](https://discord.gg/3zbfaTNN7V)

This is a very early beta release of Lithium 7, complete with kernel & ramdisk, with built-in Xposed support. It has achieved a record-setting boot time of just 40 seconds, as opposed to the 1:04 boot time of Lithium 5.

If you have any issues, feel free to join the [discussion](https://github.com/NoahDomingues/Lithium/discussions), or join the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/3zbfaTNN7V).
