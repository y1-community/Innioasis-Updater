Y2Linux 1.0 Beta 9.3.18: USB that always reconnects, a new skin and quicker settings.

## What's new
  - Luna XP skin: blue title bars, a green start button and rolling hills, made by eNKay. Pick it in Settings → Skin.
  - Quick settings from the Quick Menu: hold Power to open the Quick Menu, then hold Select on Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or Brightness to jump straight to that setting.
  - Sort an artist's albums your way: Settings → Customize → Browsing → Artist's Albums: Oldest First, Newest First or A to Z.
  - Cover Flow matches your skin: its colours, fonts and cover shape now follow the skin you picked.
  - Updates without Wi-Fi: when Wi-Fi is off, Software Update shows Connect to Wi-Fi and takes you there, instead of a check that can't work.
  - Songs the player can't play: if your card has files the player can't play (WMA or DSD, for example), Settings → Library now tells you how many and which kind, so they no longer just seem to be missing.
  - For skin makers: `{bitrate}` shows a song's bitrate, like "320 kbps".

## Fixed
  - USB connection: after unplugging at the wrong moment, a computer sometimes never found the player again until it was restarted. The player now reconnects every time.

## Known issues
  - A charge-only USB cable only charges. If your computer doesn't see the player, try a cable that transfers data with a phone.
  - Skins with their own fonts (Luna XP, Winamp and others) show boxes instead of Chinese, Japanese and Korean titles.
  - After a sleep, Bluetooth headphones reconnect within a few seconds of waking. Headphones that went back to a phone meanwhile must be picked again in Settings → Bluetooth.
  - WPA3-only Wi-Fi networks can't be joined; a "WPA2/WPA3 mixed" setting on the router works.
  - Some players turned off by themselves during playback; the cause isn't found yet.
  - Something wrong? Send an error report (Settings → System → Error Reports). Restore Previous System (Settings → System → About, tap Version seven times, then Diagnostics) takes you back to the version you had, with your data kept.
