## Step 1: Prepare Your Computer

Innioasis Updater CE runs natively on Windows, macOS, and Linux without requiring complex installation harnesses:
  - Launch Innioasis Updater CE on your desktop or laptop.
  - Set Device Model to G5.
  - Select Original Software in the software dropdown. Updater CE automatically downloads the official `rom_g5.zip` release package directly.
  - Click Install / Restore to begin preparation.

## Step 2: Connect the G5 at the Prompt

Never connect your player before the application prompts you:
  - Ensure your Innioasis G5 is fully powered off.
  - When Updater displays the USB connection prompt, plug your USB-C data cable into the computer and player.
  - Do not touch the cable or power buttons while installation is underway.
  - Once the success confirmation appears, unplug the USB cable and power on your G5.
