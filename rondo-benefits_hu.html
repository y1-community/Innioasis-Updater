Why install Rondo on your Innioasis Y1?

Rondo is an open-source, purpose-built clickwheel music player firmware designed specifically for the Innioasis Y1 hardware. It offers clean library browsing, clean typography, and fast audio decoding.

Highlights of Rondo

Fast Clickwheel Response

Carefully tuned acceleration curve matched to the Y1 physical capacitive touchwheel for smooth scrolling through large music libraries.

Focused Audio Player

No bloatware, background analytics, or unnecessary daemons: 100% of the hardware resources are dedicated to crisp audio output.

Reversible Anytime

Install easily using Innioasis Updater CE and return to Original Software or Rockbox whenever you choose.
