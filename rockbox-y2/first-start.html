Rockbox-Y2 · Innioasis Y2

Rockbox-Y2 first start

After installing Rockbox-Y2 on your Innioasis Y2, the first boot sets up the <code>.rockbox</code> folder on your MicroSD card and indexes your music library.

Get it in Updater CEFull written tutorial

First boot checklist

  1. Turn on the player and let it finish its initial startup.
  2. Insert a MicroSD card formatted as FAT32 or exFAT with your music files.
  3. Allow the Rockbox database to scan your music library.
  4. Install themes using Updater CE or copy them to the .rockbox folder on your card.

Useful settings on day one

  • Database → Update Now after adding new music folders.
  • Theme Settings to change fonts, status bars, and Now Playing screens.
  • Sound Settings for gapless playback, crossfade, and the parametric equalizer.

See the Rockbox-Y2 theme guide and the MicroSD card guide for more details.

Frequently asked questions

My player started with no music showing — what should I do?

Make sure your music is on the MicroSD card, then open Database in the menu and initialize or update it.

How do I install themes?

Updater CE transfers Rockbox themes for you when it can, and walks you through copying them when manual transfer is needed. See the theme guide.

Can I switch back to the original software?

Yes. You can reboot into the original software on builds that support dual boot, or restore Original Software in Updater CE.
