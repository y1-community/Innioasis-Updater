Why Y2Reborn for your Innioasis Y2

Y2Reborn is a complete reimagining of the Innioasis Y2. Built from the ground up on native Y2Linux and powered by a custom Rust audio player by Schulzcode, it strips away Android overhead to deliver 60 FPS GPU-accelerated graphics, bit-perfect ALSA and LDAC Bluetooth audio, Wi-Fi Internet Radio, offline Last.fm scrobbling, and Over-The-Air system updates.

Why people pick it

Pure Linux efficiency, hardware graphics, and modern connected features.

  • Native Linux & Rust engine — By discarding Android in favor of a specialized Y2Linux kernel and native Rust player (reborn), Y2Reborn achieves near-instant boots, zero garbage collection pauses, and exceptional battery life.
  • Fluid 60 FPS GPU acceleration — Direct hardware rendering through OpenGL ES on the Mali GPU (DRM + Lima driver) brings silky 60 FPS animations and snappy wheel scrolling to the Y2’s landscape screen.
  • Wi-Fi Internet Radio — Stream thousands of live radio stations worldwide over Wi-Fi directly through the radio-browser.info directory, organized by genre, country, and language with station logos and track info.
  • Last.fm scrobbling with QR login — Pair your Last.fm account in seconds simply by scanning an on-screen QR code with your phone. Enjoy seamless offline playback with an automatic queue that scrobbles your tracks as soon as you reconnect to Wi-Fi.
  • Audiophile DSP & 47 AutoEQ presets — Shape your sound with a 5-band graphic EQ, 47 calibrated AutoEQ headphone profiles for popular IEMs, custom 10-filter parametric EQ (ParametricEQ.txt), ReplayGain 2.0 volume leveling, and gapless playback.
  • 16 built-in skins & custom theme engine — Switch between 16 distinctive built-in themes (Classic iPod, Nano Glass, Walkman, Winamp, Terminal, Paper, Big Type, Cassette, Neon Night, Zune, Bunny Bow, and 5 Mini colours) or drop custom skins into /media/sd/Skins.
  • Wi-Fi Over-The-Air (OTA) updates — No computer needed for software upgrades after initial installation. Update directly from the player via Settings -> About & Updates -> Update Now.

What the community says

Y2Reborn represents a major technological milestone in the Innioasis community: bringing full embedded Linux and high-performance Rust software to dedicated portable hardware. Schulzcode’s release has redefined what the Y2 can do, transforming it from a budget media player into an audiophile-focused, community-extensible powerhouse.

The community actively shares custom skins, EQ profiles, and station lists on the r/innioasis subreddit and community Discord, praising the dramatic increase in responsiveness and audio quality compared to factory Android builds.

What the project offers

  • Custom Y2Linux kernel + Buildroot runtime eliminating Android overhead
  • Native Rust audio player binary (reborn) by Schulzcode
  • Direct GPU hardware acceleration via OpenGL ES DRM/Lima at smooth 60 FPS
  • Pinned FFmpeg 9.0.2 audio decoding: MP3, AAC, ALAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, Ogg Vorbis, Opus, DSD
  • Bit-perfect wired ALSA audio output (44.1kHz and 48kHz S16 stereo)
  • BlueALSA Bluetooth stack supporting LDAC, AAC (256kbit/s for AirPods), and SBC-XQ
  • Intelligent auto handover between wired 3.5mm jack and Bluetooth
  • Wi-Fi Internet Radio powered by radio-browser.info with search, genres, and station logos
  • Last.fm scrobbling with frictionless QR-code phone authentication
  • Offline scrobble cache that syncs automatically when Wi-Fi reconnects
  • Wi-Fi Over-The-Air (OTA) system updates directly on the device
  • 47 built-in AutoEQ headphone presets calibrated for leading IEMs and headphones
  • 5-band graphic equalizer + custom 10-filter ParametricEQ.txt support
  • ReplayGain 2.0 volume normalization (track and album modes)
  • True gapless playback and configurable crossfade (up to 12s)
  • 16 built-in themes (Classic, Nano Glass, Walkman, Winamp, Terminal, Paper, Big Type, Cassette, Neon Night, Zune, Bunny Bow, 5 Mini colours)
  • Custom skin loader reading /media/sd/Skins/
  • Ergonomic click-wheel on-screen keyboard for fast searching and Wi-Fi passwords
  • Dual-storage indexing (internal storage + MicroSD card) with instant SQLite search-as-you-type

Things to know first

  • Engineered exclusively for the Innioasis Y2 — do not attempt to flash onto a Y1
  • Replaces the Android operating system with native Linux via Updater CE (rom_y2.zip)
  • Reversible at any time: select Y2 and Original Software in Updater CE to restore the factory software
  • MicroSD cards should be formatted as FAT32 or exFAT for seamless media and skin detection
