Installation Steps

  1. Khởi chạy Innioasis Trình cập nhật CE on your Windows, Mac, or Linux computer.
  2. chọn Y1 from the Device Model dropdown.
  3. chọn Rondo from the Software dropdown.
  4. nhấp chuột Cài đặt / Khôi phục and wait for the tool to download and prepare the files.
  5. When prompted by Updater CE, power off your Y1 and connect the USB cable.
  6. Complete post-install setup
