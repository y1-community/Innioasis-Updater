Innioasis Y1 firmware

The Y1 is the 4-inch touchscreen player. Updater CE installs the firmware. Pick the package that matches the model printed on the player.

Y1 update and stock firmware

The Original Software is the stock system. A newer build of that is the usual update. Better-Y and Koensayr stay close to that system and keep its themes. Solar adds Wi-Fi and on-player downloads. Rockbox is a different system, with its own themes.

Y1 themes and skins

Themes for the Original Software, Better-Y, Koensayr and Solar are in the Themes Gallery. People also call them Y1 skins. Rockbox themes are a separate pack.

If the Y1 will not start

Charge it, then try the steps in the recovery guide. Owners often call that an unbrick. The unbrick page points at the same recovery files for the Y1.
