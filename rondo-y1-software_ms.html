Installation Steps

  1. Pelancaran Innioasis Pengemaskini CE on your Windows, Mac, or Linux computer.
  2. Pilih Y1 from the Device Model dropdown.
  3. Pilih Rondo from the Software dropdown.
  4. klik Pasang / Pulihkan and wait for the tool to download and prepare the files.
  5. When prompted by Updater CE, power off your Y1 and connect the USB cable.
  6. Complete post-install setup
