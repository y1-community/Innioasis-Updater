Solar is a custom firmware and launcher for the Innioasis Y1 — a full interface replacement with podcast downloads, a global quick menu, Reach (Deezer + Soulseek), Y1 theme support, and Rockbox-Y1 on the same ROM (switch without reboot).

Features

  • 🎵 Reach — search, play and download music from Deezer and Soulseek
  • 🎙️ Podcasts — search and download episodes to your Y1 over the internet
  • ⚡ Quick menu — global context menu for playback, queue, Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, and more
  • 🎨 Y1 themes — install and apply themes from the original Y1 firmware
  • 🔊 Rockbox-Y1 — co-installed; switch launchers from Settings (no reboot)

Assets

  • app-release.apk — signed Solar launcher
  • rom.zip — Y1 type A firmware (2.0.0+, MT6572)
  • rom_type_b.zip — Y1 type B firmware (before 2.0.0, MT6572)
