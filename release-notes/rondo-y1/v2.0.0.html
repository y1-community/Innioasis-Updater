Rondo 2.0.0

A lightweight, custom audio operating system for the Innioasis Y1.

For the Innioasis Y1, Type A. A new firmware: install rom.zip with Innioasis Updater CE (choose Keep Data to keep your settings when coming from 1.0). After that, Rondo updates itself over Wi-Fi from GitHub.

Rondo 2.0 is rebuilt as a program of its own: Android's app layer is gone, the Y1 starts straight into Rondo, and everything runs smoother at 60 frames a second.

  • YouTube and YouTube Music straight from the Y1, no server needed (your own server is optional and still works). Sign in with a code, like a smart TV.
  • Subtitles on YouTube videos and on the videos on the card, in a look of your own (YouTube's by default).
  • Download YouTube videos at 720p, 480p or 360p, with their subtitles, to watch offline.
  • Library in four: Music (with Cover Flow, album artists, genres, smart playlists), Videos, Photos and Files.
  • Sound: an equalizer for each output, Headphone match (with a server), loudness boost, volume leveling, the amplifier and a volume limit.
  • Software update from GitHub, with or without a server; the dashboard has every setting too.

Everything else is in the changelog.

Files: rondo-2.0.0.rondo is the signed update bundle; rom.zip (the firmware) and updater.png are added by the release workflow a few minutes after publishing.
