Rondo 3.0.1

A lightweight, custom audio operating system for the Innioasis Y1.

For the Innioasis Y1, Type A, on the Rondo 3.0 firmware. An update you can install over Wi-Fi.

  • Charging shows on the battery. On a charger or a computer, a green bolt shows next to the battery in the status bar and the YouTube headers, and the charge turns green.
  • YouTube and YouTube Music without internet. With Wi-Fi off they now say "No Wi-Fi connection" at once instead of "Loading…", and load by themselves when Wi-Fi is back. On a Wi-Fi network without internet they give up after 30 seconds at most and say they couldn't reach YouTube.
  • Sharper subtitles. Their lines sat between two rows of pixels, which blurred every letter.
  • The rest of SPWall is gone too: its packet filter, code injector and renamed iptables, which nothing started, and the stock firmware's distribution tag. Rondo removes them itself when it updates, and the firmware leaves them out.

How to get it

  • On Rondo 3.0.0: Settings › System › Software update, the dashboard's Overview, or the update page over USB.
  • On Rondo 2.0 or earlier: Innioasis Updater CE (Y1, Type A, Rondo 3.0.1), with Keep my settings turned on.

Files: rondo-3.0.1.rondo is the signed update bundle; rom.zip (the firmware) and updater.png are added by the release workflow a few minutes after publishing. Everything else: changelog.
