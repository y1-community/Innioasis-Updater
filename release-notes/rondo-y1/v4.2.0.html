Podcasts: any show with a public feed, on the main menu, and YouTube Music's own. An update for the 4.0 firmware: Settings › System › Software update, the dashboard or the update page. It works with Rondo server 3.1.0 or newer; YouTube Music's podcasts through a server need Rondo server 3.7.0, released with it.

New

Podcasts on the main menu, from the shows' own feeds (RSS), as podcast apps get them:

  • Podcasts, from their feeds (a new Podcasts entry on the main menu, after Library; hide it in

    Settings › Display › Theme › Main menu): Continue listening, New episodes (what your shows put out since you subscribed, for a month), Your shows, Save for later, Top shows (Apple's charts for your country, by category), Downloaded and Search podcasts (Apple's directory, and Podcast Index's with a free key). The Y1 reads the feeds and plays the episodes straight from each podcast's host, with or without a Rondo server.

  • A show's page: Subscribe, its speed, Download new episodes (on Wi-Fi, the latest 1

    to 10 kept on the card, played ones deleted if you like; nothing downloads by itself otherwise), About this show, and its episodes, newest first (a show told in order: episode 1 first), with a dot on the ones you haven't started and the time left on the ones you have. Thousands of episodes scroll as quickly as a few.

  • Episodes play as audiobooks do: their place kept, a little earlier after a pause, chapters

    (the feed's chapter file or the MP3's own) or 30 s and 15 s skips, bookmarks, the sleep timer, Episodes up next and Back to your music; speed is kept per show. A video episode opens in the video player where you left it, and Listen only plays its sound with the screen off. An episode's options also have Show notes.

  • New episodes are looked for when Podcasts opens and every 4 hours while the Y1 is on or

    playing. Podcast settings (Podcasts' last row) has Look for new episodes and the Country for the directory and the charts (the United States unless you choose another).

  • Downloads go to Podcasts/<show> on the card, as the podcast's own file, beside YouTube

    Music's episodes.

  • The dashboard's Podcasts page has these shows first (YouTube Music's below): search, Top shows,

    every list, a show's page with Subscribe, speed and Download new episodes, every episode's options, Add by address (a private or paid show's feed, read only by the Y1), Import OPML and Export OPML to move your shows from and to other podcast apps, the country, and without a server a Podcast Index key of the Y1's own.

They need no server at all. With Rondo server 3.7.0, search finds Podcast Index's shows too (with the key on its admin page), and big feeds come from it a page at a time.

Podcasts in YouTube Music, in a tab of their own:

  • Podcasts, from YouTube Music (YouTube Music › Podcasts, a tab beside Home and Library):

    your saved shows, Episodes for Later, New episodes, what's downloaded, **Continue listening**, and search for shows and episodes (song search offers matching podcasts too; Home's shelves open shows and play episodes). A show's page has Save to library, its speed and its episodes newest first, with a dot on the ones you haven't heard and the time left on the ones you've started; hundreds load as you scroll.

  • Episodes play as audiobooks do: each remembers where you stopped and starts a little earlier

    after a pause, Next and Previous move by YouTube's chapters (or skip 30 s and 15 s), with bookmarks, the sleep timer and Back to your music. Speed is kept per show. Your music waits.

  • Episodes up next: Play next and Add to queue put episodes after the episode or book playing, or

    after the song playing; each starts where you left it.

  • Played marks are shared with YouTube Music on your phone, both ways; an episode started on the

    phone opens about where you left it there.

  • Download an episode to the card (Podcasts/<show>) to play it offline, chapters and place

    included. Episodes stay out of Songs and Shuffle songs.

  • The dashboard's Podcasts page (a tab of its own): the same lists, a show's page with its speed,

    and every episode's options, played on the Y1.

Through a server, YouTube Music's podcasts need Rondo server 3.7.0; directly to YouTube they work as they are. With an older server, YouTube Music › Podcasts says so and still plays what's on the card.

Changed

  • YouTube Music's header shows the logo without the word "Music" and the battery alone when it has

    three tabs (Home, Library, Podcasts), as YouTube's does.

Fixed

  • A book or an episode started after the card came back from a computer (the USB drive) jumped

    to where the music had been at its first pause and play.

  • Big PNG pictures (a 3000 × 3000 cover, common for podcasts and some albums) show instead of an

    empty square: they're read a row at a time, without needing 36 MB at once.

Updating to Rondo 4.2.0

The same steps install this release for the first time or replace the build already on the player. Back up your music before you start.

  1. Check the model and type

    Confirm the player is an Innioasis Y1 before downloading. Y1 players come in two hardware revisions, Type A (software 2.0.0 or later) and Type B (earlier software); the two ROMs are not interchangeable.

  2. Download the package built for your computer

    Use Updater CE for the guided route, or download the portable package for Windows, macOS or Linux from this page. The release's own ZIP is also listed for manual flashing.

  3. Connect the player while it is switched off

    Use a data-capable USB cable and connect the player only when the installer asks. Keep it connected until the flash reports success.

  4. Verify the version after it reboots

    Open the player's Settings or About screen and check that it reports Rondo 4.2.0.

Questions about this update

How do I update Innioasis Y1 to Rondo 4.2.0?

Download the package for your computer from this page, or open Updater CE and choose Rondo 4.2.0 for Innioasis Y1. Connect the player while it is switched off, then let the installer finish and let the player reboot.

Is Rondo 4.2.0 the latest Rondo release?

The release list on this page is ordered newest first, so the top entry is the latest published build. Preview and nightly builds are labelled as such.

Can I install Rondo 4.2.0 from another computer?

Yes. Download the ZIP here, copy it to the computer that can talk to the player, and follow the install guide there. The package is self-contained.

Which file do I download for Windows, macOS or Linux?

Each portable package in the download section is built for one computer. The guide pages linked under "Install on your computer" name the exact file and its size.
