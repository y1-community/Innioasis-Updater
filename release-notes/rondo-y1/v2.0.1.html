For the Innioasis Y1, Type A. A fix release for Rondo 2.0.

  • Wi-Fi works after a fresh install. On a Y1 that installed 2.0.0 without keeping its data, Wi-Fi never came on ("Wi-Fi didn't start"): Rondo powered the Wi-Fi chip on while its driver was still loading, and the chip stayed stuck until the next restart. Rondo now waits for the driver first (#3).
  • Next and Previous in Now Playing slide smoothly: the slide starts on the press, and the covers of the songs before and after are ready before you press.

How to get it

  • Wi-Fi not working on 2.0.0? Install with Innioasis Updater CE (Y1, Type A, Rondo 2.0.1), or over USB from the update page in Chrome or Edge.
  • Wi-Fi working: Settings › System › Software update, or the dashboard's Overview.
  • Coming from 1.0: Innioasis Updater CE, with Keep Data to keep your settings.

Files: rondo-2.0.1.rondo is the signed update bundle; rom.zip (the firmware) and updater.png are added by the release workflow a few minutes after publishing. Everything else: changelog.
