2.4.0-koensayr-3.0.7

Koensayr is a mod for the Y1's OS that makes the following improvements to the original OS:

Browsing the albums of an artist from Main Menu > Artists > Albums

Adds Bluetooth remote control support and track info displayed on your bluetooth receiver, perfect for using your Y1 in a car with the infotainment system.
