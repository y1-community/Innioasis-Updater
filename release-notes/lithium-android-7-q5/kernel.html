Lithium 5 Kernel Beta v0.06.04.25

This is a beta release of the Lithium 5 Android kernel & ramdisk, with built-in Magisk support.

You can use this kernel with any other Android 5 ROM for Timmkoo devices, and you are welcome to modify the kernel and contribute with any changes.

If you have any issues, feel free to join the discussion, or join the Discord server.

Full Changelog: https://github.com/NoahDomingues/Lithium/commits/Kernel
