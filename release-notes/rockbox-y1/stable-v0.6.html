Since v0.5 most work went into stability updates and merging upstream updates into this fork to keep it up-to-date.

Fork specific changes:

  • Various plugin bug fixes
  • Plugin to turn off the internal speaker (by @Ferrhat)
  • Better support for AirPods (by @Semy0nBu)
  • Switching between Rockbox and Stock (well, a modified better-Y by @Leska1337) without reboot
  • Many new themes! Check them out here: https://github.com/rockbox-y1/themes/releases/tag/for_the_gays_and_the_theys

My work will now fully focus on bringing this fork closer to upstream Rockbox by removing most custom features from the Rockbox code and moving them into a dedicated settings menu. This also means the external apps launcher will come back (and many new things).
