Y1 experimental 1 (20261111-exp.1-y1)

Classy build for the Innioasis Y2, formerly Inniclassic for Y2.

y1-exp.1Y22026-11-11Preview buildClassyPlayer/Classy
Get it in Updater CEFirmware Downloads

Very experimental build for the Innioasis Y1 (Android 4.2), to collect logs for #82. Nobody has tested it on a Y1. It can stay on the boot logo. Do not use it on a Y2.

  • rom_y1_type_a.zip or rom_y1_type_b.zip: rename to rom.zip and flash with the Innioasis Updater.
  • y1-logs.sh (macOS, Linux) or y1-logs.bat (Windows): collect the logs with adb into one zip file per boot.
  • To go back: flash the stock Y1 firmware with the Innioasis Updater.
  • Changes: branch exp/y1-sdk17. Instructions: #82.
