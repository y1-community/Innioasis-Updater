Lithium 7 Kernel v7.1.06.09.25

This is a first official release of the Lithium 7 Android kernel & ramdisk, with built-in Xposed support.

You can use this kernel with some other Android 7 ROMs for Timmkoo devices, and you are welcome to modify the kernel and contribute with any changes.

If you have any issues, feel free to join the discussion, or join the Discord server.

Full Changelog: https://github.com/NoahDomingues/Lithium/commits/Kernel
