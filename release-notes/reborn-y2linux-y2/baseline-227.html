Y2Linux 1.0 Beta 9.3.18: USB that always reconnects, Luna XP, bitrate, quicker settings. Linux 6.18 and the native Reborn music player for the Innioasis Y2.

Already running Y2Linux

Settings → System → Software Update → Update Now (on Beta 9.2.8 and older: Settings → About & Updates → Check for Updates → Update Now).

What changed

  • A computer always finds the player over USB again (#102). If the cable came out while the player was restarting its file transfer service, the USB chip could switch itself into host mode, and no computer saw the player again until a restart. The player now returns to device mode after every unplug, sleep or reconnect.
  • New skin: Luna XP, contributed by eNKay. Seventeen skins now.
  • Bitrate for skins: {bitrate} shows "320 kbps" (#87).
  • Updates with Wi-Fi off (Y2Reborn #96): Software Update shows Connect to Wi-Fi instead of a check that cannot work.
  • Quick Menu (Y2Reborn #101): hold Select on the Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or Brightness tile to open its settings.
  • Artist's Albums (Y2Reborn #75): Settings → Customize → Browsing → Artist's Albums: Oldest First, Newest First or A to Z.
  • Cover Flow follows the skin (Y2Reborn #100): each skin's colours, fonts and cover shape.
  • Files the player cannot play (Y2Reborn #104) such as WMA or DSD are named in Settings → Library instead of missing silently.

Before release: the USB fault reproduced on the developer's player and gone with this build (connected again 0.5 s after the reconnect, before: never), new tests for each change, the platform's full check, Reborn's workspace tests and the tests of the release images. Installed by update on the developer's player.

What changed and why, in plain words: Beta 9.3.18 report.

Install (from stock)

You need SP Flash Tool v5.2032, a USB cable, Y2Linux-1.0.0-beta.9.3.18-first-install.zip below and the Innioasis Y2 firmware package v3.2.0 from Innioasis (only this version works; it is also your way back to stock).

  1. Unpack both.
  2. SP Flash Tool → Download Only → load MT6582_Android_scatter.txt from the Y2Linux zip. Exactly BOOTIMG, RECOVERY, ANDROID and USRDATA are ticked.
  3. Click Download and connect the switched-off Y2; wait for the green tick.
  4. Unplug and press Power: the player sets up its Internal Storage (a few seconds), then Y2Linux and Reborn start, music works.
  5. Connect the Y2 to the computer and copy (don't move) system.img from the Innioasis package into the Firmware folder on Internal Storage. The player takes the Wi-Fi/Bluetooth firmware from it and turns the radios on.

Known issues (beta)

  • After a sleep (a pause longer than 15 minutes, or the Power button) the Bluetooth link ends; the player reconnects within a few seconds of waking. Buds that have gone back to a phone meanwhile must be picked again in Settings → Bluetooth.
  • WPA3-only and Enhanced Open Wi-Fi networks can't be joined (hardware); ask for "WPA2/WPA3 mixed" where you can. A router that keeps refusing the player (#83) is still being worked on.
  • Everything listed for Beta 9.0 still applies. If anything behaves differently from 9.3.17, send an error report (Settings → System → Error Reports); Restore Previous System (Settings → System → About, tap Version seven times, then Diagnostics) goes back to the version you had, with your data kept.
  • Battery life in sleep: about 2 mA overnight on the developer's player (estimated from the battery voltage); stock is about 1.3 mA.
  • Sony WH-1000XM4 on LDAC with Wi-Fi on can still drop out (Y2Reborn #87), and a soundbar stutters with Wi-Fi on (#99); AAC or Wi-Fi off is steadier. Reports help.
  • File names longer than 255 bytes (about 85 Chinese or Japanese characters) fit on the SD card but not on Internal Storage. Bluetooth headphones that offer only mono sound cannot play yet.
  • A charge-only USB cable only charges: the player cannot tell it from a wall charger. Skins with their own fonts (Luna XP, Winamp) show boxes for Chinese, Japanese and Korean titles.
  • Some players turned off by themselves during playback; the cause is not found yet. If yours does, start it again and send a report.
  • The speaker uses the same volume as the headphones; its loudest step is the stock speaker's. Start low the first time.
  • BBC radio stations stream only in HLS, which the player does not play yet. FM radio is in the works for a later beta.
