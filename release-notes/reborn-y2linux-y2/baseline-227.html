Y2Linux 1.0 Beta 9.3.18: USB that always reconnects, a new skin and quicker settings.

What's new

  • Luna XP skin: blue title bars, a green start button and rolling hills, made by eNKay. Pick it in Settings → Skin.
  • Quick settings from the Quick Menu: hold Power to open the Quick Menu, then hold Select on Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or Brightness to jump straight to that setting.
  • Sort an artist's albums your way: Settings → Customize → Browsing → Artist's Albums: Oldest First, Newest First or A to Z.
  • Cover Flow matches your skin: its colours, fonts and cover shape now follow the skin you picked.
  • Updates without Wi-Fi: when Wi-Fi is off, Software Update shows Connect to Wi-Fi and takes you there, instead of a check that can't work.
  • Songs the player can't play: if your card has files the player can't play (WMA or DSD, for example), Settings → Library now tells you how many and which kind, so they no longer just seem to be missing.
  • For skin makers: {bitrate} shows a song's bitrate, like "320 kbps".

Fixed

  • USB connection: after unplugging at the wrong moment, a computer sometimes never found the player again until it was restarted. The player now reconnects every time.

Known issues

  • A charge-only USB cable only charges. If your computer doesn't see the player, try a cable that transfers data with a phone.
  • Skins with their own fonts (Luna XP, Winamp and others) show boxes instead of Chinese, Japanese and Korean titles.
  • After a sleep, Bluetooth headphones reconnect within a few seconds of waking. Headphones that went back to a phone meanwhile must be picked again in Settings → Bluetooth.
  • WPA3-only Wi-Fi networks can't be joined; a "WPA2/WPA3 mixed" setting on the router works.
  • Some players turned off by themselves during playback; the cause isn't found yet.
  • Something wrong? Send an error report (Settings → System → Error Reports). Restore Previous System (Settings → System → About, tap Version seven times, then Diagnostics) takes you back to the version you had, with your data kept.

More details in plain words: Beta 9.3.18 report.

Already running Y2Linux

Settings → System → Software Update → Update Now. Your music, playlists, settings, Wi-Fi networks and Bluetooth pairings stay. (On Beta 9.2.8 and older: Settings → About & Updates → Check for Updates → Update Now.)

Install with the Innioasis Updater (easiest)

The community Innioasis Updater CE installs Y2Linux for you, on Windows, macOS and Linux. No SP Flash Tool needed.

  1. Download Innioasis Updater CE for your computer from innioasis.app and open it.
  2. Choose Y2, then Reborn (Y2Linux) and the latest release.
  3. Click Install / Restore, switch the Y2 off and connect it when the updater asks. Wait until it confirms, then unplug.
  4. Connect the Y2 to the computer and copy system.img from the Innioasis Y2 firmware package v3.2.0 into the Firmware folder on Internal Storage. The player takes the Wi-Fi/Bluetooth firmware from it and turns the radios on.

Step-by-step guides for each computer: Reborn (Y2Linux) install guide. The way back to stock is in the updater too: choose Y2 and Original Software.

Install with SP Flash Tool

You need SP Flash Tool v5.2032, a USB cable, Y2Linux-1.0.0-beta.9.3.18-first-install.zip below and the Innioasis Y2 firmware package v3.2.0 from Innioasis (only this version works; it is also your way back to stock).

  1. Unpack both.
  2. SP Flash Tool → Download Only → load MT6582_Android_scatter.txt from the Y2Linux zip. Exactly BOOTIMG, RECOVERY, ANDROID and USRDATA are ticked.
  3. Click Download and connect the switched-off Y2; wait for the green tick.
  4. Unplug and press Power: the player sets up its Internal Storage (a few seconds), then Y2Linux and Reborn start, music works.
  5. Connect the Y2 to the computer and copy (don't move) system.img from the Innioasis package into the Firmware folder on Internal Storage. The player takes the Wi-Fi/Bluetooth firmware from it and turns the radios on.

How it was tested

The USB fault was reproduced on the developer's player and is gone with this build (it reconnects half a second after plugging in; before, never). Every change has new tests, and the platform's full check, Reborn's workspace tests and the tests of the release images passed. Installed by update on the developer's player.
