Y2Linux 1.0 Beta 9.3.20: audiobooks keep your place, albums play in order, steadier Wi-Fi after waking.

What's new

  • Previous Chapter / Next Chapter for audiobooks: hold Select on Now Playing. They jump to the chapter start however far away it is.
  • SBC Auto Rate in Diagnostics (Settings → System → About, tap Version seven times): lets SBC headphones lower the rate when the signal gets weak instead of stuttering. Off by default; if you hear Bluetooth dropouts, please try it and tell us.

Fixed

  • Audiobooks jumped back to the start of a long chapter when Previous or Next was pressed by accident (in a pocket, say). The buttons now go to a chapter start only when it is less than 10 minutes away; otherwise they skip 30 seconds. A book you started also keeps its place if you stopped at its very beginning.
  • An album started from the album page played shuffled when Shuffle was on. A single album now always plays in order; playlists and mixed songs still shuffle.
  • Skins: Cover Flow titles stood still while the covers slid. They now glide and fade with the covers.
  • Wi-Fi after waking could give up on a network that was right there and restart, and after a sleep without headphones the player kept looking for them for almost a minute, which held Wi-Fi up. It now looks once and keeps trying a network it can see.
  • A very long audiobook file was missing without a word: one file of more than about 50 hours is too big for the player to read. Settings → Library now counts it as a file that couldn't be read.

Known issues

  • A charge-only USB cable only charges. If your computer doesn't see the player, try a cable that transfers data with a phone.
  • Skins with their own fonts (Luna XP, Winamp and others) show boxes instead of Chinese, Japanese and Korean titles.
  • After a sleep, Bluetooth headphones reconnect within a few seconds of waking. Headphones that went back to a phone meanwhile must be picked again in Settings → Bluetooth.
  • WPA3-only Wi-Fi networks can't be joined; a "WPA2/WPA3 mixed" setting on the router works.
  • One audiobook file longer than about 36 hours may not play or not show up; split it into parts of up to 30 hours (most tools that make M4B files can).
  • Some players turned off by themselves during playback; the cause isn't found yet.
  • Something wrong? Send an error report (Settings → System → Error Reports). Restore Previous System (Settings → System → About, tap Version seven times, then Diagnostics) takes you back to the version you had, with your data kept.
