Y2Linux 1.0 Beta 9.3.25: the Y2 plays videos. This is an early version of video playback: expect bugs, and please report them.

What's new

  • Videos (early version). Copy video files onto Internal Storage or the SD card, the same way as songs; any folder works, and a folder of your own such as Videos keeps them together. After the next scan Videos appears on Home, with a picture of each film. A film plays on Now Playing: Play/Pause, Previous/Next, the wheel and the volume work as for a song, a button press shows the title and a time line, and a film goes on where you left it. The screen stays on while a film plays. Films never show up among your songs, albums or shuffles.
  • Subtitles show when the file carries a text track, or when a subtitle file with the same name lies beside the film (Film.mkv and Film.srt, .ass, .ssa or .vtt).
  • The best format: MP4 with H.264 video and AAC sound, 640×360, up to 30 pictures a second. The screen has 480×360 pixels, so a bigger picture looks no better, only fills the storage and uses more battery. About 1 Mbit/s is plenty: a two-hour film is then under 1 GB.
  • What plays: .mp4 .m4v .mov .mkv .webm .avi. H.264 and MPEG-4 (Xvid, DivX) are decoded by the player's video chip, up to 1920×1080. VP8, VP9, DivX 3 and H.263 are decoded by the processor and are only smooth at small sizes (about 640×360).
  • What does not play: H.265/HEVC, AV1, DTS sound, picture subtitles (DVD, Blu-ray), files with a picture and no sound. Convert such films first, and big ones too: see Videos on the Y2 for a ready command and HandBrake settings.
  • A song tagged "Pop/Rock" is now in Pop and in Rock (Y2Reborn #124). Tagging programs that join several genres with "/" are understood; "Singer/Songwriter" stays one genre.

How fast it is (measured on one player)

  • H.264 1280×720 at 30 pictures a second and 1920×1080 at 24: no picture dropped, the player used about 60 % of one of its four processor cores.
  • Xvid 1280×720 at 25 pictures a second: no picture dropped, 57 % of one core.
  • VP9 640×360: no picture dropped, 80 % of one core. Bigger VP9 files will stutter.

Known issues

  • Video is new. Sleep and wake while a film is open have not been tested, and nobody has measured how long the battery lasts with films. Leave Now Playing before you put the player to sleep if you see trouble.
  • Over Bluetooth the sound comes a little later than the picture (the headphones' own delay).
  • The first text subtitle and the default sound track are used; there is no menu to choose another or to turn subtitles off.
  • Skins show their own lists, but the film itself always uses Reborn's own screen.
  • Interlaced Xvid/DivX films and 10-bit H.264 are decoded by the processor and may stutter above DVD size.
  • On a card formatted as FAT32 a file can't be larger than 4 GB.
  • Everything listed for Beta 9.3.24 still applies.
  • Something wrong? Send an error report (Settings → System → Error Reports). Restore Previous System (Settings → System → About, tap Version seven times, then Diagnostics) takes you back to the version you had, with your data kept.
