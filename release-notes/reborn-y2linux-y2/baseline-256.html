Y2Linux 1.0 Beta 9.3.28: a fast Audiobooks list for big collections, and films that keep playing their picture.

Fixed

  • Audiobooks with many books was very slow (Y2Reborn #27). With a few hundred books made of many small files, the list and its Cover Flow crawled and it took minutes to reach a letter. The list is now prepared once instead of several times for every picture on the screen, and a fast turn of the wheel moves through it quickly, as in Songs.
  • A film's picture could freeze after seeking (Y2Reborn #129). Seeking back while the picture was still catching up from the seek before left it standing, with the sound playing on, until the sound got back to that point. The picture now follows every seek.
  • Some films stopped after the first pictures while the sound played on. Films made with few reference pictures (as phones and screen recorders often make them) ran the video chip out of picture memory. They now play.

Known issues

  • Everything listed for Beta 9.3.27 still applies.
  • Something wrong? Send an error report (Settings → System → Error Reports). Restore Previous System (Settings → System → About, tap Version seven times, then Diagnostics) takes you back to the version you had, with your data kept.
