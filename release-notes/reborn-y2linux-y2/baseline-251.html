Y2Linux 1.0 Beta 9.3.26: FM radio is back, for Y2s whose hardware receives FM. Also: Next and Previous move between films, and a car can pair with the Y2 from its own screen.

What's new

  • FM radio (for later-revision Y2s). Radio on Home now opens as FM Radio and Internet Radio. Plug in wired headphones (their cable is the antenna, also when the sound goes to Bluetooth), choose Search for Stations, then pick a station. Now Playing shows the frequency and Stereo or Mono; volume, outputs and Favorites work as for internet radio. Not every Y2 can receive FM: on the first revision only noise arrives, whatever software runs. If a search finds nothing, Hide FM Radio puts Radio back as it was (switch: Settings → Customize → Browsing → FM Radio). Details: FM radio on the Y2.
  • FM needs two small files from system.img. A player set up before this release doesn't have them: copy system.img (Innioasis Y2 firmware package v3.2.0) into the Firmware folder on Internal Storage once more. The player takes the files within a minute and deletes the copy; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth keep working.
  • Pair with a car from the car's screen (#116). Settings → Bluetooth → Search for Devices now also makes the player visible as Y2Linux for 2 minutes. Start the search on the Y2, add a new device on the car's Bluetooth screen and pick Y2Linux, then confirm the number the Y2 shows. The car is remembered and reconnects like headphones.

Fixed

  • Next and Previous during a film go to the next or previous film, not to a song (Y2Reborn #128). Films play in the order of the Videos list, also with Shuffle on.

Known issues

  • FM reception has not been confirmed on any player yet: the developer's Y2 is a first revision and receives nothing. If you have a later revision, please tell us whether stations play.
  • FM stops when the player goes to sleep by the Power button and does not come back by itself after the wake. There are no station names (RDS) yet.
  • Pairing from a car was tested without a car. If your car doesn't list Y2Linux, or pairing fails, please send an error report right after trying.
  • Everything listed for Beta 9.3.25 still applies.
  • Something wrong? Send an error report (Settings → System → Error Reports). Restore Previous System (Settings → System → About, tap Version seven times, then Diagnostics) takes you back to the version you had, with your data kept.
