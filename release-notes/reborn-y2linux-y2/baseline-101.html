Y2Linux 1.0 Beta 8.8: error reports you can save and send. Linux 6.18 and the native Reborn music player for the Innioasis Y2.

Already running Y2Linux

Settings → About & Updates → Check for Updates → Update Now.

What's new

  • Save an error report. Settings → About & Updates → Error Reports → Save Report, choose Internal Storage or SD Card and what the problem is about. In about ten seconds the player writes its logs and technical details, with your music, names, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, accounts and serial numbers removed, to an Error Reports folder on that storage. Copy the file over USB and attach it to your issue on GitHub. Nothing is sent on its own: sending reports over Wi-Fi comes with a later update, and only if you turn it on. Details: Error reports.
  • Reports include the charger's own state, which helps with charging problems such as #47.

Everything from Beta 8.7 is included.

Install (from stock)

You need SP Flash Tool v5.2032, a USB cable, Y2Linux-1.0.0-beta.8.8-first-install.zip below and the Innioasis Y2 firmware package v3.2.0 from Innioasis (only this version works; it is also your way back to stock).

  1. Unpack both.
  2. SP Flash Tool → Download Only → load MT6582_Android_scatter.txt from the Y2Linux zip. Exactly BOOTIMG, RECOVERY, ANDROID and USRDATA are ticked.
  3. Click Download and connect the switched-off Y2; wait for the green tick.
  4. Unplug and press Power: the player sets up its Internal Storage (a few seconds), then Y2Linux and Reborn start, music works.
  5. Connect the Y2 to the computer and copy (don't move) system.img from the Innioasis package into the Firmware folder on Internal Storage. The player takes the Wi-Fi/Bluetooth firmware from it and turns the radios on.

Known issues (beta)

  • Error reports are saved only; uploading over Wi-Fi comes with a later update.
  • The highest volume is still lower than stock's, even at High; demanding headphones may want more.
  • A headphone profile still uses some extra processor time while it plays; its effect on battery life has not been measured yet.
  • The restart fix of Beta 8.4 is not yet confirmed over many restarts. If the screen ever stays dark, hold Power for 10 s and start again.
  • Battery life in sleep is not yet stock-like (about 8 mA vs stock 1.3 mA; some players draw more, #42).
  • GC9503V players keep the old screen-off behaviour until a GC9503V owner has confirmed the new sequence (#45).
  • Some players don't charge under Y2Linux (#47); a saved report helps find out why.
  • The player cannot detect wired headphones yet; the jack plays whenever no Bluetooth headphones are connected.
  • The fast copy path has not yet been tested with the cable pulled mid-copy, across sleep, or with Windows Explorer.
  • Songs with several artists ("A;B") count as one artist; there is no Album Artist view yet.
  • FM radio is in the works for a later beta.
