Stock & recovery

Stock Firmware, Unbricking & Supported Models

Where can I find stock firmware to unbrick my player?

The Community Firmware Archive hosts official factory stock firmware and original restore ROMs. Whether you need to fix a bricked player or revert to factory software, select your device model and download the verified stock package or use Updater CE for guided flashing.

Which models does the Community Firmware Archive support?

The archive hosts verified firmwares for Innioasis Y1, Y2, G1, G3, G5, Q8, R1, PR1 and Timmkoo Q5, Q3. New firmware packages published for any of these models automatically appear in the firmware directory.

Can I return from custom firmware to factory software?

Yes. The stock restore is a standard firmware flash: in Updater CE, select your model, choose Original Software, and click Install / Restore. Never use files meant for a different model.

What each route gives you

Pick the software that matches how you listen.

Original Software

The software your player shipped with. Local music, Bluetooth, and the familiar interface with nothing extra to configure. It is the safe baseline and the quickest way back if a custom install does not behave.

Restore on Y1 · Restore on Y2

Better-Y

An enhanced stock firmware for the Innioasis Y1 by Leska1337. Multi-artist/genre tag splitting, track numbers in album views, Now Playing shortcuts, smoother RAM caching, and 100% theme compatibility.

Better-Y on Y1 · Why Better-Y?

Y2Reborn

A full Linux-based firmware for the Innioasis Y2 by SchulzCode. Native Wi-Fi support, Last.fm scrobbling, modern audio decoding, and active development.

Y2Reborn on Y2 · Why Y2Reborn?

Rockbox

The music-lover's route. A proper database that sorts albums and artists by tags instead of file names, gapless playback, crossfade, an equalizer, and a deep theme library including iPod-style interfaces. It is built around local files, so it stays offline.

Rockbox on Y1 · Rockbox on Y2 · Why Rockbox?

Solar

The network-first route. It enables Wi-Fi on the Y1 and Y2 and adds streaming and downloads: Deezer and Soulseek through its Reach music search, podcast downloads, and YouTube playback. A stem player can split a song into vocals, drums, bass, and melody for on-device remixing, and Rockbox rides on the same install so you can switch without reflashing.

Solar on Y1 · Solar on Y2 · Why Solar?

JJ Launcher

A fast, wheel-friendly launcher. Rapid library scanning, equalizer presets, track skipping with the hardware buttons from any screen, wireless file uploads over Wi-Fi, in-app Bluetooth pairing, and themes you can drop in or design with the web theme editor.

JJ Launcher on Y1 · JJ Launcher on Y2 · Why JJ Launcher?

Inniclassic

An iPod Classic tribute built on JJ Launcher. The classic menu look, a click-wheel style Music Quiz, synced lyrics, Last.fm scrobbling, plus podcasts, FM radio, and video. Y1 and Y2 community builds.

Inniclassic on Y1 · Inniclassic on Y2 · Why Inniclassic?

Y2Player

A music-first home screen for the Y2. Offline playback with a persistent queue, gapless and crossfade transitions, audiobooks that resume where you stopped, and every common format from FLAC to Opus. Bluetooth audio works, and no internet connection is required.

Y2Player on Y2 · Why Y2Player?

Koensayr

Not a new interface. It is a patcher that improves the stock Y1 software: Artist to Album navigation in the built-in music app, proper track metadata and controls over Bluetooth for car stereos, root access, and a debloated system. A good fit if you want to keep the original software and fix how it behaves.

Koensayr on Y1 · Why Koensayr?

These projects move fast. Read each project's release notes and issues tracker before installing. If anything goes wrong, restoring Original Software for your model is the safe way back.

A note on the two tools

Updater or Updater CE?

Innioasis Updater CE is the original community-made, donation-supported utility. It loads official firmware and current custom releases directly from the Community Firmware Archive, making it the easiest route for Rockbox, JJ Launcher, Inniclassic, Solar, and similar projects.

Innioasis Updater is the company-provided manual flasher for official .zip or .rar packages. Use CE when you want the app to show the latest community releases instead of searching for files and tutorials.

Community videos such as Catsteal3r's TikTok and Dammit Jeff's “How To Actually Quit Spotify” walkthrough demonstrate the CE workflow.
