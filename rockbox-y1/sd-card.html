Rockbox-Y1 and the MicroSD card

Rockbox-Y1 stores your settings, themes, playlists, and music database on the MicroSD card inside a hidden folder named <code>.rockbox</code>.

Get it in Updater CEFull written tutorial

How the .rockbox folder works

Because the firmware itself lives on the player's internal storage and your settings live in .rockbox on the MicroSD card, renaming or deleting .rockbox safely resets all settings and themes to defaults without uninstalling Rockbox-Y1.

  1. Connect the player over USB or put the MicroSD card into your computer.
  2. Show hidden files (macOS: Cmd+Shift+.; Windows: View → Hidden items).
  3. Rename .rockbox to .rockbox-backup to reset your configuration.
  4. Restart the player so it creates a fresh .rockbox folder.

MicroSD card tips

  • Organize music in normal folders; Rockbox can browse both by folder and by ID3/vorbis tags.
  • Refresh the database after copying new albums.
  • Use FAT32 (or exFAT where supported) for reliable card reading.

Frequently asked questions

Will deleting .rockbox erase my music?

No, as long as your music folders are stored outside the .rockbox folder on the card.

Why can't I see the .rockbox folder on my computer?

Folders starting with a dot are hidden by default on Windows, macOS, and Linux. Enable hidden files in your file manager.
