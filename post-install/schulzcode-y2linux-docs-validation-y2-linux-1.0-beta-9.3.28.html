Y2Linux 1.0 Beta 9.3.28: a fast Audiobooks list, films that keep their picture

Release 1.0.0-beta.9.3.28, Y2Reborn 1.0.0-beta.9.3.28, kernel 6.18.56-y2linux-1.0.0-beta.9.3.28, update sequence 16510, root 2026.08-platform-v1.279. Published as GitHub release baseline-256 (build 256; build 255 was an unpublished first build of this version). Previous release: Beta 9.3.27.

This report is written for people who use the player. A short technical appendix is at the end.

In one paragraph

Three fixes. Audiobooks with a few hundred books made of many small files was so slow that reaching a letter took minutes (Y2Reborn #27); the list is now prepared once and scrolls fast. A film's picture could freeze after seeking back while it was still catching up from the seek before (Y2Reborn #129). And, found while testing that: **films with few reference pictures (as phones and screen recorders often make them) stopped after their first pictures** on the video chip.

What changed

  • Audiobooks. The list of books (and its Cover Flow) used to be worked out again several times for every picture

    drawn and for every turn of the wheel. It is now kept until the library, a book's progress or the playing book changes; only the playing book's "Chapter … · …%" line follows the sound. A fast turn of the wheel moves several books at a time, as in Songs.

  • Films after seeking. After a seek, the picture decodes forward from the nearest key picture until it reaches the

    sound. A second seek during that catch-up (above all back) was ignored, and the picture stood still until the sound reached it again. Every seek is now followed.

  • Films on the video chip. The chip's picture memory was sized from the film's reference pictures alone, but the

    decoder works on several pictures at once. A film with one reference picture got four buffers, ran out after three pictures and showed a still picture while the sound played. The memory now counts the pictures in flight.

How it was tested

  • Tests: the platform's full check, Reborn's tests and the tests of the release images passed. New tests: the

    Audiobooks list is kept between frames while the playing book's place still moves, a fast turn moves several books; the rule that follows a seek during a catch-up.

  • On the computer: a list of 200 books made of 10 000 files took 1.5 ms per redraw and 2.8 ms per wheel step

    before, 0.02 ms for both after (the player is roughly ten to twenty times slower).

  • On one player (builds 255 and 256, installed over the air): 200 test books of 50 files each (10 337 tracks):

    Audiobooks opened in Cover Flow with 205 books, 40 wheel steps at 14 ms per frame on average. A 3-minute test film with one key picture per minute and a clock in the picture: on build 255 it stood still at its third picture ("all 4 frames are in use"), which led to the third fix; on build 256 it played, a seek to 1:50 followed half a second later by a seek back to 0:20 showed 0:22 two seconds later, and a seek to 2:30 showed 2:35 five seconds later. The 1080p test film still plays on the chip. The test files were removed again.

  • Not tested: the reporters' own libraries and films.

Updating

Settings → System → Software Update → Update Now. Your music, playlists, settings, Wi-Fi networks and Bluetooth pairings stay. Restore Previous System (Settings → System → About, tap Version seven times, then Diagnostics) goes back to the version you had.

Known issues

Everything listed for Beta 9.3.27 still applies.

Technical appendix

  • Reborn crates/reborn-ui/src/views_books.rs (with_book_rows: rows cached by library stamp, shelf, chapter count

    and playing book; the playing row's status refreshed on each read; with_books no longer clones every book to find one), rows.rs (row count, row and window read from the cache), actions.rs (Audiobooks accelerates like the library and the time zones).

  • Reborn app/reborn/src/video.rs: Shared::moves counts the sound's moves; follow_move seeks again during a

    catch-up when the sound moved since that seek (no loop on a demuxer that lands after the target).

  • buildroot/patches/ffmpeg/0001-y2-vdec-h264-hwaccel.patch: ff_y2vdec_open adds thread_count frames under frame

    threading, as FFmpeg's own hardware frame pools do (driver maximum 24 unchanged).

  • Images built from Linux 744c913e, Reborn dbe6c29.
