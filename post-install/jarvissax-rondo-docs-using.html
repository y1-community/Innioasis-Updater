Using Rondo

Rondo is built around the wheel and five buttons. The same few moves work everywhere, and a few screens give them an extra meaning.

The basics

ControlWhat it does
WheelMoves through lists. On Now Playing, a video or the radio, it changes the volume.
CenterOpens or selects.
Hold centerOptions for the highlighted item or the current screen.
Menu (top)Back.
Play/Pause (bottom)Plays and pauses, from any screen.
Next / PreviousNext or previous song, from any screen.
Hold Next / PreviousFast-forward or rewind.
PowerSwitches the screen off and on. Hold it to shut down or restart.

When the screen is off, any press (a button, center or the wheel) only wakes it: nothing else happens, so a press in your pocket never skips a song. Within 5 seconds of the screen dimming or going dark by itself, a press wakes it and does what it does (after Power, a press still only wakes it). A wired headset's buttons (play/pause, next, previous, volume) work with the screen on or off, like Bluetooth headphones' buttons.

Main menu

Now Playing, Library, YouTube, YouTube Music, FM Radio and Settings. The right side shows what's highlighted, or what's playing. Settings › Display › Theme › Main menu changes the order and which entries show up.

Library: music, videos, photos and files

Rondo looks through the whole microSD card (and the Y1's internal storage) for music, videos and photos. You don't need a special folder layout. Songs are listed by title, artist and album, using the files' tags. Without tags, a file named Artist - Title.flac is read the obvious way.

Library has four parts: Music, Videos, Photos and Files.

Music starts with Shuffle songs and Search, then Browse (Songs, Artists, Album artists, Albums, Cover Flow, Genres), Playlists, Listening (Recently added, Most played, Recently played, Top rated) and The card (Rescan, Organize). Every list of songs starts with Shuffle.

  • Search finds songs, artists, albums, videos and playlists as you type, on the wheel

    keyboard. Songs, Artists, Albums, Videos and Playlists each start with a Search of their own.

  • Album artists and Genres group songs by their album artist (else their artist) and their

    genre tag. Albums show the album artist and the year; two albums of one name by different artists stay apart, and an album's songs play in disc and track order.

  • Cover Flow shows the albums' covers on a wide stage, the one in front facing you and the

    others turned and set back, each above its reflection: turn the wheel to glide through them (Next and Prev jump ten), press center for the album's songs.

  • Recently added lists the newest songs first.
  • Most played, Recently played and Top rated (4 stars and up): a song counts as played

    once it's half over or 4 minutes in.

  • Playlists: New playlist, then Add songs (or hold center on any song and choose

    Add to playlist). Hold center on a song in a playlist to move it or take it out, and on a playlist to rename or delete it. Playlists are .m3u8 files in the card's Playlists folder, so music players on a computer open them too. Smart playlists, made on the dashboard from rules (a genre, a year, what you play and rate), are listed below them and pick their songs anew each time you open one.

  • Hold center on a song to play it now, play it next, add it to the queue or a playlist, or

    delete it from the card.

  • Videos lists all the videos on the card, the newest first, and each folder that has some.

    YouTube videos you downloaded are there too, in Videos/YouTube.

  • Photos shows the JPEG and PNG pictures on the card and the internal storage, by folder (and

    all of them, the newest first), as a grid: turn the wheel to pick one, press center to see it full screen. There the wheel (or Next and Prev) slides from one to the next, center shows its name and date, and Play starts or stops a slideshow. Pictures from a camera show the right way up.

  • Files is a file manager for the card and the internal storage: every folder and file, not

    only music. Center opens a folder, plays a song (the folder's songs after it, an album's in disc and track order), a video or a photo. Hold center on a file or folder to Rename it, Copy or Move it to another folder (on either storage: between the two it copies in the background, and the list says how far it is), or Delete it. Each folder starts with New folder. Songs keep their plays, ratings and playlists when they move.

  • Rescan the card after copying files to it another way than the USB drive.
  • Organize the card puts every song in Music/Artist/Album (Year)/01 - Title (a Disc 1

    folder for each disc of a multi-disc album), episodes in Shows/Series (Year)/Season 01/ and other videos in Movies/Title (Year)/, the way Jellyfin, Plex and Kodi expect, from the tags. It shows how many files would move and where (See the moves) and moves nothing until you choose Organize. Lyrics (.lrc), subtitles and a one-album folder's cover picture go along, and so do your playlists, plays, ratings and the queue.

  • Formats: every common one for music: MP3, AAC and M4A/M4B (with ALAC), FLAC, Ogg Vorbis,

    Opus, WAV, AIFF, WMA (and WMA Lossless), APE, WavPack, AC-3 and E-AC-3, DTS, DSD (DSF and DFF), TTA, TAK, Musepack, MKA, CAF, AMR, MP2 and AU; surround is mixed down to stereo. MP4 (H.264) and similar for video, with .srt, .vtt and .ass subtitles beside it. Covers come from the file's embedded artwork. Lyrics come from the file's tags or from an .lrc file with the same name next to it, and timed lyrics scroll along. Songs on the card never fetch anything from the internet.

Adding music

Plug the Y1 into a computer and choose Use as USB drive: the microSD card shows up on the computer like a memory stick. Copy files onto it, eject it on the computer, then choose **Turn off USB drive** on the Y1. While the card is on the computer, music from it is paused. Unplugging the cable gives the card back to the Y1 as well. The dashboard can also upload music over Wi-Fi.

YouTube Music and YouTube

Both work as soon as the Y1 is on Wi-Fi: Rondo talks to YouTube itself, with nothing to set up. (If you'd rather it went through a server of your own, choose My server in Settings › YouTube › Connection.) They open on a home page, with tabs across the top: turn the wheel on the tab row and press center to switch. Search uses an on-screen keyboard you type on with the wheel.

  • Signed in, you also get Library (your playlists and Liked Music) in YouTube Music, and

    Subscriptions and History in YouTube, whose home is then picked for you. Sign in with a code in Settings › YouTube › YouTube account (see Settings); with a server, the server's own account counts. YouTube Music's own home and history also need a cookies.txt (on the dashboard's YouTube page, or the server's admin page).

  • Hold center on a song to play it now or next, add it to the queue, or Start radio

    (songs like this one).

  • Songs stream as YouTube's Opus, or its AAC where a song has no Opus; Now Playing says which

    ("Streaming · Opus").

  • Videos play at the quality set in Settings › YouTube › Video quality (Auto picks up to

    360p, the screen's size; up to 720p works). A video without that height plays the next one down.

  • Hold center on a video to download it to the card (720p, 480p or 360p) with its subtitles, to

    watch with no connection. YouTube › Home › Downloads lists them (also with no connection): center plays one, hold center deletes it, and one on its way can be canceled there. They're saved in Videos/YouTube on the card, so Library › Videos has them too.

Shorts

YouTube's home page starts with Shorts: one short at a time, looping, picked for your account when you're signed in. Next / Previous move to the next or previous short, the wheel is the volume and center pauses. Moving on slides like on a phone: the short slides up and away as the next one comes in from below. The next few shorts load while you watch, so moving on takes about a second.

Now Playing

  • Wheel: volume. Center: switch between the cover and the lyrics.
  • Next / Previous: the next or the song before; the cover slides in from that side.
  • Hold center for options: scrub through the song, see what's up next, shuffle and repeat,

    and for a song on the card its rating: center gives it one star more (after five, none).

  • Hold Next / Previous to scrub: the wheel then moves the position, and center jumps there.

    Menu cancels.

Watching a video

  • Wheel: volume. Center: scrub. Next / Previous: 10 seconds forward or back.
  • Hold center for the options: Quality (YouTube), Subtitles (Off, or one of the video's

    tracks) and Download (YouTube: to the card at the height picked). A YouTube video's tracks are its own and the speech recognition's; a video on the card has the subtitle files beside it (Movie.srt, Movie.en.srt, Movie.en.forced.ass…). The language picked last is shown first in the next video.

FM radio

Plug in wired headphones first: their cable is the antenna.

  • Wheel: volume. Center: switch the wheel to tuning, in 0.1 MHz steps, and back.
  • Next / Previous: find the next station up or down.
  • Play/Pause: mute.
  • Hold center for options: your saved stations, Scan (finds every station, about 20

    seconds), save or remove this one, play through the speaker, and the region. "Most of the world" is 87.5–108 MHz; Japan is 76–95 MHz. In the stations list, hold center on a saved station to rename, move or remove it, or on a found one to save it.

Station names come from RDS where the station sends it. Starting YouTube or a song from the card turns the radio off.

Settings

Settings has the Dashboard at the top, then six groups: Connections (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth), YouTube (the connection, the account, video quality), Sound, Display (theme, brightness, screen off, subtitles, lyrics), Controls (the wheel) and System (date and time, updates, About, shut down).

Sound

Settings › Sound shows the output music plays on now (Playing on: the speaker, wired headphones or a Bluetooth device). Each output keeps its own sound:

  • Equalizer: a preset, your own bands (Custom), or a preset you saved.
  • Edit equalizer: a slider for each of the ten bands, ±12 dB. The wheel sets the gain, Next

    and Previous pick the band, center saves the bands as a preset of your own (for every output), and holding center sets them flat.

  • Headphone match (headphones only): a correction for your headphone model from

    AutoEq's measurements. Search by model; this one needs a server, which looks the model up.

  • Loudness boost: up to 12 dB louder, with the peaks held so they never clip.

Under Every output:

  • Volume leveling: songs at one loudness, measured as they play and while the Y1 charges.
  • Amplifier: the Y1's own amplifier, Standard as it ships, or +2, +4 or +6 dB louder for

    headphones and the speaker; the loudest steps add some hiss.

  • Volume limit: the loudest step the wheel goes to, on every output (No limit as it ships).

The dashboard

Settings › Dashboard turns the Y1 into a small web server for as long as that screen is open. It shows an address, a QR code of it and a 6-digit code. Open the address in a browser on the same Wi-Fi and type the code. From there you can:

  • see what's playing and control it, and shut down, restart or update Rondo,
  • browse, search, upload and delete music, videos and photos, manage the card's files and

    folders, organize the card, and make playlists and smart playlists,

  • change the theme, the sound, the menu and every setting above,
  • manage Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and the FM radio, sign in to YouTube, download YouTube videos, and

    connect a server.

Leaving the Dashboard screen on the Y1 (Menu) stops it. See The dashboard for each page.
