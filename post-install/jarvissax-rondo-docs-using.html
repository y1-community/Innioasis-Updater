Instructions published by the project, shown here word for word.

Using Rondo

Rondo is built around the wheel and five buttons. The same few moves work everywhere, and a few screens give them an extra meaning.

The basics

ControlWhat it does
WheelMoves through lists. On Now Playing, a video or the radio, it changes the volume.
CenterOpens or selects.
Hold centerOptions for the highlighted item or the current screen.
Menu (top)Back.
Play/Pause (bottom)Plays and pauses, from any screen.
Next / PreviousNext or previous song, from any screen.
Hold Next / PreviousFast-forward or rewind.
Hold center on the main menuTurns the Y1 off: keep holding for 2 seconds, until the bar fills. Let go sooner and nothing happens.

When the screen is off, any press (a button, center or the wheel) only wakes it: nothing else happens, so a press in your pocket never skips a song. Within 5 seconds of the screen dimming or going dark by itself, a press wakes it and does what it does. A wired headset's buttons (play/pause, next, previous, volume) work with the screen on or off, like Bluetooth headphones' buttons.

The keyboard

Wherever you type (a search, a message, a playlist's name), the keyboard works like a TV remote: the wheel walks the keys, Next and Previous move right and left, Play moves down, Menu moves up (on the top row it closes the keyboard) and center types. ABC and #+= switch to capitals and symbols. It takes the look of where it opens: YouTube's and YouTube Music's round keys, WhatsApp's greens, Rondo's own everywhere else.

The 😊 key (on every keyboard but a Wi-Fi password's and the server's address, code and token) opens the emoji: every emoji your Y1's font draws, in ten tabs along the top, Recent (your last 30) and then smileys, people, animals, food, travel, activities, objects, symbols and flags.

  • The wheel goes through the whole list, a fast spin a row at a time; hold Next or Previous to

    jump to the next or previous tab. Menu on the top row goes up to the tabs, where the wheel picks a tab; Menu there goes back to the letters.

  • The line under the keyboard names the emoji you're on.
  • Tone sets the skin tone for every emoji that has one (center: the next tone).
  • 🔍 finds an emoji by its name: type part of it (heart, cat, flag, fruit) and what matches is

    the row on top; Menu goes up to it.

  • Del takes off a whole emoji.

Main menu

Now Playing, Library, YouTube, YouTube Music, WhatsApp, FM Radio and Settings. The right side shows what's highlighted, or what's playing. Settings › Display › Theme › Main menu changes the order and which entries show up.

Library: music, videos, audiobooks, photos and files

Rondo looks through the whole microSD card (and the Y1's internal storage) for music, videos and photos. You don't need a special folder layout. Songs are listed by title, artist and album, using the files' tags. Without tags, a file named Artist - Title.flac is read the obvious way.

Library has five parts: Music, Videos, Audiobooks (see Audiobooks), Photos and Files.

Music starts with Shuffle songs and Search, then Browse (Songs, Artists, Album artists, Albums, Cover Flow, Genres), Playlists, Listening (Recently added, Most played, Recently played, Top rated) and The card (Rescan, Organize). Every list of songs starts with Shuffle.

  • Search finds songs, artists, albums, videos and playlists as you type, on the wheel

    keyboard. Songs, Artists, Albums, Videos and Playlists each start with a Search of their own.

  • Album artists and Genres group songs by their album artist (else their artist) and their

    genre tag. Albums show the album artist and the year; two albums of one name by different artists stay apart, and an album's songs play in disc and track order.

  • Cover Flow shows the albums' covers on a wide stage, the one in front facing you and the

    others turned and set back, each above its reflection: turn the wheel to glide through them (Next and Prev jump ten), press center for the album's songs.

  • Recently added lists the newest songs first.
  • Most played, Recently played and Top rated (4 stars and up): a song counts as played

    once it's half over or 4 minutes in.

  • Playlists: New playlist, then Add songs (or hold center on any song and choose

    Add to playlist). Hold center on a song in a playlist to move it or take it out, and on a playlist to rename or delete it. Playlists are .m3u8 files in the card's Playlists folder, so music players on a computer open them too. Downloaded YouTube Music playlists are with them (in Playlists/YouTube Music), marked YouTube Music and with their cover. A picture beside a playlist with its name (Road trip.jpg beside Road trip.m3u8) is its artwork; Photos leaves the Playlists folder out. Smart playlists, made on the dashboard from rules (a genre, a year, what you play and rate), are listed below them and pick their songs anew each time you open one.

  • Hold center on a song to play it now, play it next, add it to the queue or a playlist, or

    delete it from the card.

  • Videos lists all the videos on the card, the newest first, and each folder that has some.

    YouTube videos you downloaded are there too, in Videos/YouTube.

  • Photos shows the JPEG and PNG pictures on the card and the internal storage, by folder (and

    all of them, the newest first), as a grid: turn the wheel to pick one, press center to see it full screen. There the wheel (or Next and Prev) slides from one to the next, center shows its name and date, and Play starts or stops a slideshow. Pictures from a camera show the right way up.

  • Files is a file manager for the card and the internal storage: every folder and file, not

    only music. Center opens a folder, plays a song (the folder's songs after it, an album's in disc and track order), a video or a photo. Hold center on a file or folder to Rename it, Copy or Move it to another folder (on either storage: between the two it copies in the background, and the list says how far it is), or Delete it. Each folder starts with New folder. Songs keep their plays, ratings and playlists when they move.

  • Rescan the card after copying files to it another way than the USB drive.
  • Organize the card puts every song in Music/Artist/Album (Year)/01 - Title (a Disc 1

    folder for each disc of a multi-disc album), episodes in Videos/Shows/Series (Years)/Season 01/ and other videos in Videos/Movies/Title (Year)/, the way Jellyfin, Plex and Kodi expect, from the tags. A series' years come from its folder's name (Silo (2023-)); the dashboard's Organize looks up the ones the names don't say. It shows how many files would move and where (See the moves) and moves nothing until you choose Organize. Lyrics (.lrc), subtitles and a one-album folder's cover picture go along, and so do your playlists, plays, ratings and the queue.

  • Formats: every common one for music: MP3, AAC and M4A/M4B (with ALAC), FLAC, Ogg Vorbis,

    Opus, WAV, AIFF, WMA (and WMA Lossless), APE, WavPack, AC-3 and E-AC-3, DTS, DSD (DSF and DFF), TTA, TAK, Musepack, MKA, CAF, AMR, MP2 and AU; surround is mixed down to stereo. MP4 (H.264) and similar for video, with .srt, .vtt and .ass subtitles beside it. Covers come from the file's embedded artwork. Lyrics come from the file's tags or from an .lrc file with the same name next to it, and timed lyrics scroll along. Songs on the card never fetch anything from the internet.

Adding music

Plug the Y1 into a computer and choose Use as USB drive: the microSD card shows up on the computer like a memory stick. Copy files onto it, eject it on the computer, then choose **Turn off USB drive** on the Y1. While the card is on the computer, music from it is paused. Unplugging the cable gives the card back to the Y1 as well. The dashboard can also upload music over Wi-Fi.

Audiobooks

Library › Audiobooks has the books on the card, with Continue listening (the books you've started, the last one first) above all books. Each shows its cover, author and time left (or Finished); a dot marks a book you haven't started. Center opens a book; hold center to go on with it at once.

What counts as a book:

  • everything in an Audiobooks folder at the top of the card or the internal storage, in any

    letter case. The stock firmware's folder is Audiobooks too, so books from it carry over;

  • any .m4b file, wherever it is;
  • files whose genre tag is Audiobook.

A folder of files is one book, in disc, track and name order; each file is a chapter. Disc folders (CD 1, Disc 2) inside a book's folder are part of it, and a cover.jpg (or folder.jpg) in the book's folder is its cover. An .m4b, or any file with chapters of its own, is a book by itself. Books stay out of Songs, Shuffle songs, Artists, Albums, Genres, the smart lists and Organize. Library's Search finds them by title and author. Books from Audible or Apple Books that are copy-protected (.aa, .aax) can't play; ones you converted to .m4b can.

A book's screen shows its cover, author, narrator and length, then Resume (or Start, or Start again once it's finished), Chapters, Bookmarks, Speed and **Mark as finished**.

While a book plays:

  • It remembers where you stopped, always: every 10 seconds and whenever you pause, the screen

    goes off or the Y1 turns off. After a pause it starts a little earlier, so you don't lose the thread: about 2 seconds after a short pause, 5 after ten minutes, 10 after an hour, 30 after a day.

  • Next and Previous move by chapter. Previous in a chapter's first 3 seconds goes to the

    chapter before, otherwise to the start of this one. A book without chapters skips 30 seconds forward and 15 back. Hold Next or Previous to scrub through the chapter.

  • Now Playing shows the book, its author, the chapter with its own time left, the time left

    in the book, and a bar for the chapter. Center opens the chapters. Times are listening time at the book's speed.

  • Hold center for the options: scrub, Chapters, Add bookmark, Bookmarks, Speed,

    the Sleep timer and Back to your music.

  • Speed: 0.75× to 3×, with the voice's pitch kept. Each book keeps its own; a badge on Now

    Playing shows it.

  • Bookmarks keep a place in a book. Center on one goes there; hold center to delete it.
  • Your music waits. A book plays as its own queue: the music you were playing comes back where

    it was when the book ends (paused) or when you choose Back to your music. Play next and Add to queue on a song add it to that music. Shuffle and repeat don't apply to books.

  • A book is finished in its last 30 seconds. Mark it finished or not on its screen.

To get books onto the card, copy them into Audiobooks (as a USB drive, see Adding music), or upload them on the dashboard.

YouTube Music and YouTube

Both work as soon as the Y1 is on Wi-Fi: Rondo talks to YouTube itself, with nothing to set up. (If you'd rather it went through a server of your own, connect the server in Settings › Server, then choose Through my server in Settings › YouTube › Connect to.) They open on a home page, with tabs across the top: turn the wheel on the tab row and press center to switch. YouTube Music's home is shelves, as in the app: each section a row of covers that slides along as you turn the wheel, then on into the next. Search uses an on-screen keyboard you type on with the wheel.

  • Signed in, you also get Library (your playlists and Liked Music) in YouTube Music, and

    Subscriptions and History in YouTube, whose home is then picked for you. Sign in with a code in Settings › YouTube › YouTube account (see Settings); through a server, the server's own account counts. YouTube Music's own home and history also need a cookies.txt (on the dashboard's YouTube page, or the server's admin page).

  • Hold center on a song to play it now or next, add it to the queue, Start radio (songs

    like this one) or Download it. Now Playing's options (hold center) have Download too, for the song playing. An album has Download all at its top.

  • A playlist has Download playlist at its top: all its songs (a mix, which never ends:

    the ones it shows when you open it), and a playlist of them in Library › Playlists, in the order YouTube has, under its name and marked YouTube Music, with its cover (YouTube's picture of it). It's a file in the card's Playlists/YouTube Music folder, its cover a .jpg beside it, and has the songs on the card so far, so it plays from the first one. A song you have already is the same file, whatever playlists it's in. **Update download** (there, or holding center on it in Library) gets its new songs and takes YouTube's order; a song taken off it on YouTube leaves the playlist but stays on the card. Library plays, shuffles and deletes it, and doesn't edit it: deleting it asks whether its songs go too (not those another downloaded playlist has, or that you downloaded by themselves).

  • Downloaded songs are saved to the card the way the library keeps music, in

    Music/YouTube Music/Artist/Album (Year)/01 - Title.m4a: YouTube's own AAC (nothing re-encoded), with the song's artist, album, year, track number, cover and lyrics (timed where LRCLIB has them) in its tags. YouTube Music › Home › Downloads lists the downloaded playlists, the songs on their way and those on the card, also with no connection: center plays a song from the card, hold center deletes one (from the playlists it's in too). Library › Music has them too, and Organize leaves the folder as it is.

  • Pause and resume: Pause downloads at the top of Downloads pauses them all (videos and

    songs: they're one queue, one at a time), and center on one pauses, resumes or cancels it. A paused one keeps what it has and goes on from there, after a restart too: the queue is kept on the Y1, and what came is saved on the card as it comes. They wait by themselves, and go on when they can, while Wi-Fi is off, while the card is the computer's (USB drive) and when the card is full; the rows say why.

  • Songs stream as YouTube's Opus, or its AAC where a song has no Opus; Now Playing says which

    ("Streaming · Opus").

  • Videos play at the quality set in Settings › YouTube › Video quality (Auto picks up to

    360p, the screen's size; up to 720p works). A video without that height plays the next one down.

  • Hold center on a video to download it to the card (720p, 480p or 360p) with its subtitles, to

    watch with no connection. YouTube › Home › Downloads lists them (also with no connection): center plays one, hold center deletes it, and one on its way can be paused, resumed or canceled there. They're saved in Videos/YouTube on the card, so Library › Videos has them too.

Shorts

YouTube's home page starts with Shorts: one short at a time, looping, picked for your account when you're signed in. Next / Previous move to the next or previous short, the wheel is the volume and center pauses. Moving on slides like on a phone: the short slides up and away as the next one comes in from below. The next few shorts load while you watch, so moving on takes about a second.

WhatsApp

WhatsApp works through your own Rondo server (3.3.0 or newer; reacting needs 3.4.0): the server becomes one of your WhatsApp's linked devices, like WhatsApp Web on a computer, and the Y1 reads and sends through it. Connect the Y1 to the server in Settings › Server, then link WhatsApp once on the server's admin page: WhatsApp › Get a code, and type the code on your phone under WhatsApp's Settings › Linked devices › Link a device › Link with phone number instead (more). Your recent chats come over in a minute or two.

  • Chats: the newest first (pinned ones on top), each with its picture, its last message (a mic

    and the length for a voice message, an icon for a photo or a video), the time, ticks on yours and the unread count. Hold center on a chat for its profile: the picture, the name, the number and the about. At the top: - Search: type a name (any case, with or without accents) or a number (three digits or more, wherever they are in it) and press Search for the chats and contacts that match; Menu goes back to the keyboard. It doesn't search messages. - New chat lists your contacts (Next and Previous jump a letter), with a Search of its own at the top for contacts only.

  • A chat shows its messages as bubbles, the newest at the bottom, with the day between them;

    in a group, each one's sender. Replies show what they answer, and reactions sit under the bubble. Turn the wheel to go through the messages; up past the first, earlier ones load. Under them are three buttons: Message, Voice and Photo.

  • Center on a message opens what it holds: a photo full screen, a video in the player, a voice

    message played (the music pauses and comes back after; center again stops it). On a text, or holding center on any message: the reactions on top, then Reply, open or play it, the sender's profile (in a group) and the chat's profile. A message that couldn't be sent says so: center sends it again.

  • Reacting: the top row is WhatsApp's 👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 🙏 and +. The wheel goes along it and

    then down the menu (Play jumps between them); center reacts, in place of your earlier one. Yours has a gray circle, and center on it takes it back. + slides up every emoji, as the keyboard has them (Recent first, the tabs, Tone, Find): center on one reacts with it, Menu on the tabs closes it. Once you've reacted, Take back is there too.

  • Message opens the keyboard (see The keyboard), in WhatsApp's colors. What you

    typed and didn't send is still there next time. Send (the paper plane) sends it; from a message's Reply, it goes as a reply.

  • Voice records with the Y1's built-in microphone at once (what's playing pauses): center

    sends it, Menu throws it away. Up to 15 minutes.

  • Photo lists the photos and videos on the card, the newest first: center shows the one you

    picked, and center again sends it.

Your messages' ticks are as on your phone: a clock while it goes, one tick sent, two delivered, two blue read (or played).

  • No notifications. The Y1 doesn't say a message came: it's there when you open WhatsApp. The

    server never shows you as online, so your phone keeps its own notifications.

  • Opening a chat marks it read, as on your phone (blue ticks, if you have them on).
  • On the card: the Y1 keeps your recent chats, and the photos, videos and voice messages you

    opened, in a hidden folder (.rondo/whatsapp), up to 1 GB, the oldest going first. They open at once, also without a connection.

  • Not yet: opening documents (open them on your phone), calls and status updates.
  • WhatsApp's terms don't allow unofficial apps like this one, and WhatsApp may close accounts

    that use them. You can unlink any time, on the server's admin page or on your phone under Linked devices.

Now Playing

  • Wheel: volume. Center: switch between the cover and the lyrics.
  • Next / Previous: the next or the song before; the cover slides in from that side.
  • Hold center for options: scrub through the song, see what's up next, shuffle and repeat,

    for a song on the card its rating (center gives it one star more; after five, none), and the Sleep timer.

  • Sleep timer: 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or the end of the song (of the chapter, for an

    audiobook). The sound fades out over the last 10 seconds, then pauses. A moon on Now Playing shows it's running and how long is left.

  • Hold Next / Previous to scrub: the wheel then moves the position, and center jumps there.

    Menu cancels.

Watching a video

  • Wheel: volume. Center: scrub. Next / Previous: 10 seconds forward or back.
  • Hold center for the options: Quality (YouTube), Subtitles (Off, or one of the video's

    tracks) and Download (YouTube: to the card at the height picked). A YouTube video's tracks are its own and the speech recognition's; a video on the card has the subtitle files beside it (Movie.srt, Movie.en.srt, Movie.en.forced.ass…). The language picked last is shown first in the next video.

FM radio

Plug in wired headphones first: their cable is the antenna.

  • Wheel: volume. Center: switch the wheel to tuning, in 0.1 MHz steps, and back.
  • Next / Previous: find the next station up or down.
  • Play/Pause: mute.
  • Hold center for options: your saved stations, Scan (finds every station, about 20

    seconds), save or remove this one, play through the speaker (the headphones play too: the Y1's speaker is fed from their amplifier, and they stay in as the antenna), and the region. "Most of the world" is 87.5–108 MHz; Japan is 76–95 MHz. In the stations list, hold center on a saved station to rename, move or remove it, or on a found one to save it.

Station names come from RDS where the station sends it. Starting YouTube or a song from the card turns the radio off.

Sound

Settings › Sound shows the output music plays on now (Playing on: the speaker, wired headphones or a Bluetooth device). Each output keeps its own sound:

  • Equalizer: a preset, your own bands (Custom), or a preset you saved.
  • Edit equalizer: a slider for each of the ten bands, ±12 dB. The wheel sets the gain, Next

    and Previous pick the band, center saves the bands as a preset of your own (for every output), and holding center sets them flat.

  • Headphone match (headphones only): a correction for your headphone model from

    AutoEq's measurements. Search by model; this one needs a server, which looks the model up.

  • Loudness boost: up to 12 dB louder, with the peaks held so they never clip.

Under Every output:

  • Volume leveling: songs at one loudness, measured as they play and while the Y1 charges.
  • Amplifier: the Y1's own amplifier, Standard as it ships, or +2, +4 or +6 dB louder for

    headphones and the speaker; the loudest steps add some hiss.

  • Volume limit: the loudest step the wheel goes to, on every output (No limit as it ships).

Apple's emoji, from your own Mac

Rondo draws emoji with Google's Noto Color Emoji. With a Mac you can give your Y1 Apple's instead, converted from your Mac's own copy of the font. Apple's emoji are Apple's, so the font isn't part of Rondo, this repository or its releases: each Mac makes its own.

  1. On the Mac, in a copy of this repository: node tools/emoji/make-font.cjs. It writes

    emoji.ttf (about 13 MB, only the one size the Y1 uses) to the folder you're in.

  2. With the Y1 connected over USB: adb push emoji.ttf /data/rondo/emoji.ttf, then restart Rondo

    (Settings › System › Shut down › Restart).

Rondo takes emoji.ttf in its data folder over its own emoji, draws them at the same size and in the same place, and keeps it through updates. To go back: adb shell rm /data/rondo/emoji.ttf and restart. About a hundred emoji of people facing right don't draw with it, and the newest emoji only come with a newer macOS: the keyboard offers only the emoji the font draws.

The dashboard

Settings › Dashboard turns the Y1 into a small web server for as long as that screen is open. It shows an address, a QR code of it and a 6-digit code. Open the address in a browser on the same Wi-Fi and type the code. From there you can:

  • see what's playing and control it, and shut down, restart or update Rondo,
  • browse, search, upload and delete music, videos and photos, manage the card's files and

    folders, organize the card, and make playlists and smart playlists,

  • change the theme, the sound, the menu and every setting above,
  • manage Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and the FM radio, sign in to YouTube, download YouTube videos, and

    connect a server (its own Server page).

Leaving the Dashboard screen on the Y1 (Menu) stops it. See The dashboard for each page.
