Why

Owner after 45: "does the update process also have a cleanup, so that we can guarantee there is always enough space for users to update?" Staging 45 had failed update_data_space_reserve because a cancelled package stayed in pending, and nothing removed disposable data (the owner's player held 330 MiB of test logs).

ChangeDetail
Stale packageDeleted before every download and on Cancel Update.
Rollback copiesThe recovery deletes the previous copies right before writing new ones; the space check counts them as free.
CleanupIf the download does not fit, disposable data goes one entry at a time until it does: /data/logs except system.log, recovery-last.log, recovery-display.txt; rotated *.previous.jsonl platform journals; finished qualification runs. First for the manifest (a check works on a nearly full volume), then for the payloads. Never music, settings, library or the rollback copies.
Refusaldownload.json needed_bytes; Reborn: "the update needs N MB of system space. Your music is not affected."
RestartDefault: restart into the recovery and back. Override restart.json {"mode": "poweroff"}. Low battery in the recovery always turns off. Outcome screens say Restarting. Reborn: Restart and Install.

Owner steps

  1. Player on Wi-Fi: Settings → About & Updates → Check for Updates, Download Update, Restart and Install.
  2. The agent checks the install log, the display, Acknowledged and the cleaned-up package afterwards.
