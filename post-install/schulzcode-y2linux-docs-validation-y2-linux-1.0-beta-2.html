Why

A Bluetooth/Wi-Fi review of Beta 1 (2026-10-15) against archive 20261016/155 and the live player (AirPods Pro connected, AAC negotiated at 44.1 kHz) found the faults below; the owner asked to fix all of them and to choose the audio output automatically.

Since Beta 1

ChangeEvidenceFix
BTIF receive lost bytesEvery BTIF timeout on record (4 of 4, incl. 155's morning wake) showed the reply already in the RX ring, unread: RX flag=0 valid=7 / valid=2. The handler read the write pointer, drained, then cleared the (edge-like, write-one-to-clear) RX status, dropping the interrupt of bytes that landed in between. Cost: a 4 s WMT timeout (opcode 07/08 −110) and a full controller restart.btif.c: acknowledge first, then drain until the write pointer stops moving. New host test reproduces the race; it fails on Beta 1's handler.
Reconnect gave up for goodWith the AirPods away, BlueZ answered "Host is down" ~18–19 s after the radio came up (three wakes in 155); y2-bt-reconnect stopped waiting at 10 s, could not tell what happened and inhibited every later reconnect (completion_unknown_user_retry_required, also on the 26 nights).reconnect.c waits 45 s for BlueZ's answer; an absent peer is an ordinary failed attempt. The HCI driver programs the page timeout to 0x2000 slots (5.12 s) after init (Linux stores BlueZ's PageTimeout but never sends it) and logs the value it replaced.
Wi-Fi start slowed by the failing page155 morning wake: 22.4 s from wlan0 to DHCP while the page ran; 7.5 s on 2026-10-15 with the AirPods present. Shared 2.4 GHz radio.Shorter page timeout above; to be measured on the next wake.

IdentityY2Linux and Reborn 1.0.0-beta.2.

Not changed, on purpose: Wi-Fi power save stays off (owner decision; association blocks deep idle either way); the Wi-Fi status loop keeps its wpa_cli/ip probes (0.2–0.7 % CPU measured; they feed the Online/update decision); BlueZ's uinput message stays (headphone buttons reach Reborn through its media player; a second, uinput key path could deliver them twice).

Device results

Published without a device test first (owner: "publish directly, i want to update only over ota in the future"). Owed after the owner's over-the-air install: version/health/firmware check; page timeout … slots line in the kernel log; radio start without BTIF timeouts over many wakes; AirPods away at wake → state Exhausted/Waiting, not Inhibited, and they connect when taken out; output switches to the AirPods on connect; Wi-Fi time to Online after a wake.

Known gaps

Battery in sleep ≈9.4 mA (Beta 1 night, archive 155) vs stock ≈1.3 mA; rare slow display start after a restart; USB after deep-sleep wake not confirmed over many wakes; AAC with AirPods negotiated but not listened to; no headphone-jack detection; full install from stock run on one player only; maintenance self-wake race (wake_event_in_progress) open.
