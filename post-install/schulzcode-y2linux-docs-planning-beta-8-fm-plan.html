Beta 8: FM radio — gap audit evidence and phased plan (2026-10-16)

Status (2026-11-12): brought back onto today's code, hardware NOT_RUN. The October branches beta8-fm were never built into a release or installed. On 2026-11-12 the owner asked for FM again ("bring back the FM Radio"): the kernel part (tuner on STP channel 1, Y2 FM Capture) applies unchanged and compiles; the platform part was written again for the Rust platform (radiofw/fm.rs: the tuner's two files as an optional pair from the owner's system.img, taken with the radio firmware or from a later copy; the sleep helper switches a left-on tuner off); Reborn has the tuner under Radio → FM Radio beside Internet Radio, and **Settings → Customize → Browsing → FM Radio** hides it. What changed against the plan below: FM is no longer hidden by itself where a search found nothing (a switch, and a Hide FM Radio row on that page, by the owner's wish); the tuner interface stays the root-only sysfs group, not V4L2. The owner's player is a first-revision Y2 that receives no FM: it can prove power-up, the patch download, tuning, the signal read-out, the capture path and sleep, not reception. Audit: FM re-entry. The text below is the plan of 2026-10-16 as it was written.

On the owner's player, 2026-11-12 (test builds 249 and 250, first-revision Y2)

  • Firmware: system.img copied into Firmware on Internal Storage; the next two passes took the tuner's two

    files and deleted the copy; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth were not restarted.

  • Tuner: the first power-up worked: chip=6627, VCN28 on (one user), tuning accepted across the band, signal

    −102 to −107 dBm on every frequency tried (the noise floor the owner's Android app measured on this player in August). Connectivity: functions=0xb (Bluetooth, FM, Wi-Fi together), no transport error, no recovery.

  • Sound: arecord -D hw:0,2 at 48 kHz delivers a steady stream (two seconds: 96,000 frames, noise at about

    −43 dBFS, left and right identical).

  • Reborn: Radio shows FM Radio and Internet Radio; the search runs in about seven seconds and saves its result

    (it listed 88.3 and 104.0 MHz, then 104.0 MHz alone: on this player false hits or very weak stations, not judged by ear). Playing failed on 249: the stream decoder did not accept WAV (fixed, Reborn media.c, test build 250). On 250 an FM station plays (position advances, "Mono" on Now Playing, no playback error); pausing stops the capture at once and switches the tuner and VCN28 off after the three-second grace; resuming brings both back.

  • Not checked: reception (needs a later-revision Y2); sleep with FM on; FM over Bluetooth by ear; the hide

    switch on the player (covered by tests only); long play, drain.

2. Gaps (what Y2Linux lacks today)

  1. Kernel transport (kernel/platform/connectivity/): y2_conn_get/put and y2_wmt_function accept only BT/Wi-Fi;

    y2_stp_send/deliver only WMT and BT channels; power_on_once forces VCN28 off ("co-clock") and no path turns it on; recovery does not notify an FM consumer.

  2. Tuner driver: none. Needs the MT6627 packet layer (FM opcodes on STP 1), patch/coeff download, power-up sequence,

    tune/seek/scan, RSSI/CQI, mute/volume, I2S on, later RDS. The register sequence comes from the stock ELF; the donor is the readable reference.

  3. Audio: kernel/audio/mt6582-afe.c is playback-only ("No FM, capture"). Needs AFE I2S-in (slave), I2S ASRC

    32 kHz → 44.1/48 kHz, interconnect and a capture memif, exposed as an ALSA capture PCM; optionally the hardware loop to O00/O01.

  4. Firmware intake: tools/platform/y2_platform/radiofw.py knows five Wi-Fi/BT files; FM needs three more (§3). Players that

    already installed firmware deleted their system.img; older roots never had FM files, so the recovery copy cannot supply them.

  5. Platform API: no FM service, scan cache, activity lease or sleep policy for FM. Connectivity suspend() refuses while any

    function is on, so FM must stop (or sleep must wait) before full suspend.

  6. Reborn: no FM source; internet radio (reborn_platform::radio, screens Radio/RadioStations) is the neighbour.
  7. Antenna/jack: the headphone cable is the aerial, and wired-headphone detection is a known open gap (DTS: "EINT16/jack is a later test").
  8. Reception on the owner's unit is unproven — the dominant risk. Nothing above helps if the RF input is dead.

4. Phased plan

Each phase ends with targeted validation (AGENTS.md); the release candidate gets the full boundary.

Phase 1 — firmware intake (host + platform, no tuner power). radiofw.py accepts the FM files as an optional group with reviewed sizes/hashes: Wi-Fi/BT readiness never depends on them; a system.img dropped on an already provisioned player adds only the FM files. State in /run/y2/radio-firmware.json gains fm: present|missing. Host tests with synthetic images. Check issue #39 (oversized system.img on Y2DATA) on the same path.

Phase 2 — kernel FM function and tuner bring-up (first hardware power-up; separate owner go).

  • Connectivity: Y2_CONN_FM = 1 in get/put; VCN28 in software mode on for FM only (stock hwPowerOn(LDO_VCN28)), off after;

    WMT opcode 6 function 1; STP channel 1 send/deliver to an FM receiver; whole-chip recovery stops FM cleanly.

  • New kernel/platform/connectivity/fm.c: the MT6627 subset needed for receive — packet layer, patch/coeff via

    request_firmware("mediatek/mt6582/mt6627_fm_v1_*.bin"), power-up (pwrup_clock_on, digital init, fine tune), SetFreq with channel parameters, mute/volume, RSSI/PAMD/full CQI, soft-mute tune, hardware seek/scan, mono/stereo, I2S on (master, 32 kHz). No TX, no Android ioctl ABI.

  • Interface: a standard V4L2 radio device (/dev/radio0: frequency, seek, signal/stereo in G_TUNER, RDS reads later)

    plus a root-only debugfs dump of CQI for the lab.

  • Experiment (owner presses buttons; image via the usual owner flow): Wi-Fi off; power FM; read chip/ROM version and patch

    result; tune a station the owner confirms on another receiver; repeated RSSI/CQI with no cable, cable A, cable B; full band sweep at 100 kHz (50 kHz if decision 2 says so) with 1–2 s settle and median of several reads. This is feasibility §9.1/9.2/9.4 done under Linux, where we control every register.

  • Gate: a known station clearly above the floor (several dB, stable) → continue. Otherwise stop: FM stays hidden, and the

    owner decides on the physical checks of feasibility §9.6 (jack-to-MT6627 path, nearby low-power transmitter).

Phase 3 — audio path. AFE: I2S-in slave + ASRC to the output rate, interconnect to a capture memif, ALSA capture PCM "Y2 FM Capture" (S16, stereo, 44.1/48 kHz). Reborn reads it into the existing sink, so volume, the wired path and (decision 5) Bluetooth behave like any other source. Bit layouts from the MT6582 manual and the stock HAL's interconnect numbering above; confirmed read-only against stock register dumps where possible. A zero-CPU hardware loop (I00/I01 → O00/O01, stock "direct") is a later power optimisation, wired only.

Phase 4 — platform API. One FM owner in the platform: power/tune/seek/scan/signal/RDS, firmware state, scan cache on Y2DATA, activity lease while FM plays (sleep with the screen off like music), clean stop before full suspend and on Power sleep, coexistence with Wi-Fi/BT (shared CONSYS; stock has display/CPU desense hooks — replicate only if interference shows).

Phase 5 — Reborn UI. Radio section gains "FM" beside internet radio, shown only after a scan finds a station (owner decision 2026-10-15). FM screen: frequency, signal, stereo (and RDS name/text if decision 3). Wheel tunes, Next/Previous seek, hold saves (decision 4), first-run scan builds the list. A hint that wired headphones are the aerial. Skins get the new screen.

Phase 6 — qualification and Beta 8. FM on/off loops, seek/scan, long play on battery (drain per hour, CPU/temperature), sleep/wake around FM, Wi-Fi/BT on together, STP recovery during FM, firmware-absent path, OTA install; audit row and report.

5. Open owner decisions

  1. Authorisation and gate: agree to phases 1–2 and the phase 2 experiment; accept the reception gate (stop and keep FM hidden if no real station is received).
  2. Band and step: 87.5–108 MHz (stock Y2 config); scan/tune step 100 kHz, or 50 kHz (some German transmitters sit on a 50 kHz raster); wheel step.
  3. RDS (station name, radio text) in Beta 8 or later.
  4. Presets: shared Favorites with internet stations, or a separate FM preset list.
  5. Bluetooth output: allow FM over Bluetooth (capture path makes it possible; a wired cable is still needed as aerial), or wired only like stock.
  6. Sleep while FM plays: keep playing with the screen off (activity lease) and stop on Power sleep — recommended.
  7. Firmware re-intake for already provisioned players: ask the owner to copy system.img again when FM is first opened; keep or compile in the cfg.
  8. Release shape: ship Beta 8 with FM hidden on players that find no station even if the owner's unit cannot receive, or hold FM until one unit is proven.

Recommendations where a choice is technical rather than the owner's: V4L2 radio interface; capture path first, hardware loop later.
