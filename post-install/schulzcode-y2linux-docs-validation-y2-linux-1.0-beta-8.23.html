What changed since Beta 8.22

FindingChangeEvidence
F34 (users and the owner's player): after an update restarted the player, the recovery screen stood at "Installing Update – Checking the update. Step 1 of 4" for hours until Power was held; a forced restart then workedThe recovery feeds the SoC watchdog (31 s) only while the installer runs and its progress file changes; 120 s without progress stops the feeding and the watchdog restarts the player, which resumes or restores the install. The installer's steps go to pstore's message buffer, and the recovery keeps the previous start's trail and console in /data/logs/recovery-previous-boot.txt (reports collect it). The freeze's cause is still unknown; the next one leaves its trailLinux aa0b483, 565b1a6; tests/test_production_handover.py (keeper, and every command the initramfs scripts run exists in the initramfs)
#52 on 8.22: the crystal started, but stopped while skipping quickly (17 error flags)Reborn keeps the headphone output open across track changes with the same output, rate, format and wired settings (old audio dropped, 10 ms fade-in; closed 2 s after the last stream or at Stop), so the amplifier and its charge pump no longer power-cycle per track. Stock keeps its output open tooReborn 0779d8b (sink_reused / sink_reopened events)
#54 on 8.22: the crystal was not ready even 45.9 s after power-upThe codec powers up as stock cs43131_hw_init: the crystal, the PLL and CLKOUT come up together, 20 ms later the crystal becomes MCLK (22.5792 MHz) and stays it until the codec powers down; its ready flag is read once there (ready_ms). The three DAC enables switch back to back with one 50 ms settle after the last (stock cs43131_on; was 50 ms after each)Linux 16c0911, d04ee8e (overlay 0030, DT, tests/test_codec_clock.py, tools/validation/dev_dtb.py)
#52/#54: stock keeps the DAC powered from screen-on to screen-offy2_hp/hold (kernel) holds the codec powered; Reborn sets it at startup and screen wake and releases it at screen-off and shutdown. System sleep still forces the codec down; after screen-off it powers down after the 2-min idle delayLinux 16c0911, Reborn 0779d8b
