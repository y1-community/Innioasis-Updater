Y2Linux 1.0 Beta 9.3.39: the cover stays while you seek, and films that can't play say so

Release 1.0.0-beta.9.3.39, Y2Reborn 1.0.0-beta.9.3.39, kernel 6.18.56-y2linux-1.0.0-beta.9.3.39, update sequence 18000, root 2026.08-platform-v1.298. Published as GitHub release baseline-275 (build 275; test build 274 came before it on the developer's player). Previous release: Beta 9.3.38.

This report is written for people who use the player. A short technical appendix is at the end.

In one paragraph

Two fixes. Seeking within a song (holding Next or Previous) no longer blanks the cover and no longer flashes "Starting…" (Y2Reborn #151, fixed by jonasevcik in #154). And a film in a picture format the player cannot show (HEVC/H.265, AV1 and others) is no longer listed among your songs: it stands in Videos and says it can't play.

What changed

  • The cover stays while seeking (#151, thanks to jonasevcik): the cover on Now Playing and Home turned into the

    music note for about half a second after every seek, and the quality line showed an orange "Starting…". Now both stay, also when Play continues a paused song. The short blank when a different song starts is not changed yet.

  • The restored song's cover survives Play (#150): the cover shown for the song a restart brought back now stays

    when you press Play, and it is fetched again once the SD card is ready.

  • Films the player cannot show (reported on Reddit): an MP4 or MKV with an HEVC (H.265) picture, the usual format

    of anime episodes, was read as its sound alone and appeared among the songs. Now it is a film: Videos lists it as "Can't play · HEVC (H.265)" (or AV1, MPEG-2, …), and selecting it says to convert it to H.264 MP4 instead of playing. It never appears among songs, albums, shuffles or on Last.fm. After the update the player reads every file's details once in the background, so files already in your library move to Videos by themselves.

How it was tested

  • Tests: the platform's full check, Reborn's tests and the tests of the release images passed. New tests: the

    same song keeps its cover and another song, another occurrence of it or a station logo do not; "Starting…" only for a song opened for the first time; an HEVC film is read as a film without a picture, an H.264 film as a film, a song's embedded cover stays a song; the Videos note and message.

  • On the developer's player, the screen filmed at 10 pictures a second while seeking in a 3-minute MP3 with an

    embedded cover: Beta 9.3.38 showed the note instead of the cover in 12 of 40 pictures (three seeks); with the fix 0 of 40, and 0 of 50 with "Starting…" never shown when seeking like a held button. An HEVC test film was read as hevc/aac, listed in Videos as "Can't play · HEVC (H.265)", and selecting it showed the message and played nothing.

  • Not tested on a player: AV1 and the other formats (they take the same path), a library of thousands of songs

    being read again after the update.

Updating

Settings → System → Software Update → Update Now. Your music, playlists, settings, Wi-Fi networks and Bluetooth pairings stay. Restore Previous System (Settings → System → About, tap Version seven times, then Diagnostics) goes back to the version you had.

Known issues

  • After the update the player reads every file's details once in the background (as after Beta 9.3.33's year);

    on a very large library the first minutes are a little slower.

  • When a new song starts, its cover still takes a moment to appear.
  • Everything listed for Beta 9.3.34 still applies.

Technical appendix

  • #151 / #154 (jonasevcik, rebase-merged): Runtime::art_owner (queue entry + library id) and

    runtime::keeps_art: load() keeps art for the same owner; AppModel::reopening set by apply_seek, ended by TrackStarted, a boundary or load(); views_playing::starting. #150 follow-up: restored_picture returns the owner, track_art::sources_changed.

  • Films: rb_video_unplayable_codec (reborn-media/native/video.c: HEVC, AV1, VVC, MPEG-1/2, VC-1, WMV1–3,

    MS-MPEG4 v1/v2, Theora, FLV1, VP6, ProRes, RealVideo); admit_streams and extract_metadata name such a file "<picture>/<sound>" with no artwork; Track::video_codec, Track::video_playable; Videos unplayable_note / unplayable_message; METADATA_REVISION 6. Fixtures film-hevc.mp4, film-h264.mp4 (tools/build/fixtures.py --films).

  • Images built from Linux a3794d82, Reborn 9d2930a.
