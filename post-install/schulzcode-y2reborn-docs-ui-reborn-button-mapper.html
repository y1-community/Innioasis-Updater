Button Mapper: plan

Owner request 2026-11-13: a custom button mapper with an animated UI. Any button the Y2 has can be given any action Reborn has, in two stages (screen on and screen off). Presets, with Reborn Default (how the buttons work today) always there to go back to. Above all, nobody may lock themselves out of the player.

Status: phases 0-2 and most of 3 built (2026-11-13), owner test build; not published. Assessed against Beta 9.3.28. The owner left every decision in section 7 to the proposals ("do all as you think it is best"). What was built differs from the first draft where section 8 says so.

1. Is it possible today?

PartPossible today?Why
Screen on: remap the buttons, the wheel and the volume buttonsYes, Reborn onlyEvery press already passes one place, reborn_platform::input::ActionRouter::route (physical event → Action); the UI then gives the action its meaning per screen. A table can replace the hard-coded match there. No kernel or platform change
Screen off, player awake (music playing, or the first minutes after a pause)Yes, Reborn onlyThe router already knows screen_on; "Buttons While Screen Off" (Locked / Buttons / Wheel and Buttons) is a fixed version of exactly this stage
Screen off, player asleep (paused for a while, then the system suspended)No, only Power worksPower is the only wake source the kernel enables (mtk-pmic-keys wakeup=enabled). The five front buttons are polled GPIOs (gpio-keys-polled, every 20 ms, kernel/dts/innioasis-y2.dts:185): no interrupt, so nothing can wake on them. The wheel (EINT 55) and the volume buttons (keypad, SPI 116) have interrupts and the SPM's suspend wake mask already includes WAKE_SRC_KP and WAKE_SRC_EINT (spm-suspend-policy.h:13), but neither device is wake-enabled, and the sleep helper treats every wake as "wake the screen"
PowerYes technically, but it must stay fixed (see 3)Power is the only button that wakes the player from sleep, and Power held (Quick Menu, Power Off) is the guaranteed way out

What playing music means for the stages: while a song plays the player never sleeps, so the "screen off" stage covers listening completely. It is the paused, asleep player where only Power works. Waking on other buttons is a kernel and power project of its own (phase 4); everything else needs no kernel change.

2. Refinements (things the request did not name)

  1. Contexts, not just two stages. Today one button already means different things in different places: the

    wheel scrolls a list but sets the volume (or seeks) on Now Playing; Select picks a row but plays and pauses in the dark. A mapper with only "on" and "off" would force one meaning everywhere. Proposal: three layers, Menus (every list and page), Now Playing (falls back to Menus for anything left unset), **Screen off**. The owner's two stages are Menus + Now Playing (screen on) and Screen off.

  2. Gestures. Each button gets Press and Hold; Previous/Next/volume also Hold repeats. Optional

    Double press, off by default: it makes a single press of that button wait ~300 ms, so it is only armed on buttons that have a double press mapped.

  3. Semantic actions, not raw keys. "Next" stays smart: next song, next chapter in a book (or 30 s, #103), next

    station on FM, next film. The mapper chooses what, Reborn keeps how per context.

  4. Some pages always use Reborn Default: the mapper itself, the Quick Menu, the on-screen keyboard (its

    arrows are the four ring buttons), pairing codes, alerts and the turn-off hold. These are the rescue path and the places where a remap would make a page unusable.

  5. Feedback in the dark. With the screen off a remapped button gives no sign of what it did. Use the haptics

    motor: one short tick for an action that was done, a double tick for "nothing mapped here", optionally a distinct pattern per action group. (Haptics already exist, Beta 8.41.)

  6. Pocket safety. Screen-off actions are easy to trigger by accident. The screen-off layer keeps its

    "Locked" preset as a one-tap choice, and actions that change state permanently (Delete, Clear Queue, Power Off, Forget…) are not offered in the screen-off layer at all.

  7. Migration of today's settings. "Wheel on Now Playing" and "Buttons While Screen Off" become parts of the

    mapping. A player that changed them gets a preset "My Buttons" made from its current settings on the first start, so nothing changes under anyone's fingers. The two old rows become shortcuts into the mapper.

  8. Presets as files. Presets are small JSON files; Reborn Default and a few built-ins ship with Reborn, the

    user's own live on /data. Later: export to and import from Internal Storage (Buttons folder), so people can share layouts (like skins and EQ profiles).

  9. External controls are out of scope here: Bluetooth headphone buttons (AVRCP), a wired headset button

    (parked PR #11). They could become a fourth layer later; the action catalogue is built so they can use it.

  10. Rollback safety. /data must stay readable by the previous version (Restore Previous System): the

    mapping is a new, separate file; an older Reborn ignores it and uses its own defaults.

  11. Timing for people. The hold time (650 ms today) could be adjustable (short / normal / long) for people

    who press slowly. Small, cheap, belongs on the same page.

  12. Skins never change the mapping, but the mapper page and the button help screen are drawn by Reborn, not

    the skin (as Wi-Fi Share), so they look the same in every skin.

3. Nobody gets locked out: the safety design

The rules, from the hardest to the softest. Each one alone already prevents a lockout; together they make one impossible even with a corrupt file.

  1. Power is not mappable. Power short = sleep/wake, Power hold = Quick Menu then Power Off, stay as they are.

    Power is the only wake source, and the PMIC's ~10 s hold reset works below any software. Proposal for later: allow a double press of Power (screen on only) as an extra mappable gesture.

  2. The Quick Menu is the guaranteed way back. It opens with Power held (fixed), it always uses Reborn Default

    inside, and it gets a tile Buttons with Reborn Default as its first choice. So from any state: hold Power → turn the wheel → Select → Reborn Default. The Quick Menu itself cannot be remapped away because Power is not mappable.

  3. The mapper page uses Reborn Default while it is open, so whatever was saved, the page that fixes it always

    works the same way.

  4. A layout must pass a reachability check before it can be saved. Reborn's UI is a pure model

    (Ui::action on an AppModel), so the check does not guess, it proves: starting from Home it explores the screens using only the inputs of the new layout and must be able to (a) reach every screen in the settings tree, (b) go back to Home from each, (c) scroll a long list both ways, (d) play, pause and leave Now Playing. A layout that fails is refused with the reason in words ("Nothing goes Back. Give Back to a button."). Host tests run this check over every shipped preset and thousands of random layouts.

  5. Keep or revert. After saving, a sheet asks "Keep these buttons?" with a 15-second countdown. It must be

    confirmed with the new layout's Select; otherwise the old layout comes back by itself (as screen resolution changes on a computer).

  6. Start-up escape. Holding Back (in its hardware position, whatever it is mapped to) while Reborn starts

    starts with Reborn Default for that session and offers to make it permanent. The start-up splash says so when a custom layout is active.

  7. Bad files fall back. An unreadable file, an unknown version or an action this Reborn does not know (an

    older Reborn after a rollback, a newer preset file) falls back to Reborn Default for that binding, logs it, and the error report shows it. The reachability check runs again at load; a stored layout that fails it is not used.

  8. Reset everything still resets the buttons (System → Reset & Maintenance → Reset Settings).

4. The design

Model

  • reborn_core::buttons: Control (Select, Back, Previous, Next, PlayPause, WheelForward, WheelBack, VolumeUp,

    VolumeDown), Gesture (Press, Hold, HoldRepeat, Double), Layer (Menus, NowPlaying, ScreenOff), Command (the catalogue below), Layout ((Layer, Control, Gesture) → Command), Preset (name, built-in or own, a Layout), validate(&Layout) -> Result<(), Problem>.

  • ActionRouter::route keeps the event machinery (holds, repeats, keyboard arrows, the screen-off swallow rules)

    and looks the command up in the active Layout instead of its match. Reborn Default is the table that reproduces today's match exactly, and a test proves it: every input sequence of the current router tests gives the same actions through the Reborn Default table.

  • Storage: /data/reborn/state/buttons.json (active preset name, own presets, hold time). Additive; no change to

    settings records, so the /data gate keeps passing.

Command catalogue (first version)

Each command carries what it may do: which layers offer it, whether it repeats when held, whether it needs the screen (then it lights the screen first), whether it is "destructive" (never in Screen off).

NavigationSelect, Back, Home, Up/scroll back, Down/scroll forward, Context menu (Options), Now Playing, Queue
PlaybackPlay/Pause, Next, Previous, Seek forward, Seek back, Restart song, Stop, Shuffle on/off, Repeat (cycle), Shuffle Albums, Play something (shuffle all)
Volume and soundVolume up, Volume down, Mute, Next output (Bluetooth / wired / speaker), EQ on/off
Books, radio, filmsNext/previous chapter, Rewind 30 s, Speed (cycle), Next/previous station, FM ⇄ Internet, Favourite station

The catalogue maps onto what already exists (Action, Effect, the Quick Menu actions); a new command is added in one place and shows up in the mapper automatically.

Built-in presets

  • Reborn Default: exactly today. Read-only.
  • Pocket: as Default with the screen on; in the dark only the volume buttons and Play/Pause work.
  • Wheel Seeks: the wheel moves through the song on Now Playing (today's setting, as a preset).
  • One Hand: Next/Previous on the volume buttons when the screen is off, volume on the wheel.
  • My Buttons: made once from a player's current settings during migration (only if they differ).

5. The UI (animated)

Place: Settings → Customize → Buttons (rename "Wheel & Buttons" to Buttons; it keeps Wheel Click and Haptics), per the settings map rule. Rows: Layout (preset picker), Screen On ›, Screen Off ›, Hold Time ›, Wheel Click, Haptics.

  1. The player face. Screen On / Screen Off open a drawing of the Y2: the wheel ring with its four buttons,

    the centre, Power and the volume buttons on the side. Each button shows its current command as a small label on a leader line. The labels slide in one after another on open (motion.rs springs, as the Quick Menu tiles).

  2. Press a button to change it. The page listens to the real buttons: pressing one makes it pulse on the

    drawing, its label lifts into a card "Next · Press → Next song · Hold → Seek forward". Turning the wheel selects the wheel the same way. The page uses Reborn Default for its own controls, so a pressed button is both "picked" and does nothing else; for people who prefer it, a row Choose with the Wheel walks the buttons one by one instead.

  3. Pick the gesture, then the command. The card flips to the command list, grouped as in the catalogue, the

    current one marked, with a one-line hint for each. Choosing animates the new label back to the button.

  4. Changed buttons glow in the accent colour; a "Changed from Reborn Default: 3" line leads to Reset.
  5. Save runs the reachability check (a short sweep animation over the face while it runs, ~50 ms), then the

    Keep-or-revert sheet with its countdown ring.

  6. Try it. A "Try Buttons" mode lights each button on the drawing and shows its command as you press it,

    without doing anything, so people can learn a layout.

  7. Screen off has the same face, dimmed, with a moon badge, and offers only screen-off commands.
  8. Help at any time: holding Select on the face shows a legend card of the whole layout.

6. Phases

  1. Audit + facts (docs only): roadmap/gap entry (a new user-facing subsystem); button combination test on

    the owner's player (which chords the hardware reports, ghosting); the settings-map change.

  2. Model and router (no UI): reborn_core::buttons, Reborn Default as a table, router reads the table,

    equivalence tests against today's router, buttons.json with fallbacks, the reachability checker and its random-layout tests. Shipped invisibly: behaviour identical.

  3. Mapper UI: Customize → Buttons, the face, capture, command lists, presets, Keep-or-revert, Quick Menu

    tile, start-up escape, migration of the two old settings, haptic feedback in the dark. Preview images via reborn-preview, device test with rebornctl input injection (every lockout rule tried on the player).

  4. Extras: double press, hold time, more built-in presets, export/import on Internal Storage, an owner page

    (Y2Linux docs/BUTTONS.md).

  5. Buttons while asleep (kernel + platform, separate go): read the stock kernel for the navigation GPIOs'

    EINT lines (GPIO 6/7/9/10/54) and whether stock woke on them; move the five buttons from polled to interrupt-driven gpio-keys with wakeup-source where the hardware allows, wake-enable the wheel and the keypad; teach the sleep helper a "button wake" that delivers the button to Reborn and goes back to sleep without lighting the screen when the command does not need it; measure drain (pocket presses waking the player) and wake latency (the first press would act after ~0.5-1 s). Hardware evidence first (AGENTS rule: new hardware subsystem → audit), and only the buttons that prove reliable are offered as "works while asleep".

Device test (owner's player, test builds 257 and 258, 2026-11-13)

Input injected as evdev records into the real input devices, so the new router ran (rebornctl actions bypass it):

  • The two older settings were at their defaults: migration chose Reborn Default (migrated: true).
  • Wheel and Select reached Customize > Buttons; Next pressed on the In Menus face picked Next (queue unmoved).
  • Next press → Shuffle On/Off, Save: the Keep sheet came with the new layout active; left alone, the old layout was

    back after 15 s and stored. Done again and Keep chosen with the physical Select: "own:0" stored; on Customize the physical Next toggled Shuffle instead of skipping.

  • Screen Off: Centre → Shuffle; in the dark the Centre toggled Shuffle with the screen staying off; Back did

    nothing; Power woke the screen.

  • Power held (injected on the PMIC keys) opened the Quick Menu; on 257 the Buttons row was drawn over Power Off

    (fixed in 258: its own tile); on 258 the tile's Select brought back Reborn Default.

  • Not testable by injection: holds of the five front buttons (the polled GPIO driver reports the release within

    20 ms), hence the Back start-up escape and front-button holds are host-tested only. The haptic tick in the dark was not felt (nobody at the player).

  • Afterwards: Reborn Default, the test layout deleted, Shuffle off again.
