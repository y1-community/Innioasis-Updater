What changed

The question came from an email about stock's bass roll-off and "output gain switching with impedance".

PartChange
Headphone levelKernel ALSA control HP Output Level (Low/Medium/High = CS43131 OUT_FS 1.0/1.41/1.73 Vrms with upstream hv_seq's HV_EN) and HP Output Level Live. Written only while the amplifier is down (DS1155F2), otherwise at the next power-up; probe default Low, the stock headphone level (before: High). Linux 2c59a31, test tests/test_hp_level.py (C harness on the real driver functions).
LoudnessReborn's DAC Master −19.5 dB with the control (was −24 dB): Low plays 0.26 dB below the old level, never louder; Medium +2.7 dB, High +4.5 dB. A higher level restarts wired playback and fades in over 2 s.
Headphone profilesEqualizer → Headphone Profile: AutoEq ParametricEQ.txt (peaking, low/high shelf with Q, up to 10 filters), clip-safe preamp (the file's or lower), limiter kept. 47 profiles of common headphones and IEMs come with the image (/usr/share/reborn/eq, AutoEq 7ae0f56, MIT); more from an EQ folder on Internal Storage or the SD card. FFmpeg gains lowshelf and highshelf.
DAC filterFast (default), Fast/Slow phase compensated, Slow, Nonoversampling (upstream codec controls).
Settings layoutSound fits one screen: Equalizer, Normalize Volume, Gapless, Crossfade and the Output card ("Level Low · Filter Fast"); the card opens the Output page (outputs, Headphone Level, DAC Filter). Owner chose this of three layouts.
Build5de9c95: a resumed build rebuilds FFmpeg when its component options change (it never compiled new filters before).
Toolstools/audio/test_signals.py: 88-s headphone measurement signal; Reborn tools/assets/fetch_eq_profiles.py.

Known gaps

  • Not measured: the real output voltage, noise and frequency response per level and load, and the cause of stock's

    bass roll-off (plan and test signal in Headphone output).

  • Not run on the player: the level and the fade after a raise from the Settings screen, a level kept across a full

    sleep (needs USB unplugged), the new Output page with the buttons.

  • Even High plays at most about 0.18 Vrms at full volume (stock about 0.71 Vrms): the Master limit is unchanged.
  • A 10-band profile costs about 7 % of one core while it plays; one filter thread may be cheaper.
  • Unchanged from 8.4: the restart fix over many restarts, the sleep drain (#42), the GC9503V panel (#45).
