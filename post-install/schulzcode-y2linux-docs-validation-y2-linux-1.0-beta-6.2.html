Known gaps

FM radio (next beta); no 12/24-hour switch (the zone decides); in-place decoder seek (≈1 s on Bluetooth); sleep ≈9.4 mA vs stock ≈1.3 mA; Bluetooth range hardware-limited; no headphone-jack detection.
