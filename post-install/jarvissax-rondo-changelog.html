What's new in Rondo

Rondo's releases, newest first. Each one is on the releases page, with the firmware (rom.zip) for Innioasis Updater CE and the update for a Y1 that already runs Rondo. Since 3.0.2 the server has releases of its own, in server/CHANGELOG.md.

3.0.3 (November 14, 2026)

An update for the 3.0 firmware: Settings › System › Software update, the dashboard or the update page. It works with Rondo server 3.1.0 or newer, as 3.0.2 does; with your own server, update it to Rondo server 3.2.0 (its admin page offers it) so YouTube Music downloads get their year and track number.

New

  • The dashboard fills in what your songs' tags leave out (Library › Fill in). A missing genre,

    year, album or track number is looked up album by album in the iTunes Store, then MusicBrainz; you see what was found for each album and untick any that look wrong, then it's saved into the songs themselves (FLAC, MP3, M4A), where Rondo, Jellyfin and any other player find it. Only what's missing is filled in.

  • Lyrics for every song that has none, from LRCLIB and timed where it has them, saved into the

    song (or an .lrc beside it for a kind that can't hold them). Songs with lyrics keep theirs.

  • Organize looks up a show's or a movie's years on the dashboard when the names don't say them

    (TVmaze for shows, Wikidata for movies): you pick the right one for each, and the folders keep the years from then on.

  • YouTube Music downloads. Hold center on a song and choose Download, or Download all on

    an album or playlist: the song goes to Music/YouTube Music/Artist/Album (Year)/01 - Title.m4a, YouTube's own AAC with its artist, album, year, track number, cover and lyrics (timed where LRCLIB has them) in its tags, as Jellyfin reads them. YouTube Music › Home › Downloads plays them from the card, also with no connection; Library › Music has them too. The dashboard's Downloads saves songs as well. With your own server, update it to Rondo server 3.2.0 for the year and the track number (an older one names a song from its row).

Changed

  • YouTube Music's Home looks like the app's: each section is a titled shelf of covers (square for

    songs, albums and playlists, wide for videos, round for artists), Downloads above them. Turn the wheel to move along a shelf, which slides as you go, and on into the next one; the background takes the chosen cover's color. Library stays a list.

  • A song's menu has an icon for each action (Play now, Play next, Add to queue, Start radio,

    Download).

  • Shows and movies go inside Videos, the way Jellyfin expects them:

    Videos/Shows/Silo (2023-)/Season 01/, Videos/Shows/The Walking Dead (2010-2022)/Season 01/ and Videos/Movies/Arrival (2016)/. A show still running is (2023-), one that has ended (2010-2022). Organize moves what's in the old top-level Shows and Movies folders and removes those folders once they're empty. Videos uploaded on the dashboard go to Videos.

Fixed

  • A YouTube video downloads and plays in its original language. On a video YouTube has dubbed,

    Rondo (connected directly to YouTube) could take a dub, such as the English one of a Turkish video; it takes the original track now. To get the original sound on a video downloaded before, download it again.

  • The dashboard's notes keep their distance from what's above and below them.
  • Changing Repeat or Shuffle no longer stops the song for a moment (Now Playing's options, hold

    center). Rondo decodes again only when the next song is already lined up; Play next from a song's menu is the same.

  • Library › Files slides in like every other screen: the first time after a start, counting a

    big card's free space held it up until its slide was over.

  • The dashboard's Wheel acceleration and Wheel vibration can be changed again (a choice's value

    read as nothing).

3.0.2 (November 12, 2026)

An update for the 3.0 firmware: Settings › System › Software update, the dashboard or the update page. With your own server, update it once to Rondo server 3.1.0 (its admin page offers it); from then on, Rondo and the server update apart.

Changed

  • Downloads are about three times faster. A YouTube download now comes in four parts at once

    instead of one piece after another: a 10-minute 720p video took 50 seconds instead of 145, and a two-hour episode about 8 minutes.

  • A download survives a dropout. When the Wi-Fi or the internet goes away for a while, the

    download waits and carries on where it stopped, instead of failing or starting over (Software update's download too).

  • The Y1 stays awake while a download runs, unplugged with the screen off too.
  • Rondo and the server have their own version numbers now. Most releases change only the Y1, so

    they no longer ask you to update your server. This Rondo works with Rondo server 3.1.0 or newer; with an older one, Settings › YouTube › Connection, the dashboard's server test and the YouTube screens say Server needs an update.

Fixed

  • YouTube's pictures in search, the feeds and on a download's row. YouTube sent them in a format

    the Y1 can't show, so they stayed empty.

  • A download's row says what it's doing: Downloading video, Downloading sound, Getting subtitles

    and Saving (it said "Picture").

3.0.1 (November 12, 2026)

An update for the 3.0 firmware: Settings › System › Software update, the dashboard or the update page. A Y1 still on 2.0 installs 3.0.1 with Innioasis Updater CE, as for 3.0.0.

Added

  • Charging shows on the battery. On a charger or a computer, a green bolt shows next to the

    battery in the status bar and the YouTube headers, and the charge turns green.

Fixed

  • YouTube and YouTube Music without internet. With Wi-Fi off they said "Loading…" for a long time;

    now they say "No Wi-Fi connection" at once and load by themselves when Wi-Fi is back. On a Wi-Fi network without internet they give up after 30 seconds at most, and say they couldn't reach YouTube instead of "HTTP 502".

  • Sharper subtitles. Their lines sat between two rows of pixels, which blurred every letter.

Security

  • The rest of SPWall is gone too: its packet filter, code injector and renamed iptables, which

    nothing started, and the stock firmware's distribution tag. Rondo removes them itself on a Y1 that updates, and the firmware leaves them out.

3.0.0 (November 12, 2026)

A new firmware without the stock firmware's root backdoors. It installs only with Innioasis Updater CE (Y1, Type A, Rondo 3.0.0); turn on Keep my settings so your settings, Wi-Fi networks, server connection and paired headphones stay. Software update on 2.0 shows 3.0.0 but can't install it (Moving from Rondo 2.0).

Security

  • The stock firmware's root backdoors are gone. Innioasis's firmware, and so every Rondo

    firmware before this one, ran three programs as root that nothing on the Y1 needs: - aggdll, the agent of an app pusher ("zxly"): a hidden su and a silent app installer, which also put a program of its own in place of Android's am; - wifi_init, the downloader it starts: it checks in with a server and runs what it downloads as root. That server doesn't answer today; - SPWall (libsp.so), a service that runs commands as root for other programs.

The firmware leaves all three out, with what they left behind, and puts Android's own am back. Rondo also removes them itself if it ever finds them.

Fixed

  • A Y1 installed again keeps the firmware's Rondo. With Keep my settings, a Y1 that took an update over

    Wi-Fi before would start that older update instead of the Rondo the firmware brought; an update no newer than the firmware's own Rondo is now set aside.

Changed

  • Rondo 1.0's Android app is out of the repository. Its source stays at the

    v1.0.2 tag; the dashboard's page, the font and the root certificates it shared with Rondo 2.0 moved to native/assets/.

2.0.1 (November 12, 2026)

A fix for Wi-Fi on a Y1 that installed 2.0.0 without keeping its data. Such a Y1 can't update over Wi-Fi, so install 2.0.1 with Innioasis Updater CE (Rondo, 2.0.1) or over USB from the update page in Chrome or Edge. Any other 2.0.0 gets it through Settings › System › Software update.

Fixed

  • Wi-Fi after a fresh install. On a Y1 installed without keeping its data, Wi-Fi never came on

    ("Wi-Fi didn't start"): Rondo powered the Wi-Fi chip on while its driver was still loading, the driver started twice, and the second one left the chip stuck until the next restart. Rondo now waits for the driver first (#3).

  • Next and Previous in Now Playing slide smoothly. The cover slid in a quarter of a second late and

    most of the way already done, and the new cover appeared only at the end: the slide now starts on the press, and the covers of the songs before and after are ready before you press.

2.0.0 (November 11, 2026)

A new firmware, with Rondo rebuilt as a program of its own, and YouTube that no longer needs a server. Install it with Innioasis Updater CE and choose Keep Data to keep your settings (moving from 1.0). From then on, updates come over Wi-Fi straight from GitHub.

YouTube without a server

  • YouTube and YouTube Music straight from the Y1. Settings › YouTube › Connection: **Directly

    to YouTube or My server**. Directly, there is nothing to set up: home, search, albums, artists, playlists, up next, Shorts and playback come from YouTube itself, and lyrics from YouTube Music and LRCLIB. A Y1 without a server uses it on its own.

  • Sign in to YouTube directly, with a code like a smart TV: on the Y1 (Settings › YouTube ›

    YouTube account) or the dashboard's YouTube page. It brings YouTube's home, Subscriptions and History, and YouTube Music's playlists and Liked Music; a cookies.txt (dashboard) adds YouTube Music's own home and history. Both stay on the Y1.

  • Your own server is optional now. With My server, the Y1 reaches YouTube only through

    it, as in 1.0, and it adds Headphone match (below). A server you already have keeps working.

Subtitles and downloads

  • Subtitles on YouTube videos and on the videos on the card: hold center while a video plays and

    pick a track under Subtitles. YouTube's tracks are the video's own and the speech recognition's; on the card they're the .srt, .vtt and .ass files beside the video, named as Jellyfin and Plex name them ("Movie.en.srt"). The last language picked is shown first next time.

  • Their look, your way: Settings › Display › Subtitles (and the dashboard's General page, with a

    preview): size, text color and opacity, background color and opacity, and edges (drop shadow, raised, depressed, outline). It starts as YouTube's own, white on a 75 % black box, and **Use YouTube's look** goes back to it. Through your server, YouTube's subtitles need the 2.0.0 server.

  • Download YouTube videos to watch with no connection, at 720p, 480p or 360p (the next height

    down when a video lacks it), with their subtitles: hold center on a video in a list, choose Download in a playing video's options, or paste a link on the dashboard's YouTube page. They're saved in Videos/YouTube and show in Library › Videos and in YouTube › Home › Downloads (there even with no connection), where you can cancel one on its way or delete one. Organize leaves them where they are.

Rondo, rebuilt

  • Rebuilt, with Android's app layer gone. Rondo is now a program of its own that draws straight to

    the screen, so it starts sooner and every screen opens and scrolls more smoothly. Everything looks and works as in 1.0.

  • 60 frames a second. The screen refreshes at 60 Hz instead of 42, so scrolling and slides are

    smoother and videos at 30 frames a second play evenly.

  • Motion like an iPod. Screens slide in and out, lists and their highlight glide, and artwork

    fades in. On Now Playing, the next song's cover slides in from the right and the one before from the left.

  • Shorts swipe like on a phone: the short slides up and away as the next one comes in.
  • Pocket-safe wake. With the screen off, any press only wakes it; nothing else happens. Within

    5 seconds of the screen dimming or going dark by itself, a press wakes it and does what it does (after Power, a press still only wakes it).

  • Wired headset buttons work: play/pause, next, previous and volume, screen on or off.
  • Back where you were. After a restart, the queue and the place in the song come back.

Library

  • Library in four: Music (in groups: Browse, Playlists, Listening, the card), Videos (all, the

    newest, by folder), Photos and Files, a file manager for the card and the internal storage: open, rename, copy, move (between the two storages too, in the background), delete, new folder. Plays, ratings and playlists follow what moves.

  • A richer library: Album artists and Genres. Albums show their year, two albums of one name by

    different artists stay apart, and songs play in disc and track order. Most played, Recently played and Top rated; rate a song in Now Playing (hold center) or on the dashboard. Rondo reads every song's tags once more after the update.

  • Cover Flow (Library › Music › Browse) on the Y1 and the dashboard: the albums' covers on a

    wide stage, the one in front facing you.

  • Photos on the Y1 and the dashboard: the pictures on the card by folder, full screen, sliding

    from one to the next, with a slideshow; camera photos turned the right way up.

  • Smart playlists: songs picked by rules (a genre, a year, a folder, ratings, plays, when they

    were added or last played), made on the dashboard and played from Library › Playlists. They follow your library as it changes.

  • Organize the card (Library, and on the dashboard): songs into Artist/Album (Year) folders,

    episodes into Shows and other videos into Movies, as Jellyfin, Plex and Kodi expect. You see every move first; lyrics, subtitles, covers, playlists, plays, ratings and the queue go along.

  • Every common music format: WMA, APE, WavPack, AIFF, AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS, DSD, TTA, TAK,

    Musepack, MKA, CAF, AMR, MP2 and AU besides MP3, AAC, ALAC, FLAC, Ogg, Opus and WAV, with their tags, lengths and covers; surround mixes down to stereo.

Sound

  • Settings › Sound: an equalizer with presets or ten bands of your own, kept for each output (the

    speaker, wired headphones, each Bluetooth device). Edit equalizer has a slider for each band, as on the dashboard, and saves your bands as a preset.

  • Headphone match: a correction for your headphone model, from AutoEq, through your server.
  • Loudness boost (peaks held, never clipped) and volume leveling (songs at one loudness).
  • Amplifier: the Y1's own amplifier, as it ships or 2, 4 or 6 dB louder.
  • Volume limit: the loudest step the wheel can set, on every output.
  • Opus for YouTube Music: songs stream as YouTube's Opus where the song has it (Now Playing

    says "Streaming · Opus"), else AAC as before. Through your server, this needs the 2.0.0 server.

Settings and the clock

  • Settings in groups: the Dashboard, then Connections, YouTube, Sound, Display, Controls and

    System.

  • A glyph and a color for every row: no screen shows one twice; Shuffle and Repeat have theirs

    everywhere, and every list of songs starts with Shuffle.

  • The date and the time in the status bar, in your time zone, kept while the Y1 is off.

    YouTube's headers keep only the battery, to leave room for their tabs.

  • Settings › System › Date & time: the time and the time zone each set automatically or by hand. Set

    automatically, the Y1 takes the time from the network and the time zone of wherever it is online, each time it joins Wi-Fi. By hand, you set the date, the time and the time zone yourself, and choose 24-hour time or AM/PM. A new Y1 starts in UTC (it used to stay on China's time zone).

  • Current time zone rules. The firmware's time zone data was from 2012, so clocks in Russia, Turkey,

    Mexico and other places that changed their rules since were an hour off for part of the year. Rondo's firmware has IANA's 2026 data.

  • A fixed Wi-Fi address. The Y1 shipped without one, so it showed up on the network as a new

    device at every start. Rondo now gives it one of its own, the same after any reinstall. Settings › System › About shows it.

The dashboard

  • Every setting: a Sound page (each output's equalizer, your own presets, headphone match,

    loudness boost, volume leveling, the amplifier, the volume limit), Subtitles, Date & time, and the choice between YouTube directly and your server, with the YouTube sign-in and downloads.

  • In groups: Connections (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, YouTube) and Settings (Sound, Theme, General); its

    Library in Music, Videos, Photos and Files, with Shuffle, sorting by genre, year, plays and rating, and Cover Flow.

  • A file manager: the dashboard's Files makes folders, renames, moves and deletes (several at

    once), uploads into any folder and downloads files.

  • On a phone: five tabs (Overview, Library, Radio, YouTube, Settings) instead of nine, the

    Library's search on a row of its own, the outputs in Sound one per line. The server's admin page fits a phone too.

  • Uploads of photos from the Library go to Pictures, and videos to Movies (never into Music).
  • The Overview shows the Wi-Fi address and the time zone.

Battery and safety

  • Both processor cores, at the speed the work needs: the second core and the lower clock steps

    are used again, as Android used them.

  • Wi-Fi saves power with the screen off, and scans less often while it isn't connected.
  • Overheating and charger warnings are back: "Too hot" shuts the Y1 down to cool off, and the

    charger and battery warnings (voltage too high, battery too hot, charging too long) appear as they did on the stock firmware, in the Y1's words.

Updates

  • Software update comes from GitHub, with or without a server. Settings › System › Software

    update checks the update page itself and installs a new Rondo over Wi-Fi, so a Rondo without a server gets its updates there too. The dashboard's Install downloads them from GitHub as before, and the update page installs them over USB.

  • Signed updates: an update is now a signed Rondo bundle (rondo-<version>.rondo), and Rondo

    installs only its own. An update that fails to start three times in a row is set aside and the firmware's own Rondo runs.

  • Rondo 1.0 hears of 2.0 on its dashboard (and, with a server, in Settings › Software update) and

    points to Innioasis Updater CE, since 2.0 needs its new firmware.

Fixed

  • Shorts: moving to the next or previous short showed the one before restarting without sound for a

    moment; the new short now shows from its own first picture.

  • A video quality the video lacks fell to 360p; it now plays the next height down (720p → 480p).
  • Videos shorter than 10 seconds had no thumbnail.

Smaller changes

  • It starts about a second sooner, and screens open without a stutter, also when their content

    arrives as they slide in.

  • Long Wi-Fi names and titles in the header end in "…" instead of running into the status icons.
  • Emoji in titles show in color, and long titles wrap without breaking them apart.
  • Artwork that comes every time. Card videos show their picture again, in Rondo and on the

    dashboard. Covers that don't arrive at once (Wi-Fi, a busy moment) are asked for again, on the Y1 and on the dashboard. On the dashboard, the song playing from YouTube Music shows its cover.

  • The Wi-Fi switch, the music volume of each output and the clock are kept across restarts.

Server

  • The server streams YouTube's Opus audio and the videos' subtitles as well, and looks up

    Headphone match's corrections (from AutoEq). Update it with Update on its admin page; an older server keeps working, with AAC, and without subtitles or Headphone match.

  • The server tells the Y1 its time zone, from the Y1's public address

    (privacy). With an older server the Y1 stays in UTC until you set the time zone by hand.

1.0.2 (November 3, 2026)

Nothing changes on the Y1 itself; this release is about the server.

Server

  • Update with one click. When a newer Rondo is out, the server's admin page offers Update:

    the server downloads the new version and restarts on it in a minute or two, keeping your settings, YouTube account and connected Y1. A small companion container, rondo-updater, does the work; it only ever touches the Rondo server's own container.

  • Ready-made server images. Installing the server now downloads it instead of building it, for

    regular PCs and 64-bit ARM (Raspberry Pi 4 and 5, many NAS boxes), which makes the first start much quicker on small machines.

  • Moving an older server over: a server set up before 1.0.2 built itself and has no updater

    yet. Update it by hand once (how); after that, Update on the admin page does it.

1.0.1 (November 3, 2026)

An update over Wi-Fi, the dashboard or USB: no reinstall needed, and everything on your Y1 stays.

Fixed

  • Click sound stays off. Turning off Settings › Click sound (or the switch in the

    dashboard) now lasts through a restart. Before, the Y1's own sound profile turned the click back on every time it started.

Updates

  • Every release now comes with its firmware. The rom.zip that Innioasis Updater CE installs

    is built automatically for each release, so a new install always gets the newest Rondo.

  • Rondo knows which updates it can install. The firmware now carries a version of its own.

    If a future release changes the firmware itself (not only Rondo), Settings › Software update, the dashboard and the update page say so and point you to Innioasis Updater CE, instead of offering an install that couldn't work. The Y1 refuses such an update however it arrives.

  • One place for news of a release. The Y1 (through your server), the dashboard and the update

    page all learn about a new Rondo from the update page, and only once its firmware is ready, so they always agree with each other and with Innioasis Updater CE.

Server

  • The server's version is now 1.0.1 as well. It reads Rondo's updates from the update page and

    tells a Y1 when a release needs a new firmware. Update it as described in Keeping it running.

Documentation

  • A new README with screenshots, and updated guides for installing, using, updating and the

    dashboard.

1.0.0 (November 2, 2026)

The first release of Rondo as a firmware: the Y1 starts straight into Rondo, with nothing else on it. Install it with Innioasis Updater CE (Y1, Type A, then Rondo).

  • iPod-style menus for the click wheel, with optional vibration and a click on every step, and

    wheel acceleration for long lists.

  • Your music and videos from the microSD card, with covers, lyrics, search and playlists, and

    the card as a USB drive when the Y1 is plugged into a computer.

  • YouTube Music and YouTube, streamed live through your own Rondo server, including Shorts, with

    or without a YouTube account.

  • FM radio with station names, scanning and saved stations.
  • Bluetooth headphones and speakers, and Wi-Fi, set up from the wheel.
  • A dashboard in your phone's browser for music, playlists, settings, the theme, Wi-Fi,

    Bluetooth, the radio and updates.

  • Light and dark looks, accent colors, text sizes and a main menu you can reorder.
  • Shut down and restart from Settings, or by holding the Power button.
  • Updates without reinstalling: from the Y1 over Wi-Fi, the dashboard, or the update page over

    USB.
