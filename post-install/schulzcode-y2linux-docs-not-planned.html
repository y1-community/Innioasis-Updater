Ideas that won't be built, and why

Thank you for every idea. Some have been asked for more than once and won't be built, for the reasons below. If yours is on this list, please don't open a new request for it; if you have a new argument or a different way to do it, comment on the issue linked there instead.

Something missing here may still be a duplicate: please search the open and closed issues of Y2Linux and Y2Reborn first.

Battery and charging

IdeaWhy notIssue
Battery percentage on the charging screen while the player is offThe Y2 can't measure the charge going in or out, so the percentage is estimated from the battery's voltage. While charging, that voltage reads too high; the running player corrects for it, a switched-off one can't. The number would be wrong most of the time. Switch the player on to see the level.Y2Reborn #72, Y2Linux #109
Stop charging at 80 % or 90 %For the same reason the percentage isn't precise enough for a charge limit to mean what it says.Y2Reborn #64
Battery level of Bluetooth headphonesMost headphones report it only over the phone-call side of Bluetooth, which the player doesn't run: a second connection to every pair of headphones makes Bluetooth less steady. Without it only a few would show a level (AirPods never).Y2Reborn #95

Buttons and wheel

IdeaWhy notIssue
Double tap on the centre buttonTo tell a double tap from a single one, the player would have to wait after every tap, which makes the button slower everywhere. Tap selects, hold opens the menu.Y2Reborn #98
Wake the player with the centre button or the wheelOnly Power wakes it, so it can't wake in a pocket or bag. What the other buttons do with the screen off is in Settings → Customize → Buttons → Screen Off.Y2Linux #71
A setting for the wheel's seek stepThe wheel already seeks faster the faster you turn it.Y2Reborn #50
A setting for button debounceIf buttons bounce on a player, that gets fixed for everyone rather than turned into a setting. Send an error report if you see doubled presses.Y2Reborn #54

Menus and screens

IdeaWhy notIssue
Reorder the rows of a menuEvery menu lists its actions in the same order, so each action is in the same place wherever you are. (The places on Home can be reordered: Settings → Customize → Home Menu.)Y2Linux #66, Y2Reborn #98
Play state and track time in the standard status barThe standard bar stays clock, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and battery. A skin can show both anywhere: see the skin guide.Y2Linux #69
More Cover Flow and artist display modesDifferent looks are what skins are for; the player itself keeps one.Y2Reborn #65
Sort options for AudiobooksOne order: in progress first, then new ones A to Z, then finished.Y2Reborn #27

Sound and playback

IdeaWhy notIssue
More volume or gain for Bluetooth, or with an equalizer profileThe player lowers the level just enough that the profile's highest boost can't distort. Louder would mean distortion; a profile with smaller boosts keeps more volume.Y2Linux #80
Crossfade or gapless on a manual skipA skip should be immediate. Gapless and crossfade apply when one song runs into the next.Y2Linux #65

Wi-Fi

IdeaWhy notIssue
WPA3-only Wi-Fi networksThe Y2's Wi-Fi chip is too old for WPA3. A "WPA2/WPA3 mixed" setting on the router works.Y2Linux #91
