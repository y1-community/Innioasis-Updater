Y2Linux 1.0 Beta 9.3.32: newer memory chips, USB that comes back, the wheel click and films over Bluetooth

Release 1.0.0-beta.9.3.32, Y2Reborn 1.0.0-beta.9.3.32, kernel 6.18.56-y2linux-1.0.0-beta.9.3.32, update sequence 16900, root 2026.08-platform-v1.286. Published as GitHub release baseline-263 (build 263). Previous release: Beta 9.3.31.

This report is written for people who use the player. A short technical appendix is at the end.

In one paragraph

This release comes out of a pass over every open issue of both projects. It lets Y2s with a newer memory chip install Y2Linux, brings USB back by itself after a dropped connection, fixes the wheel click after a USB transfer and keeps films in step with Bluetooth headphones. Playlists get Clear Playlist and no longer take a song twice; album pages show their year; skin makers get three new pieces.

What changed

  • Stuck at "Preparing storage" (Y2Reborn #130). Some Y2s

    carry a memory chip whose security area (RPMB) is 4 MB instead of 512 KB. Y2Linux knew only one chip layout and waited forever. Where the music area starts depends on that size: the stock firmware works it out as 20 MB minus both boot areas and the security area, and Y2Linux now does the same. Players with the chip known so far start exactly as before.

  • USB stayed dead until a restart (Y2Linux #117). A report

    showed the player's USB connection undone in the middle of a copy while the transfer program ran on; nothing set it up again, so the computer saw nothing even after replugging. The player now notices that and sets the connection up again. Why it was undone during that copy is not known yet.

  • The wheel clicked on every other row (Y2Linux #127). After a

    USB file transfer the music is stopped, and while stopped the player forgot each click right after starting it; the output ran empty and the next click was lost waking it up. A restart helped because it brings the player back paused.

  • Films ahead of the sound on Bluetooth (Y2Reborn #133). The

    player's idea of where the sound is left out most of the Bluetooth delay, so the picture ran ahead by up to that delay. Now it counts it, and the time shown on Now Playing follows what you hear too.

  • Clear Playlist (Y2Linux #103): hold Select on a playlist,

    Favorites included. It asks first; the songs stay in your library.

  • No song twice (Y2Linux #106): Add to Playlist leaves out songs

    the playlist already holds and says how many.

  • Album pages show the year first ("2019 · 12 songs · 48:10", Y2Linux #125),

    and album and artist pages say Play All and Shuffle All (Y2Reborn #145).

  • Skins: {year} (#135), {page_title} is the album's or

    artist's name on their pages (#144), and "if": "scrollbar" inside list rows (#138). See the skin guide.

How it was tested

  • Tests: the platform's full check, Reborn's tests and the tests of the release images passed. New tests: both

    memory chip layouts (every other one refused), USB set up again after an unbind, every wheel click after a stop on an output that runs dry, the position behind a long Bluetooth delay, the new skin values and condition, the album line and labels, Clear Playlist and songs already in a playlist.

  • On one player (Beta 9.3.30): the click problem was reproduced (stopped, a click every 0.6 s: an underrun on

    every second one). Holding a volume button with the screen off, set to Next, skipped songs paused and playing (simulated presses). Copies of 545 MB and 2.5 GB over USB across the screen going dark finished.

  • Not tested on a player: a Y2 with the 4 MB chip, a USB connection undone during a copy, films over Bluetooth,

    and this release itself.

Updating

Settings → System → Software Update → Update Now. Your music, playlists, settings, Wi-Fi networks and Bluetooth pairings stay. Restore Previous System (Settings → System → About, tap Version seven times, then Diagnostics) goes back to the version you had.

Known issues

  • AirPods can stay silent after you walk out of range and come back while the link holds; pause and play brings the

    sound back (Y2Reborn #132).

  • Holds of the volume buttons with the screen off did nothing for one listener

    (Y2Linux #124); not reproduced yet.

  • Everything else listed for Beta 9.3.30 still applies.

Technical appendix

  • Kernel kernel/platform/storage-layout.h: y2_emmc_user_offset() = 40960 − 256 × (2 × BOOT_SIZE_MULT +

    RPMB_SIZE_MULT); y2_emmc_layout_valid accepts RPMB_SIZE_MULT 4 or 32; patch 0009 keeps y2_user_offset per host and passes it to y2_emmc_wire_arg. The disk stays 15203328 sectors on both chips.

  • Platform service/usb.rs: lost_bind() (empty UDC under a bound gadget) fails the attempt, so the responder stops

    and the next tick sets the gadget up and binds it.

  • Reborn playback/output.rs: stopped() forgets clicks once per stop (stop_seen); heard_ms() = block start +

    written − delay, clamped at zero. skin/: Var::Year, Flag::Scrollbar, {page_title} from source_label. catalog.rs page_detail; PlaylistEdit::Clear, ConfirmAction::ClearPlaylist; picker skips resolved songs.

  • Images built from Linux 91dc6a81, Reborn 5e4b024.
