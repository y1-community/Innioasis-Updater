The dashboard

The dashboard is a web page served by the Y1 itself. Use it from a phone or computer to upload music, change settings and look after the Y1 without turning the wheel through every menu. It needs no server, only the same Wi-Fi as the Y1 (and the internet for what comes from YouTube or GitHub, like signing in or an update).

Opening it

  1. On the Y1, open Settings › Dashboard. It shows an address like http://192.168.1.118:8080,

    a QR code of it, and a 6-digit pairing code.

  2. Open that address in a browser on the same Wi-Fi, or scan the QR code with your phone.
  3. Type the pairing code.

The dashboard runs only while that screen is open on the Y1. Press Menu on the Y1 to stop it. The pairing code changes every time. If someone types five wrong codes, the dashboard pauses pairing for half a minute.

Overview

What's playing, with play/pause, skip and a volume slider, plus the battery, how full the card is, the Wi-Fi network with the Y1's IP and MAC addresses, where YouTube comes from (directly or your server) and the time zone, and Rondo's version, with Restart and Shut down buttons.

Your browser checks GitHub for a newer Rondo (the update page's site). When one is out, a row says so, with Install: the browser downloads the update and sends it to the Y1, which installs it and restarts with everything kept (the dashboard stops with it). Update from a file, under the Rondo row, does the same with a rondo-<version>.rondo you downloaded from the releases page. A release that needs a new firmware shows no Install button: it points to Innioasis Updater CE instead.

The sidebar has the pages in groups: Overview, Library and FM Radio, then Connections (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, YouTube) and Settings (Sound, Theme, General). On a phone the tabs at the bottom are Overview, Library, Radio, YouTube and Settings, which lists the rest.

Library

Everything on the card, in four parts as on the Y1: Music (songs, albums and playlists), Videos, Photos and Files. Music has search, Shuffle, and sorting by title, artist, album, album artist, genre, year or date added, and by what you listen to: most played, recently played and rating. Albums are a Cover Flow: arrow keys, the mouse wheel or a drag move through the covers, and the album's songs are listed below. A song's details show its tags and plays, with stars to rate it.

  • Upload music, videos and photos straight onto the card, several at a time: music goes to

    Music, videos to Videos, photos to Pictures; Organize then puts them where Jellyfin expects them. Expect about 3 MB a second over the Y1's Wi-Fi.

  • Play a song on the Y1, or delete it.
  • Rescan after copying files onto the card some other way.
  • Photos: every picture on the card by folder; click one to see it large, with the arrow keys,

    a slideshow (Space) and Download.

  • Files: the card as it is, folder by folder (and the internal storage). Make folders, rename,

    move and delete files and folders, several at once with their checkboxes, upload into the folder you're in (pictures, lyrics and subtitles too) and download a file to your computer. Songs and videos keep their plays, ratings and playlists when they move, and a moved folder takes them all.

  • Organize: every file into the folders Jellyfin expects (as Library › Organize the card on

    the Y1), with the full list of moves to look through first; nothing moves until you press Organize. Shows go to Videos/Shows/Silo (2023-)/Season 01/ and movies to Videos/Movies/Arrival (2016)/. When a show's or a movie's name doesn't say its years, Look up their years asks TVmaze (shows) and Wikidata (movies) and lets you pick the right one for each; a show still running is (2023-), one that has ended (2010-2022). The folders keep the years from then on.

  • Fill in (the dashboard only): what your songs' tags leave out, looked up online and saved into

    the songs themselves, where Rondo, Jellyfin and any other player find them. Only what's missing is filled in, nothing is replaced. - Song details: a missing genre, year, album or track number, album by album from the iTunes Store (in English, from the stores of your browser's languages, the songs' own and the US one), then MusicBrainz. You see what was found for each album and untick any that look wrong before anything is saved. About three seconds an album. - Lyrics: LRCLIB's, timed where it has them, for every song that has none: saved into the song (FLAC, MP3, M4A), or as an .lrc beside a song whose kind can't hold them. - It runs on the Y1: close the page and come back to see how far it got. Details are saved into FLAC, MP3 (ID3v2) and MP4/M4A files; Ogg, WMA and the other kinds keep theirs as they are.

  • Playlists: make, rename and delete playlists of songs on the card, add songs (from the

    playlist, or Add to playlist in a song's details), put them in order and play them on the Y1. They're the same playlists as in the Y1's Library: .m3u8 files in the card's Playlists folder.

  • Smart playlists: songs picked by rules instead of by hand: a title, artist, album, album

    artist, genre or folder that is, contains or starts with something; a year, rating, play count or length at least or at most a number; added or last played in the last so many days. Match all rules or any, choose the order (random, by artist, most played, best rated…) and a limit. The Y1 works the songs out whenever you open one, so it follows what you add, play and rate; it lists them under Library › Playlists. They're .smart.json files beside the playlists.

Theme

The Y1 redraws while you change things here.

  • Accent: the color of the selection bar, from a few presets or any color you pick.
  • Appearance: light or dark menus.
  • Text size: small, medium or large.
  • Main menu: show, hide and reorder the entries. Settings is always there.

General

The same settings as on the Y1 (see Using Rondo): brightness, screen timeout, lyrics, wheel acceleration, wheel vibration and the click sound. The Theme page above matches the Y1's Settings › Display › Theme, and video quality is on the YouTube page.

Subtitles, as the Y1's Settings › Display › Subtitles: on or off, the language looked for first, size, text color and opacity, background color and opacity, and edges, with a preview that changes as you pick; Use YouTube's look goes back to YouTube's own.

Date & time, as on the Y1: the time and the time zone each set automatically or by hand, and 24-hour time. By hand, set the date and the time (or take your computer's) and pick the time zone.

Sound

The Y1's Settings › Sound. Each output keeps its own sound: pick the speaker, wired headphones or a Bluetooth device (the one music plays on now is marked), then:

  • Equalizer: a preset, or ten bands you drag yourself; the sound follows as you drag. **Save as

    a preset keeps your bands for every output; Flat** sets them back.

  • Headphone match: find your headphone model in AutoEq's measurements and Rondo corrects its

    sound. This one needs a server, which looks the model up.

  • Loudness boost: louder, the peaks held and never clipped.

For every output: volume leveling, the Y1's amplifier, and the volume limit, the loudest step the wheel and the dashboard can set.

Wi-Fi

The network the Y1 is on, your saved networks and the ones nearby. Join a network with its password, forget one, or refresh the list. Typing a password here is much quicker than on the wheel.

Bluetooth

Turn Bluetooth on or off, search for headphones and speakers, pair and connect them, and forget devices you no longer use. Put the headphones in pairing mode before you search.

FM Radio

What the radio is playing, with the station name and signal strength, and controls to tune, seek up and down, scan the whole band, change the volume, mute, and switch between headphones and the speaker. You can also name, reorder and delete saved stations, save the ones a scan found, and set the region. Wired headphones must be plugged into the Y1, because they're the antenna.

YouTube

Where YouTube Music and YouTube come from: Directly to YouTube (nothing to set up, and what a Rondo without a server uses) or My server, the Rondo server the Y1 is connected to. The Y1's own Settings › YouTube does the same on the Y1.

  • YouTube account (directly to YouTube): Sign in with a code, as a smart TV does: **Get a

    code**, open google.com/device, enter the code and allow access; the page updates by itself. That brings YouTube's home, Subscriptions and History, and YouTube Music's playlists and Liked Music. The Y1 can do the same itself (Settings › YouTube › YouTube account). With a server, the server's own account counts: sign it in on its admin page.

  • YouTube Music home and history (optional, directly to YouTube): a cookies.txt from a

    browser. Open a private window, sign in at youtube.com, export the cookies with a cookies.txt browser extension, close the window at once, then Upload cookies.txt here. The page says who is signed in. The file stays on your Rondo (only its youtube.com lines are sent) and is never shown; Sign out deletes it. A separate Google account for your Rondo is the safest choice. With it, YouTube Music has your own home and history too.

  • Video quality: Auto (up to 360p, which plays smoothest), or a fixed height.
  • Your server: its address, whether the connection is encrypted, and a link to its admin

    page.

  • Connect to a server: enter the server's address and the code from its admin page

    (Device › Connect a Rondo), then press Connect.

  • Use a token instead: only for servers started with an API_TOKEN setting. Servers set up

    as in the server guide don't need it.

  • Connection test: the Y1 calls the server and reports how long it took, whether yt-dlp is

    ready and whether an account is connected.

Downloads: paste a YouTube link (or a video id), pick 720p, 480p or 360p and press Download: your Rondo saves it to Videos/YouTube on its card with its subtitles. Choose Song (a YouTube Music link picks it by itself) to save a song instead, to Music/YouTube Music with its details, cover and lyrics. The list shows each one on its way (Cancel), then the videos and songs on the card (Save to your computer, or the bin to delete one). The Y1 has the same lists in YouTube › Home › Downloads and YouTube Music › Home › Downloads.

For developers
