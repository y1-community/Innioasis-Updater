FM radio on the Y2

Status: in Beta 9.3.26 (2026-11-12; owner test builds 249 and 250 before it). FM has played on one player, a first-revision Y2 that receives no stations; nobody has confirmed reception under Y2Linux yet. This page says who can use it, how, and what is known.

Which players

The Y2's radio chip contains an FM tuner in every unit, but not every unit can receive with it.

Your Y2FM
A later revision (the stock software's FM radio found stations on it)Supported: this is the player FM is for
The first revision (the stock FM radio never found a station)The tuner switches on and tunes, but only noise arrives. No software can change that: hide FM (below)

If you do not know which you have, try a search. It takes a few seconds and harms nothing.

Setting it up

FM needs two small files of MediaTek's from the same system.img that Wi-Fi and Bluetooth need (the Innioasis Y2 firmware package v3.2.0). Y2Linux may not hand them out.

  • A new installation takes them along when you copy system.img into the Firmware folder

    (INSTALL).

  • A player that was set up before FM arrived has Wi-Fi and Bluetooth but not these two files, and the player

    deleted its copy of system.img back then. Make a folder named Firmware on Internal Storage (or the SD card) and copy system.img into it once more. Within a minute the player takes the two files and deletes the copy; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are not interrupted. Until then FM Radio says "Needs the tuner firmware".

Using it

  • Radio on Home now opens as FM Radio and Internet Radio. (On a player without FM, or with FM hidden,

    Radio is the internet stations as before.)

  • Plug in wired headphones: their cable is the antenna, also when the sound goes to Bluetooth.
  • Search for Stations goes through 87.5 to 108 MHz and lists what it finds by frequency. Search Again is

    the last row of that list; search again in another place.

  • A station plays like any radio station: Now Playing shows the frequency and Stereo or Mono, the volume, the

    outputs and Favorites work as usual, and it keeps playing with the screen off.

  • FM stops when the player goes to sleep by the Power button, and does not come back by itself after the wake.

Hiding FM

On a player that receives nothing, FM Radio is only in the way.

  • The page of a search that found no station has Hide FM Radio.
  • Settings → Customize → Browsing → FM Radio is the switch for it, and brings FM back.

What was checked, and what was not

On a first-revision Y2 (test builds 249 and 250): the tuner switches on and reports the right chip, tunes across the band, and reads a signal level (−102 to −107 dBm everywhere: noise); its sound arrives as a steady stream; the search runs and saves its result; a station plays in Reborn; pausing switches the tuner off after three seconds and resuming switches it on again; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth keep working beside it. The commands the driver sends are the stock firmware's, compared byte for byte in an emulator.

Not checked: reception of a real station (needs a later-revision Y2), sleep with FM playing, FM over Bluetooth by ear, long listening and battery use. There is no RDS (station names) yet. The search on the first-revision player listed one or two frequencies that are probably not stations.
