Flash tool for Innioasis and Timmkoo players

Both tools flash a player, so the difference is what happens around the flash. Updater CE is the flash tool for Innioasis and Timmkoo players when you want your music, settings and Bluetooth pairings left alone: it chooses the package for the model, runs the install, and warns you before an incompatible file is used. The Universal Flash Tool is the recovery-first option, and it wipes the player.

Reach for Updater CE for a normal install, an update, or a return to original software. Reach for the Universal Flash Tool only when Updater CE cannot finish and you have a model-matched package and scatter file. The Οδηγός SP Flash Tool covers that manual route for Innioasis Y1 and Y2.

The community route

Innioasis Updater CE (Community Edition)

Innioasis Ενημερωτής CE is the tool creators recommend first. It updates, customises and recovers your player without putting your music or settings at risk.

  • Updates without wiping internal media or settings: Updater CE updates your player without wiping internal storage. Your settings, paired Bluetooth audio devices (headphones and speakers), and onboard music and videos are safely preserved. (Media stored on microSD cards is not affected by either tool.)
  • Active update notifications: The app actively notifies you right on your computer when new software updates, features, or stability releases become available — both for the firmware/software on your player and for the Updater app itself. Player updates and new versions of the tool show up in the same place.
  • Built alongside custom software developers: Created hand-in-hand with the developers behind Rockbox, Better-Y, Solar, JJ Launcher, Inniclassic, Y2Player, and original factory software, so everything works together and keeps getting maintained.
  • Works on older and more kinds of computers: Works reliably across older and modern machines alike - supporting Intel x86_64 Macs and Apple Silicon Macs (macOS 10.15+), Linux (official AppImage for Intel/AMD), and Windows (Windows 10/11 x64 and experimental ARM64).
  • Self-explanatory interactive prompts: Guides you through every step directly on screen with visual cues (when to power off, when to plug in USB, and when flashing is complete). No external video manuals or PDF guides needed.

The factory utility

Innioasis Universal Flashing Tool / Shared Flash Tool

Ο Innioasis Universal Flash Tool is also called the Innioasis Shared Flash Tool. It was created strictly for offline manual installations of factory stock software packages and was not designed in collaboration with custom software creators.

  • Wipes internal storage: Flashing with the shared/universal flash tool wipes internal storage on devices completely. This means having to reconnect Bluetooth audio devices, re-configure all your preferences, and replace your music and videos after every update. (Media on microSD cards is unaffected by either tool.)
  • No update notifications: An offline-only flasher with no mechanism to alert you when new updates or improvements are released for your player.
  • Not built with custom project creators: Designed only for official raw ROM archives; community firmware projects are unsupported.
  • Fewer computers supported: the Mac build needs Apple Silicon on macOS 15 or later, so Intel Macs are out. There's no Linux or ARM64 Windows version either.
  • Tough to follow: no in-app guidance. You have to consult outside videos and multi-page PDFs just to connect and flash.
