Privacy

Last updated 8 November 2026

innioasis.app hosts the Innioasis Updater CE downloads, the firmware archive and the guides that go with them. It is run by Ryan Specter with the Innioasis community. Luci Ltd provides hosting. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

What stays on your device

The site saves a few settings in your browser: your language, colour theme, text size, privacy choices, whether you hid the donation appeals, and translations it has already fetched. These stay on your device. Clearing site data removes them.

What we measure

We use Google Tag Manager with Google Analytics to count page views and to see which pages fail or load slowly. Cloudflare provides hosting, security and attack protection. Download counts are kept in aggregate. We do not ask for your name, email or postal address, and we do not sell data.

Cookies and local storage

The site uses browser storage for the settings above. Google Analytics and Cloudflare may set their own cookies. You can block or clear cookies in your browser at any time; the download pages will still work.

Your choices

You can turn analytics off without losing access to any download. The setting is remembered on this device and shared with the themes gallery through a cookie on innioasis.app.

Analytics is currently on for this browser.

Donations

Donation buttons link to Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, PayPal, Revolut, Patreon and Boosty. Those services handle the payment and their own data. This site never sees your card details.

Links to other sites

Guides link to manufacturers, GitHub and community sites, and the footer links to Discord and Reddit. Those sites have their own privacy policies. We do not control what they collect.

Your rights

If you live in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you can ask what personal data relates to you, ask for a copy, ask us to delete it, or object to processing. California residents can ask to know or delete personal information and can opt out of sale; we do not sell it.

Because the site has no accounts, we may not be able to connect a request to a visitor without the consent record stored in that visitor's browser. You can contact us through the repository issues page or r/innioasis.

Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted on the home page.

The English text on this page is the authoritative version. Translated copies are provided so the policy is readable in your language; if a translation and the English text disagree, the English text applies.
