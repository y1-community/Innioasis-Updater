Timmkoo and other Innioasis firmware

These players do not use the Y1 and Y2 theme gallery. Their system updates are here, and the package has to match the model printed on the player.

Models

  • Innioasis / Timmkoo Q3 and Q3E
  • Innioasis / Timmkoo Q5 and Q8
  • G1, G3, G5
  • A5, D1, R1, PR1, SR1

Install with Updater CE. Choose the model first, then the software, then a stable release.

Y1 and Y2

Those two have their own pages: Y1 firmware and Y2 firmware. Their themes are a different download, on themes.innioasis.app.
