Your privacy on Innioasis.app

No cookies. No fingerprinting.

Innioasis.app — the site for Updater Community Edition — does not use cookies and does not do any browser fingerprinting. There are no trackers, no analytics beacons, and no third-party ads.

Your site theme and text size are always remembered — they are the look and readability you chose. Links to GitHub, YouTube, Discord, Ko-fi, PayPal, Revolut, and Patreon open those sites' own pages, which have their own policies.
