Questions people ask

What is Rondo?

A lightweight custom audio system for the Y1 with iPod-style click-wheel menus. Rondo is built for the Y1's wheel: fast menus, local music and videos, a dashboard, and optional streaming through your own server.

Is Rondo Y1 the same as Rondo?

Yes. Rondo Y1, Rondo firmware, Rondo music player all refer to the same project.
