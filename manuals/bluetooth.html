Pair Bluetooth headphones or a speaker

Bluetooth is for listening, not for transferring files. Pair once, and the player remembers the device.

All player manuals Firmware archive
  1. Put your headphones or speaker into pairing mode. Most devices flash a light when they are ready.
  2. On the player, open Settings → Bluetooth and turn Bluetooth on.
  3. Wait for the device to appear under Other devices, then select it.
  4. Confirm the pairing on both sides if you are asked.

To disconnect, select the device under My Device and confirm. To forget it completely, long-press the device and choose to ignore or remove it. If the list looks stale, use Refresh the device list.

No sound? Check the volume on both the player and the headphones. Some speakers start at a very low volume after pairing. If the player still plays through its own speaker, disconnect and pair again rather than changing any firmware setting.
