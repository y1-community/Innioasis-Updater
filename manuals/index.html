How-to articles

Same steps, any model.

First start: charge, power on and pick a language

A new player only needs three things: a little charge, a long press on power, and a minute in Settings.

Put music, audiobooks and video on your player

Your player behaves like a USB drive with a screen. Copy files into the right folder and it finds them.

Pair Bluetooth headphones or a speaker

Bluetooth is for listening, not for transferring files. Pair once, and the player remembers the device.

Use FM radio (and why it needs wired headphones)

FM radio uses the headphone cable as its antenna. Plug in a pair, scan once, then save the stations you like.

Battery, charging and making a charge last

These players are designed to run for days, not hours. A few habits keep the battery healthy.

Storage, microSD cards and finding files again

Internal storage fills up quickly with lossless music. A microSD card is the simplest upgrade.

Update or restore your player's software

A software update is not the same as a factory reset. One keeps your files; the other erases them.

Factory reset: what it does and how to do it safely

A factory reset returns the software to its first-run state. It also erases the player's internal storage, so read this first.

Reset and recovery

A factory reset erases the player's internal storage. A software restore replaces the system. They solve different problems, so the reset guide starts by explaining which one you need. If the player will not turn on at all, go to the recovery guide instead.

About these pages

These manuals are a community transcription of the official PDF manuals at innioasis.com/pages/support-center and the support knowledgebase. The manufacturer's PDF remains the authoritative document, and every model page links to it. If you spot a difference, the PDF wins.
