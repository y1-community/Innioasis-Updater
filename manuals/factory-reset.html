Factory reset: what it does and how to do it safely

A factory reset returns the software to its first-run state. It also erases the player's internal storage, so read this first.

All player manuals Firmware archive

There are two different things people call a reset:

The reset pin

Forces the player to shut down when it freezes. It does not erase anything and does not restore factory settings.

Factory reset

Erases internal storage and returns settings, accounts and the home screen to their first-run state.

Before you reset

  1. Copy music, recordings, photos and documents to a computer.
  2. If the player has a microSD card, remove it or check that your files live on the card. Cards are usually left alone, but a backup is cheap.
  3. Note any Wi-Fi or Bluetooth device you will need to pair again.
  4. Charge the player to at least half.

Reset from Settings

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Choose Factory reset.
  3. Read the warning, confirm, and wait. The player restarts on its own.

If the player will not reach Settings, use the model guide: Y1, Y2, G1, G3, G5, R1, PR1, SR1, Q3 or Q5.
