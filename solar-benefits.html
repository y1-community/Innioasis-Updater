Why Solar for your Innioasis

Solar is the network-first software. It switches on the Wi-Fi chip that stock software mostly ignores and turns the player into a device that streams, searches, and downloads music — Deezer, Soulseek, podcasts, even YouTube — all without a phone in the middle.

Why people pick it

The player connects to the internet.

  • Stream and download from Deezer — Reach searches, streams, and downloads from Deezer using your own account — MP3 on free accounts, FLAC with Premium.
  • Soulseek search — Search peer libraries on Soulseek, the long-running file-sharing network, and grab tracks directly.
  • Podcast downloads — Subscribe, browse, and download episodes straight to the device over Wi-Fi.
  • A stem player — Split songs into vocals, drums, bass, and melody with Lalal.ai stem separation, then mash up one to three tracks with the scroll wheel.
  • Rockbox on the same ROM — Solar and Rockbox-Y1 ride together — switch launchers from Settings without reflashing or rebooting.
  • YouTube playback — The network-first route includes YouTube playback, turning the player into more of a pocket media machine.

Community

What the community says

Solar is the software for people who looked at their player, saw Wi-Fi hardware sitting unused, and asked “why can’t this just work like my phone?” It is the route most discussed in the r/innioasis community when the topic turns to streaming and downloads on the device itself.

The project’s own releases describe Solar as a work-in-progress interface, and the community treats it that way: exciting when it clicks, with setup steps (like connecting your Deezer account) that reward a patient reader.

  • r/innioasis · Solar discussions

What the project offers

  • Reach: unified Deezer + Soulseek search, streaming, and downloads
  • Get Music: combined Deezer and Reach search from the home menu
  • Podcast subscriptions and episode downloads over Wi-Fi
  • Stem Player: Gen1-style vocal / drums / bass / melody mixing with the scroll wheel
  • Global quick menu for playback, queue, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth from any screen
  • Y1 theme support — apply themes from the original Y1 software
  • Rockbox-Y1 co-installed with a unified keymap; switch launchers without reboot
  • About screen with version, attribution, and OTA update check

Things to know first

  • Solar is a work-in-progress interface — releases iterate quickly and rough edges are expected
  • Deezer needs your own account’s arl cookie, set up from a PC on the same Wi-Fi; treat the arl like a password
  • FLAC quality needs a Deezer Premium account; free accounts use MP3
  • Streaming and downloads need a Wi-Fi network — this is not an offline-first software
  • If something fails, restoring Original Software for your model is the way back
