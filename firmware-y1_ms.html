How to Install or Update Y1 Firmware

Upgrading or un-bricking your Innioasis Y1 is simple with our multi-platform software suite:

  1. Open Innioasis Updater CE: Launch the updater on macOS, Windows, or Linux.
  2. Select Model Y1: pilih Y1 in the Device Model dropdown.
  3. Pick Your Operating System: Select Rockbox, Better-Y, Solar, or Original Software.
  4. Click Install: Updater CE downloads the firmware package directly from the developer's release. When prompted, connect your powered-off Y1 via USB to begin flashing.
