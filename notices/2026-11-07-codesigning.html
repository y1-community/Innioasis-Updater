Windows 10/11 app launch issues reported

Users are reporting that they are unable to launch the app - find out more here.

From 2026-10-25 · until 2026-12-31 · Models: Y1,Y2

We're aware an issue where Microsoft Endpoint Protection and Smart App Control are blocking launches of our app. This has been reported to Microsoft asa false-positive and we are awaiting registration with SignPath so we can sign our builds and avoid these false-positives in the future.

A temporary workaround is to Open Settings > Privacy and Security > Windows Security > App and Browser control > Smart App Control > Off. This will allow you to open the app.

If Windows or Edge don't let you download Updater CE, simply click more options then Keep, if you have time, can you please Report the download as safe to Microsoft
