About Notices in Updater CE

You'll get important alerts about device safety issues reported by the community here, such as when devices are being bricked by certain firmware.

From 2026-10-25 · until 2026-12-31 · Models: Y1,Y2

Notices are how the community warns about device safety issues, firmware that should not be installed on a particular hardware revision, and new software releases.

  • Each notice has its own page on this site, so a warning can be read in full instead of being cut down to one line.
  • The app shows the same text, with the notice's detail opening from the banner.
  • A notice without a specific page simply opens its own page here; a link to the bare site root is ignored.

Use the bell icon to see the current notices at any time.
