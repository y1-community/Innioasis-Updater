Tại sao mọi người chọn nó

A focused player, not a mini phone.

  • Offline, on purpose — No streaming, no search, no cloud. Your music on your card, played back locally.
  • Every format, one engine — One pinned FFmpeg engine decodes MP3, AAC, ALAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, Ogg Vorbis, and Opus.
  • A queue that behaves — A persistent queue with Play Next, reordering, and removal — plus gapless and crossfade transitions.
  • Audiobooks that remember — Books are grouped by folder and resume at the minute you stopped, even mid-chapter.
  • Bluetooth audio — Pair A2DP headsets and speakers; playback keeps running with the screen off.
  • Built for the wheel — Every screen is navigable with the click wheel and hardware buttons, sized for the Y2’s landscape display.

Cộng đồng nói gì

Y2Player is the answer Y2 owners kept asking for: a music-first launcher that does not need an account, a stream, or a cloud anywhere. Its author shares release notes and a short Reddit release post with the r/innioasis community, and the response has been warm because it is built around how a dedicated player should behave.

The project is transparent about being a first Android app, and the community’s bug reports and diagnostics logs have directly shaped the 2.x releases — a genuinely community-shaped piece of software.

  • r/innioasis · Y2Player release threads

Dự án cung cấp những gì

  • Local library: Songs, Albums, Artists, Folders, Playlists, Favorites, Recently Played
  • Persistent playback queue with Play Next, reordering, and removal
  • Audiobooks grouped by folder, resuming inside the chapter you left
  • One FFmpeg engine for MP3, AAC/M4A, ALAC/M4A, FLAC, WAV/PCM, AIFF/PCM, Ogg Vorbis, and Opus
  • Repeat one/all, configurable seeking, playback resume, gapless, crossfade, and resume fades
  • Sleep timer: 15, 30, or 60 minutes, or stop at the end of a track, album, or queue
  • Dark and light themes, plus independent left/right balance
  • M3U/M3U8 playlist import and export
  • Bluetooth A2DP audio pairing and management
  • Full click-wheel and hardware-button navigation; playback continues with the display off

Những điều cần biết đầu tiên

  • Currently Y2 only — there is no Y1 package
  • The author is clear that this is their first Android app: bugs are possible, and a diagnostics log with any report is genuinely helpful
  • It is deliberately offline — no streaming, search, video, or cloud services
  • Check the release notes and your software version before flashing
