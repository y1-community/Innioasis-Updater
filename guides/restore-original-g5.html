Step 1: Prepare Your Computer

Innioasis Updater CE runs natively on Windows, macOS, and Linux without requiring complex installation harnesses:

  1. Launch Innioasis Updater CE on your desktop or laptop.
  2. Set Device Model to G5.
  3. Select Original Software in the software dropdown. Updater CE automatically downloads the official rom_g5.zip release package directly.
  4. Click Install / Restore to begin preparation.

Step 2: Connect the G5 at the Prompt

Never connect your player before the application prompts you:

  1. Ensure your Innioasis G5 is fully powered off.
  2. When Updater displays the USB connection prompt, plug your USB-C data cable into the computer and player.
  3. Do not touch the cable or power buttons while installation is underway.
  4. Once the success confirmation appears, unplug the USB cable and power on your G5.
