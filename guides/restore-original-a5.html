Restore Original Software on Innioasis & Timmkoo A5

Follow this tutorial to restore official factory software on your Innioasis A5 or Timmkoo A5 player with verified packages from the Innioasis Firmware Archive.

Get Updater CE View A5 Downloads

Step 1: Setup in Updater CE

  1. Launch Innioasis Updater CE on your Windows, macOS, or Linux computer.
  2. Set Device Model to A5.
  3. Select Original Software. Updater CE downloads the official rom_a5.zip release package directly.
  4. Click Install / Restore and wait for preparation to complete.

Step 2: Connect at the Prompt

  1. Power the A5 device off completely.
  2. Wait for the prompt on your computer screen before connecting the USB data cable.
  3. Allow the flashing process to finish without interruption.
  4. Disconnect and reboot your freshly restored A5 player.
