Rockbox-Y1 themes

Change how Rockbox looks on your Y1. The quickest way is to let Updater CE install the themes for you. You can also copy them over yourself.

Theme pack (360p)Rockbox tutorialsCommunity themes gallery

Recommended: let Updater CE install them

Installing Rockbox-Y1 with Updater CE 2.5 or later? It installs every 360p Rockbox-Y1 theme for you during the install. There's nothing to download or unzip.

Already on Rockbox? Open Updater CE and choose Settings → Install Rockbox 360p Themes, or press Cmd+T on a Mac and Ctrl+T on Windows and Linux. If it can't reach the player on its own, it walks you through USB Storage Mode.

360p themes, by hand

Recommended if you're copying themes yourself. They work on Rockbox-Y1 0.5 or later on every Y1, and you can copy them onto a Y2 by hand too.

The theme pack is every theme merged into one .rockbox folder. The individual download has each theme in its own folder. Either way, you merge a .rockbox folder into the player's: copy what's inside, merge folders, and replace files that already exist.

240p themes

240p themes are the Rockbox iPod classic themes on rockbox.org. Download one there and copy it into .rockbox by hand, the same way. They need a 240p Rockbox-Y1 build, and those ended at 0.4.

Check your Y1 first

240p builds can't be used on a Y1 bought new after May 2026, or on any Y1 that shipped with Original Software 3.0.7 or later. Flashing one there stops the player from starting.

iPod classic themes on rockbox.org

Pick a theme on the player

  1. Unplug the Y1 once the files are on it.
  2. Open Settings → Theme Settings → Browse Theme Files.
  3. Choose a theme. It applies straight away.

Frequently asked questions

Which Rockbox-Y1 version do 360p themes need?

Rockbox-Y1 0.5 or later. Every release from 0.5 on is 360p only.

Do I need to unzip anything if I use Updater CE?

No. Updater CE 2.5 or later installs every 360p theme during a Rockbox-Y1 install.

Can I use 240p themes on my Y1?

Only with a 240p build, and those ended at 0.4. 240p builds can't be used on a Y1 bought new after May 2026, or on any Y1 that shipped with Original Software 3.0.7 or later. Flashing one there stops the player from starting.

Do these themes work on a Y2?

You can copy the 360p files onto a Y2 running Rockbox-Y2 by hand. Rockbox-Y2 is a separate port, so check its release notes.
